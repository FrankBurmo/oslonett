Practical information

The ship carries survival suite, with floating elements, that are used as sailing equipment on cold days or colder areas. The need for equipment is thus reduced to, rain-gear and proper shoes, dockside sort of soles, with a good grip. Gloves and woollen-socks are also important, but are easily bought in Norway (at reasonable prices). No need for sleeping bag, unless the expedition plans land excursions including nights away from the boat (the ship carries tents and the common campgear). The ship is especially under sail giving minimal effects of seasickness. If you consider it to be a problem, Scopoderm is recommended, together with ordinary ginger (we can fill capsules onboard). We always carry a stock of both, just in case.The whole last season only gave one single sea-sick person for one afternoon, so it is not as big a problem as you might think.!
