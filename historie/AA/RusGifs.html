Solovetsky Monastery (45918 bytes)
This monastery with its history back to the 1500th century are an inevitable part whenever the history of the Northern Russia is told. After being a Gulag campus (where most of the intelligencia were and where Soltsjenitsjyn wrote «A Day In Ivan Denisovitsj Life») is now returned to the church (1992). Ressembling more than 200.000 pilegrims each year tell something about its position in Russian cultural and theological history.
