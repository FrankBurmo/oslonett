Image Archive from The 1994 Winter Olympics at Lillehammer

For your private use only(!), feel free to browse around and view these images from The 1994 Winter Olympics at Lillehammer.

All images are captured from the Norwegian channel NRK - "Norsk Rikskringkasting". If you redistribute these images, please make a note which will be tied to the files saying that these images are captured from NRK and copyrighted NRK. Thank you.

About these images - please read this

All these images are screen captures from an analog video source fed into my workstation. We split the video signal and distribute it to those lucky souls with videoin cards in their workstations, while the audio signals are distributed using IP multicasting. The loosers get both their video and audio signals through IP multicasting and use the software used in the Internet MBONE project (ie. nv, sd and vat).

The quality of the resulting images are not always good, they are some times diffuse due to motion in the frame during the capture. I am sorry for that, but it is nothing I can do with it. I use GIF as storage format since this will consume a minimum of disk space.

If you tell me in advance, I MAY BE able to take some shots from your favorite events and favorite athlets, but I cant promise anything. Also note that I don't have an Indy at home and that I need to sleep some times. I will typically NOT be able to capture so much from Figure Skating, Ice Hockey and other late-in-the-evening events.

The image index below is manually maintained, but you can also try to access a complete directory listing which is built regularly and automatically.
