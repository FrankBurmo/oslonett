Audio Clips from The 1994 Winter Olympics at Lillehammer

For your private use only(!), feel free to browse around and listen to these audio clips from The 1994 Winter Olympics at Lillehammer.

All audio clips are recorded from the Norwegian channel NRK - "Norsk Rikskringkasting". If you redistribute these clips, please make a note which will be tied to the files saying that these audio clips are recorded from NRK and copyrighted NRK. Thank you.

Note that we started to make recordings on Saturday 26, the day before the end of The 1994 Winter Olympics. Please don't complain about the selection and sound quality - we did this just to demonstrate the power of World Wide Web, and to give an impression of what can be expected in similar future services from Oslonett.
