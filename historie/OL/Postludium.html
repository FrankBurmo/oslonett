Olympic Announcement

We at Oslonett hope you have enjoyed our "WWW meets the 1994 Olympics" service and wish to thank you all for all the encouraging feedback we have received and the impressive usage statistics the service has produced.

We want to tell you a little about whats up for the future here from Norway, the land of snow, Olympics and WWW-sites.

The Oslonett News in Sports Database

Oslonett plans to follow up on the success of the "WWW meets the 1994 Olympics" by extending it to a permanent database of Winter Sports. The Olympic results will thereby be available for future reference.

Shortly after the Olympics ends, a report on the "WWW meets the 1994 Olympics"-project will be presented with statistics on its usage.

Oslonett is currently working to establish coverage of future sports events and to offer winter sports related data to the Internet.

Stay tuned to Oslonett for more exciting services.

To make the new services as interesting and functional as possible, we would be interested in more feedback from you. We encourage you to spend a minute of your time, and answer the below form. This will assist us in designing new exciting WWW based services of potential interest to the global Internet community.

Also, answering the below questions, bear in mind that future services can be expected to be of more commercial quality than the "WWW Meets The 1994 Winter Olympics" - this was just a quick hack which was created the evening before the games started!

Possible elements in new services, include

  • MPEG video clips
  • Audio clips (interviews, music, ..)
  • An even more extensive image archive
  • Better quality images (bigger, JPEG)
  • Better navigation and search possibilities
  • Access mechanisms other than HTTP (ftp, gopher)
  • Your choice!
