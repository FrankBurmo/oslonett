achieved Wednesday. Now Norway is the world's most winning country in
The Winter Olympics. When Johann Olav Koss
got his 1500m Gold Medal, this was Norway's medal #195 in The Winter Olympics
during all times. At present, the Sovietunion has 194 medals.

However, the Soviets has 78 gold medals, Norway has 67.
