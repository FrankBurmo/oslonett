February 14: Men's 30 km Cross Country is NOT canceled

Previous - Next

Received from NTB at 10:01. 



Lillehammer (NTB): The International Cross Country jury decided that 
Men's 30 km will be held as planned, despite low temperatures this morning.
Start will be 10.30 am as planned, the temperature is now just 
-20 degrees Celsius.

At 07.30am this morning the temperature was -28 Celsius.
The Results from The 1994 Winter Olympics are distributed by

Oslonett AS. and Norsk Telegrambyrå.
