Flash News from NTB, The 1994 Winter Olympics at Lillehammer

«Flash News from NTB» is a special service from Oslonett to the Internet where we manually translate a small selection of wire messages from NTB into English. The messages are selected by Oslonett staff - a typical selection criteria is messages of potentional interest to an international reader group.

Don't expect too many messages to show up here. Most of the NTB stuff is of local interest primarly. Even if there are a number of messages on the Harding and Kerrigan case, we think you are fed up with that already.
