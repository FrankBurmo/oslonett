Tender bank

Tender bank,- seek the bids for your future projects here.

To improve the business opportunities within IPMA in the field of Project Management, a tender bank facilities will be developed based on the same principals as in the working groups, except cost for distribution of the subscription has to be covered by the user of this services.

IPMA plan to offer the members «procurement opportunity facilities» through ICBM. This based on classifying in categories the various disciplines, branches, and geographical as criteria. There is demand to provide professional PM companies easy access to tender bank for their «special area» and IPMA wount to serve their members in this field.

In the aspect European Bank for Restructuration and Development is providing so-called «Procurement Opportunities» in subscription for pre defined criteria, and among those Project Management.

In this aspect similar bid / «procurement opportunity» as European Bank, will be possible to develop as a tender bank from other officials as i.e World Bank, EU as there is a requirement regarding openess. This tender bank will also be open for offer bids for members to the IPMA community.
