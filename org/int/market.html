Marketplace

In order to provide members information regarding new products and services in the field of Project Management, This Marketplace has been established. The Marketplace is based on three mainlines:
  • PM Products
  • PM Services
  • PM Education
The main presentation for the suppliers is one page on the Web server and if a consumer finds the product/ service of interest, he/she will click and then be transferred automatically to the homepage of the supplier by hyperlink.

For each of the three main objects, there will be developed branches for instance PM products are divited in PM Software, Tools, etc. Then the PM Software will be type of software, i.e: project management, Risk, Cost control, etc.
