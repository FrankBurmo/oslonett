Distribution of administration matters

Below are an overview of IPMA mailinglists regarding distribution of administrative information. If you want to join one of the lists, follow the description at the bottom of the page. Some of the mailinglists are closed. That means that the list-administrator can deny people access to the list.
board
Closed

cod
Consul of delegates.

exco
Closed

ipma
Information regarding general matters of IPMA. "Broadcasting channel".

na
Information in between national associations and IPMA.
If you want to join one of these lists, do as described below:
  1. You must send an email to the address: majordomo@oslonett.no, with empty subject-field.
  2. Put the following line in the body of the mail: "subscribe listname", where listname is one of the listnames board, cod, exco, ipma or na.
If you need help, you can contact John H. Heier at IPMA.
