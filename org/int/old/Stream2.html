Stream#2:Organizations for Dynamics and Proactivity

The Project Approach 

Session 2.1 Dynamic Leadership
What is it really?
Time: thursday June 9, 1400 - 1800 hrs.

Leadership is recognized as a critical strategic element on the management and operation of future societies, organizations and corporations. The demand for successful leadership will increase substantially as environmental conditions for the execution of activities change more and more rapidly. The dynamic leadership approach deals with establishing a predictable and reliable business performance and operation in world of change. 

Session 2.2 Proactive Behavior and Attitudes
How do we stimulate it?
Time: June 10, 0830 - 1200 hrs.

Dynamic leadership and management of change require a proactive behavior and attitude by the individual as well as by the organization. Such an approach must be rooted in the basic rules and ethics of the corporation, each organizational unit by the individual. This includes the personal and organizational commitment that leads to enhanced performance and reliability. A holistic vision of leadership and management is essential in this context. 

Session 2.3 Selection and Establishment of winning Teams
Time: Friday June 10, 1300 - 1630 hrs.

The selection and establishment of winning teams are critical factors for organizations and business units with sustainable competitive advantages. This part of the planning process will become more dominant and crucial for achieving success. Dynamic human resource planning with continuous monitoring, planning and forecasting is an essential part of this process, which should result in the recruitment and development of the "right" people. 

Session 2.4 Proactivity
why is it a winning outlook for the future
Time: Saturday June 11, 0900 - 1230 hrs.
A common characteristic in the future will be the necessity of continuous learning and flexible methods of organizing directly implemented into business activities. A critical success factor in such process is proactivity. Vital in this context is a winning culture with synergy and co-operation among the individuals involved.
