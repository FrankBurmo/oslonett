Stream#4: Project Control

A Guide to future requirements and approaches 

Session 4.1 The Project Control Function
Canned into the Computer?
Time: Thursday june 9, 1400 - 1800 hrs.

The latest generation computer systems are replacing several operations and activities that previously were manually executed by dedicated project control personell. Is it possible to change the project function into a full automatic control process including methodology, monitoring, control and decision making? Complete project control by the auto-pilot? Guidance will be given and forecast generated in respect to the expected future development in project control methodology, management approaches and the automation aspect. 

Session 4.2 The Multidisiplinary approach
Necessity for Project Control?
Time: Friday June 10, 0840 - 1200 hrs.

Future project organizations will be characterized by integrated social networks rather than rigid hierarchic type organizational structures. The rate of projects executed is increasing with strong interaction among the individuals, and less people will be involved in most projects. This leads to a need for the recruitment of primarily multidisciplinary type personell to the successful project organizations. The challenge are to combine enhanced in-depth expertise with an integrated multidisciplinary attitude and practice by the organization as well as by the individual. 

Session 4.3 Control Tools and Methodology
State of the Art
Time: Friday June 10, 1300 - 1630 hrs.

Information about the State of the Art in Control Tools and Methodology will be given. Particular focus is on the applicability of decision making approaches and systems for sustainable improvement in an organization's execution of projects. It deals with systems and methodology of qualitative type, and with varying degree of detailing and sophistication. Recommendations are given with respect to category methodology and tools to be used. 

Session 4.4 How to select the right Control tool
Time: Saturday June 11, 0900 - 1230 hrs.

A large number of organizations have not been sufficiently conscious about selecting adequate Project Control methodologies, and have invested in control systems that are either inappropriate of too large or complex for the actual need. It is of primary importance that full consistency is obtained in terms of matching the specified needs for the Project or organization in question. However, many organizational units do not know what they really need. This session gives an overview of criterias and premises to be established prior to the selection of Project Control Methodology and Control Tool.
