Countries within IPMA

Below are an overview over the IPMA mailinglists regarding national matters. If you want to join one of the lists, follow the description at the bottom of the page.
denmark
Open for all matters within Danish association of PM.
norway
Open for all matters within Norwegian association of PM.
If you want to join one of these lists, do as described below:
  1. You must send an email to the address: majordomo@oslonett.no, with empty subject-field.
  2. Put the following line in the body of the mail: "subscribe listname", where listname is one of the listnames denmark or norway.
If you need some help, you can contact John H. Heier at IPMA.
