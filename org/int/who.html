The members

A most significant INTERNET characteristic is the parallel development of associated national societies which serve the specific development needs of each country in its national language. INTENRET has thus emerged as an itnernational association of national project management societies.

Member societies have been established in

  • Austria
  • Iceland
  • Czechoslovakia
  • Italy
  • Denmark
  • The Netherlands
  • Egypt
  • Norway
  • Finland
  • Russia
  • France
  • Spain
  • Germany
  • Sweden
  • Greece
  • United Kingdom
  • Hungary

New Societies are being founded in the new states of Eastern Europe (Slovenia, Kroatia, the Baltics, Ukraine, etc.).

INTERNET also has a continuing cooperation with the Project Management Institute of North America (PMI), the Engineering Advancement Association of Japan (ENAA), the International Associations of Cost Engineers and Project Management Associations in Australia.
