<Normal>The target group-orientated hotel chain.

<Normal>The French Accor group runs more than 2000 hotels worldwide and is a pioneer in orientating their hotels towards the needs of different segments in the market. Sofitel and Pullman chains towards the upscale segment. Novotel, Mercure and Altea are first-class hotel chains, Ibis and Arcade are economy hotel chains, Etap and Formule 1 and Motel 6 are very economical hotel-motel chains. The Accor group nearly has a sub-chain for each segment in the market and is expanding rapidly on a worldwide basis. The speech will present the different chain concepts and the ideas behind them, with emphasis on the low scale-concepts.
