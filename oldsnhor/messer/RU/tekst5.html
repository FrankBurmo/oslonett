Trends in the European demand situation and its consequences for product development and marketing of Scandinavia.

Trends in the European demand situation according to the European Travel Monitor. Which destinations are winners and which are the losers? Which travel products are growing in popularity? Which target groups are travelling more and which are travelling less? Changes as to how travel products are organized and bought? What are the consequences of these trends from a Norwegian point of view and what should be done?
