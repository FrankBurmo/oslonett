Managing Director
Rolf Freitag

In 1969, Rolf Freitag founded IPK, Munich, a consulting company for market research and marketing planning in the field of tourism. Mr. Freitag has been responsible for numerous marketing development programs at the international level and has gained professional working experience in more than 20 countries and is specialised in strategic tourism planning.

Mr. Freitag is also the brain behind the European Travel Monitor which provides quantitative analysis of international travel - the only one in existence It`s activities in the Scandinavian countries are coordinated by NORTRA Consult.

Currently, Mr. Freitag is working hard on the Ecodata Eco - Tourism System, essentially a data bank providing information on ecological aspects of tourism destinations and products.

Ulf Prytz

Ulf Prytz er bl.a. hotellutdannet med bakgrunn fra SAS-hotellene. Dr. Aulina Poon

An economist by training, Aulina Poon holds a B.A. and M.Sc. (Economics) from the University of the West Indies, Trinidad, and a Ph.D. (Tourism) from the University of Sussex, England. Since graduation she has pursued a career in the field of international tourism consulting.

She is now head of the Caribeean Futures consulting practice in Trinidad and Germany, which specializes in strategic action planning and competitive strategies for tourist destinations.

Director
Trevor J. Ward

Trevor J. Ward is an honours graduate in hotel catering administration of the University of Surrey, where he was President of the University of Surrey Students Union. He is a Member of the Hotel, Catering and Institutional Management Association, and of the British Association of Hotel Accountants. Prior to joining Horwath Consulting he held line management positions with one of the UK's leading hotel chains.

He has undertaken consultancy work in many areas of the UK, Europe, Africa and the Far East, specialising in hotel project appraisal. He has worked extensively on assignments in Turkey, ranging from city centre and resort hotels, to major integrated developments incorporating real estate, hospitals, sports facilities and marinas. He has carried out a number of healthcare assignments, including feasibility studies for proposed private hospitals in the Middle East.

He was appointed a Director of Horwath Consulting in 1989, and has special responsibility for professional training. He speaks frequently at conferences and seminars and is co-author of "An Examination of Tourism Investment Incentives". He also contributed to the International Hotel Association's Hotel Investment Code, and represents the firm on the World Tourism Organisation's working party on information exchange.
