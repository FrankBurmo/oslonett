Population: Metropolitan area USA Rank: 6

Stadium Facts: Built in 1975, this domed indoor stadium will have natural grass put in place for the 1994 World Cup.Capacity 80,435. The Silverdome is in Pontiac, MI, 18 miles from downtown Detroit.

June/July weather: average temperature is 71 degrees F/21.7 degrees C. Temperature inside dome: avg. 70 degrees F/21 degrees C.

Attractions: The automotive industry made its home in metropolitan Detroit in the 1920's and Detroit became synonymous with the car and American manufacturing skill. In the 1960's, Detroit gave rise to the Motown recording label and stars such as the Jackson 5, Stevie Wonder, and the Supremes. Today, the 1,000 acre Belle Isle Park attracts tourists with its beautiful beaches, yacht basin, zoo, aquarium and botanical garden.
