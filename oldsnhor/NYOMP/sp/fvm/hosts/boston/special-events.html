Mayor's Neighborhood World Cup Celebration, May 1 - July 17

 Weekly event in each neighborhood of Boston featuring skill competition, celebrity all-star games, ticket raffle, barbecue, and giveaways. (617) 635-GOAL

Artifacts, photo panels, video and hands-on exhibits showcase the history of soccer and the World Cup at the Sports Museum of New England. (617) 621-0520

American Barbecue Round-Up, June 2-5

Enjoy live entertainment and family activities while munching on world-famous BBQ ribs at Fan Pier on Boston's waterfront. (617) 439-7700

Legacy Tour Soccer Celebration, June 3 & 4

Test your soccer-playing skills one and all during this 2-day celebration, which includes festivities at City Hall Plaza to be hosted by Melrose Place's Andrew Shue. (617) 357-1994

Saturday Night at the Oldies, June 4 - August 13

Groove to oldies as musical performers such as Gary Puckett & The Grass Roots, Girl Group Show, Brenda Lee, Gene Pitney, Petula Clark, the Everly Brothers, Lou Christie, Paul Revere & the Raiders, and Dion, rock Boston at City Hall Plaza. (617) 635-GOAL

Dairy Festival, June 6-10

Barnyard meets city during this festival when farm animals graze on Boston Common, cows participate in milking demonstrations, and butter is made from scratch. (617) 635-4505

Scooper Bowl Ice Cream Festival, June 7, 8, & 9

A 3-day ``all you can eat'' ice cream fest! (617) 635-4505

World Cup USA '94 Faneuil Hall Marketplace Kickoff, June 11

This FREE event, open to the public, will be a kickoff for the World Cup activities in Boston. A formal flag-raising ceremony will be combined with ball passing, international music and dance, jugglers, marching bands and professionals demonstrating their soccer skills. (617) 523-1300

Boston Globe Jazz Festival, June 12-18

Sound truly travels, especially this time of year when jazz performances pop up at various city locations all week long. (617) 523-4047

A World of Welcome to 1994 World Cup, June 16 - August 1

You won't believe your eyes and ears at South Station! Listen to music from each of the five countries competing for the World Cup at Foxboro--Thursdays at noon--June 16 - July 14, and see images of youth, sports and culture at a visual arts project on display--July 9 - August 1, 1994. (617) 451-2266

Harborlights Pavilion, June 18 - September 9

Boston Soccer Expo, June 18 - 20
