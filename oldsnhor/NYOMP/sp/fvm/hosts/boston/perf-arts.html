PERFORMING ARTS

Boston offers some of the finest performances in music, dance and theater in the nation. Whatever your preference, the city is bound to have a diversion that suits your taste.

The Boston Symphony Orchestra performs at Symphony Hall. In the summer and during the holiday season, the Boston Pops perform at the Hatch Shell on the Charles River Esplanade. Boston boasts the Boston Lyric Opera and the Handel and Haydn Society, the oldest choral organization in the country.

The Bank of Boston Celebrity Series brings international artists to halls and theaters around the city. The Boston Ballet performs old favorites such as ``The Nutcracker'' and new works by contemporary choreographers. There are also several modern dance companies.

Popular long runs include ``Shear Madness,'' a comedy whodunit, and the Guinness Book of World Records' holder of the ``Longest Running Non-Musical Play in American History.''

Annual festivals include the Boston Globe Jazz Festival and First Night, the city-wide New Year's Eve celebration of the arts. Summer music festivals abound at Great Woods in Mansfield, at Tanglewood in Lenox, at the Hatch Shell on the scenic Charles River Esplanade, as well as many outdoor concerts throughout the City.
