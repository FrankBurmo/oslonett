Population: Metropolitan area USA Rank: 3

Stadium Facts: Built in 1922 with a capacity of 66,946. Soldier Field is ideally located on the shore of Lake Michigan, south of downtown Chicago, Illinois.

June/July Weather: Average temperature is 70 degrees F/21.1 degrees C.

Attractions: Housing major air, rail and highway hubs, Chicago has historically remained a vibrant pulse of transportation. Tourists can visit the Museum of Science and Industry, the Field Museum of Natural History, the Chicago Art Institute, and the Lincoln Park Zoo. Three of the five tallest man-made structures in the world give Chicago a world-famous skyline. Chicago will host the Opening Ceremonies and Opening Match of the 1994 World Cup.
