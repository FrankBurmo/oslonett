  • AT&T Teleticket Service

    AT&T's new TeleTicket Service offers an easy way for international travelers to Los Angels to place telephone calls to any location in the U.S. and more than 190 countires and destinations around the world. This prepaid, 24 hour information and calling service features online language interpretation, capsulated world news, international currency rates and can be used to place calls from any push button telephone.
    TeleTicket offers service in English, French, Spanish, German, Dutch, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese and Korean.
    While in Los Angeles, visitors may obtain these services at the Bureau's Visitor Information Center in Downtown Los Angeles, 685 S. Figueroa St., or to order the service while in the U.S., call (800) 462-1818, ext. 912.
