Other Theater

There are hundreds of Off- and Off-Off-Broadway theaters performing plays of all types. Off-Broadway theaters range in size from 100 to 499 seats. Off-Off-Broadway theaters are smaller, with less than 100 seats. Tickets are inexpensive. For info:

Ticket Central, 1-8pm, (212) 279-4200

TKTS Times Square Theatre Center 47th St & Broadway. If you want to take a chance and don't mind waiting in line, get discounted tickets (25%-50% off) on day of performance for Broadway and Off-Broadway shows at the TKTS Times Square booth. For evening performance tickets, hours are Mon.-Sat. 3-8pm; for matinees, hours are Wed. & Sat. 10am-2pm. Sun. hours for all performances are 12-7pm.
Discount day of performance tickets and full-price advance sale tickets are also available at TKTS booth in Mezzanine of 2 World Trade Center, Mon.-Fri. 11am-5:30pm and Sat. 11am-3:30pm. Limited matinee & Sun. tickets available one day prior to performance.

TDF NYC/ON STAGE hotline: (212) 768-1818 for info on theater/dance/music in NYC

The Broadway Line (212) 563-2929 for information on Broadway, Off-Broadway, tickets, locations, reviews, synopses and schedules
