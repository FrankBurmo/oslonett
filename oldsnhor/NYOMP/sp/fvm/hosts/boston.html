June/July weather: average temperature is 70.5 degrees F/21 degrees C.

Attractions: Boston dates back to the American Revolution and is a fabled cradle of American liberty. Boston grew as a harbor town and is still one of the nation's finest ports. The city is now a center of banking and finance. Tourists can visit Boston Common, Faneuil Hall, the Swan Boats, and the USS Constitution. More than 20 colleges and universities are located in Boston, including Harvard University, the oldest college in the United States.
