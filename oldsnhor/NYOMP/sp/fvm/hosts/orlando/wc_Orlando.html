Venue: Citrus Bowl Stadium Directions to the Park-and-Ride locations:

Park and Ride Shuttle - LYNX, the regional transit system for the Orlando Urban Area, will provide shuttle service to the stadium from three easily accessible locations: Central Florida Fairgrounds on West Colonial Drive (SR 50) is the primary location; International Drive at Republic Drive serving the southwest and attractions area; and Ben White Raceway at the intersection of (US 441) and Lee Road (SR 435) for the northern suburbs and Winter Park hotels. Many area hotels will offer chartered bus service directly to the game or to one of the three Park-and-Ride lots.

Areas of Interest

Natural Florida Allow time to venture "off the beaten path" and enjoy the natural beauty of the area. Watch live gator wrestling, then take a breath-stopping bungee jump or hot air balloon ride. Then spend an afternoon horseback riding, fishing, or take a guided boat tour through a scenic chain of lakes. Try a canoe trip along the unspoiled Wekiva River or a pontoon boat ride or riverboat cruise along the historic St. John.

Orlando Science Center "Hands-on" science center, planetarium shows. 407/896-7151

A World of Orchids The world's largest permanent exhibit of flowering orchids. 407/396-1887

Sea World of Florida Marine life park featuring live shows. Home of Shamu the killer whale. 800/327-2424

Cypress Gardens Explore breathtaking gardens and the new butterfly conservatory. 800/237-4826.

Busch Garden, Tampa Bay African theme zoological park. Exciting thrill rides, live entertainment, exotic shows, and Kumba, one of the world's largest and fastest roller coaster. 813/-987-5082.

DisneyMGM Studios Theme Park Walt Disney World Resort The most popular movie and television studios in the world where Disney magic bring your favorite movies to life. 407/824-4321

EPCOT Center Walt Disney World Resort Disney's dream of two dazzling worlds, Future World and World Showcase. 407/824-4321

Magic Kingdom Walt Disney World Resort Over 40 attractions and seven lands featuring Adventureland, Frontierland, Tomorrowland, Main Street U.S.A., Mickey's Starland and Fantasyland. 407/824-4321

Kennedy Space Center/Spaceport USA Showplace for the United States space program/home of the space shuttle. 407/452-2121

U.S. Astronaut Hal of Fame Memorabilia of Mercury and Gemini astronauts and their historic missions. Home of the U.S. Space Camp. 407/269-6100

Malibu Grand Prix Scaled Indy and Formula One style racing. 407/351-4132

Universal Studios Florida Over 40 rides, shows and attractions including back to the future ride. 407/363-8000

Activities

Sea World of Florida

Marine life park featuring Shamu the killer whale and other marine life shows and exhibits. Open year round from 9am. Admission (plus tax) adult $29.95, children (3-9) $25.95.

Gator Jungle 26205 E. Colonial Drive, Christmas, FL, 407/568-2885

Exciting jungle cruises through gator country. Four daily wildlife shows, crocodile exhibit. Every day 9am-6pm.

Gatorland 14501 S. Orange Blossom Trail, 407-855-5496

Over 5,000 alligators and crocodiles. Alligator breeding marsh with observation tower, natural cypress swamp, other animals, birds and reptiles.

Kennedy Space Center Spaceport 407/452-2121

Launch and primary landing site of the U.S. Space Shuttle. Space center bus tour and IMAX movies. 9am-dusk.

Silver Springs P.O.Box 370, Silver Springs, FL, 904/236-2121

A 350 acre multi-themed nature part and national landmark. Major attractions are Lost River Voyage, Jeep Safari, Glass Bottom Boats Cruise, Doolittle3s Petting Zoo. Open 9am-5:30pm, adults $19.95 plus tax, children (3-10) $14.95 plus tax.

Busch Gardens 3000 Busch Blvd. Tampa, FL 33674, 407/351-3931

Experience thrilling rides, spectacular shows and the nation's fourth largest zoo. 9:30am-6pm, adults $31.90, children (3-9) $25.50.

Disney-MGM Theme Park and EPCOT Center P.O. Box 10,000, Lake Buena Vista, FL 407/824-4321

See movies and TV shows actually being made, and meet TV stars in person. EPCOT Center takes you on incredible "journey" through time and space at Future World and across exotic continents at World Showcase. Every day, hours vary by season.

Greyhound racing Seminole Greyhound Park, 407-699-4510

Ripley's Believe It or Not! 8201 International Drive, 407/363-4418

A unique museum with theme galleries that feature antiques, oddities, miniatures, and disasters. Every day, 10am-11pm.

Pepsi 400 NASCAR Winston Cup Series Race Takes place in early July at the Daytona International Speedway, 904-253-RACE
