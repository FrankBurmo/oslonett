Here in a city better known for its politics, its power lunches, its highticket lawyers, and its instantly recognizable landmarks and monuments, soccer once again is being celebrated as the world's game. Washington, D.C. is a must as a World Cup venue. Could you imagine a multi-sited international sporting event in this country that did not embrace this city?

This is truly an international city, whether you're talking about the diplomatic community, the ethnic community, or power and prestige. This is a city that loves soccer and has a very sophisticated soccer community.
