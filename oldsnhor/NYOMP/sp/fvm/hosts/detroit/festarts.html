DETROIT FESTIVAL OF THE ARTS

Background:
For one weekend every September, the streets of the University Cultural Center are filled with musicians, mimes, jugglers, artists, poets and street performers. The Detroit Festival of the Arts features 15 blocks of live entertainment in the city's artistic core.

What's Special:
The festival offers something for everyone. Visitors can wander through the marketplace where 125 artists will display or sell their works. With puppets and storytelling, the children's fair offers special hands-on activities for kids. The international arts square hosts a jazz cabaret, heritage exhibits and a coffee house. And festival food courts serve a variety of ethnic specialties in outdoor cafes.
In addition to the outdoor festival, the Cultural Center's institutions remain open through the weekend. Attractions include the Detroit Institute of Arts, Detroit Historical Museum, Center for Creative Studies, Children's Museum, Detroit Science Center and Wayne State University.

Details:
Admission to the Festival is free. The 1994 Detroit Festival of the Arts is scheduled for September 16, 17 and 18.
The Festival site is located in the University Cultural Center, off Woodward Avenue.
