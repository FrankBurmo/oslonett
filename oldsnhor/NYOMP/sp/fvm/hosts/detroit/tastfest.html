MICHIGAN TASTEFEST

Background:
Southeastern Michigan's premier outdoor culinary Festival takes place in Detroit's New Center area the July 4th weekend. Now entering its sixth year, TasteFest features food from more than 30 restaurants located around the Metropolitan Detroit area, cooking demonstrations by the area's renowned chefs, the Hiram Walker culinary showcase, top musical entertainment (including the spinners in 1994) and fun-filled family activities.

What's Special:
Proceeds from the Festival support the charitable activities of the New Center Foundation, the Festival's producer.

Details:
TasteFest is scheduled for July 1-4, 1994 in the New Center area, five miles North of Downtown Detroit off Woodward Avenue.
For more information contact, TasteFest at (313) 872-0188. Admission is free. Food can be purchased by tickets.
