RENAISSANCE CENTER

Background:
Soaring an eighth of a mile into the sky is Detroit's Renaissance Center, a gleaming hotel - office - retail complex that dominates the downtown skyline. Opened to the public in 1977, the Renaissance Center's Phase I included four 39-story office towers, the 73-story Westin Hotel and 80 retail stores and restaurants. Phase II included two 21-story office towers. The Renaissance Center still stands as the largest privately-financed development in United States history with more than $380 million contributed by private investors.

In 1992, the Metropolitan Detroit Convention & Visitors Bureau conducted a tourism research study that revealed the Renaissance Center as the most visited attraction in the Metropolitan Detroit area.

What's Special:
In June 1988, the Renaissance Center completed a $27 million renovation project that included a centralized retail shopping area on the first level, a redesigned entrance and lobby for The Westin Hotel, a new pedestrian circulation system and a more accessible and appealing Jefferson Avenue approach and lobby.

Extra Special:
Visitors to the Renaissance Center can marvel at the most spectacular view of Detroit from the observation deck atop the 73-story Westin Hotel. Cost is $3 for adults and $2 children. The Summit Restaurant, a revolving restaurant, is located at the top of the Westin offering stunning views of the city during lunch and dinner.

Details:
The Renaissance Center is located on Jefferson Avenue between Brush Street and Beaubien. The complex is managed by Renaissance Center Venture, 100 Renaissance Center, Suite 1400, Detroit, MI 48243, (313) 568-5600.
Retail stores are open from Mon.-Sat. 10 a.m.-6 p.m.
