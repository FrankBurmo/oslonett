CRANBROOK EDUCATIONAL COMMUNITY

Background:
One of the nation's greatest architectural complexes, Cranbrook is an internationally-renowned center for arts, education, science and culture. Located here is the Cranbrook Academy of Art Museum, three private schools, the Cranbrook Institute of Science, Cranbrook House & Gardens and Christ Church.
In 1932, newspaper publisher George Booth recruited Eliel Saarinen to design an Academy of Art in his sleek and modern Scandinavian tradition. The grounds were made complete with numerous works by famous Swedish sculptor Carl Milles. Today, the museum offers an everchanging collection of the finest contemporary art.
The Institute of Science is a natural history museum featuring a large mineral collection, fossil lab, planetarium, observatory and physics hall.

Visitors can also tour Cranbook House, home of founder George and Ellen Scripps Booth. Surrounding the English Tudor mansion are 40 acres of magnificent gardens. Lunch is available by reservation.

Extra Special:
From June 11 - September 5th, Cranbrook will host the Ontario Science Center's traveling exhibition, "Sport." Sport is the world's largest and most interactive exhibition devoted entirely to the science behind athletics.
