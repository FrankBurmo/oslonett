CONCOURS d'ELEGANCE

Background:

Since 1979, automobile collectors and enthusiasts from across the U.S. gather for the "competition of elegance" on the beautiful grounds of Meadow Brook Hall. The who's who of the automotive world are often caught wondering or judging.

What's Special:

The finest classic cars are exhibited in the Concours d'Elegance, where participants and guests experience the splendor of the automotive era in America.
Awards are given for Best Antique Car, Best Race Car, American Classic and more.
A few of the vintage cars that can be seen include: Bugatti, Rolls Royce, Mercedes-Benz, Cadillac and Jaguar.

Extra Special:

The Concours d'Elegance also features a vintage auto race called "Concours in the Park." Racers gather at the Waterford Hills Race course in nearby Clarkston. The historic races, along with the auto show, build a complete weekend of nostalgic festivities for the world's best car collectors, vintage racers and auto lovers.
The festivities also include a special International Art Exhibition showcasing the finest automotive art from across the country.

Details:

The Concours d'Elegance is held every August at Meadow Brook Hall, on the Oakland University campus in Rochester, (810) 370-3140.
In 1994, the event will run August 6 and 7 and spotlight Pierce-Arrow, Ford and Morgan sports cars and German-made classics.
