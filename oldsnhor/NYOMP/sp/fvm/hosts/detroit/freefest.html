INTERNATIONAL FREEDOM FESTIVAL

Background:
Celebrating its 36th anniversary in 1994, the International Freedom Festival is a whirlwind of activity for people in both Detroit and Windsor, offering more than 100 free events over a two week period. The event attracts more than three million people each year.
The first International Freedom Festival was held in 1959 to commemorate Queen
Elizabeth II's historic visit to Windsor.
Celebrating the friendship and freedom the two countries share along this common border, Ontario and Michigan also mark the birthdays of their nations (Canada Day on July 1 and Independence Day on July 4) during the festivities.
The 1994 Festival takes place June 22-July 4.

What's Special:
The signature event for the Freedom Festival is the annual fireworks display, the world's largest annual display. Taking place over the Detroit River near the Renaissance Center, the display features more than 3,500 pyrotechnic explosions in the 35 minute display.
Hudsons, Detroit's famous retail firm, has sponsored the fireworks since its inception.
The 1994 Hudson's Freedom Festival is scheduled for June 29.

Details:
The Freedom Festival is produced by the Parade Company and the Windsor Freedom Festival committee. The Parade Company is located at 1915 W. Fort St., Detroit, MI 48216, (313) 923-7400.
