RIVERTOWN

Background:
In rejuvenated, historic warehouses and carriage houses, the dining establishments of Rivertown have attracted many people to Detroit's hottest nighttime district. Some of the major restaurants and nightclubs in the area include: The Rhinoceros, fine dining with a piano bar in an old stone stable; Soup Kitchen Saloon, the one-time bordello has casual dining with local and national blues entertainment; Woodbridge Tavern, an old-fashioned speakeasy owned by the same family since 1905; and the Franklin Street Brewing Company, a restaurant/bar in a warehouse setting with an eclectic mix of industrial decor and house beer and ale.

What's Special:
The Chene Park Riverfront Music Theatre, a 5,000 seat outdoor amphitheater, opened in 1990, hosting a variety of musical concerts. Also, St. Aubin Park Marina, a 67-slip transient marina opened in 1990.

The Rattlesnake Club, famed restaurant of Chef Jimmy Schmidt, debuted at Stroh Riverplace in June 1988.
Harbortown, a complex of condominiums, apartments and retail stores opened on the riverfront in 1987. Since then, several other riverfront residential properties have been opened.

Details:
Rivertown is located along the shores of the Detroit River between the Renaissance Center and Belle Isle Park. It is maintained by the Rivertown Business Association,
