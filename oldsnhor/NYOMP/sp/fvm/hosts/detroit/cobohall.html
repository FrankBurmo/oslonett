COBO CONFERENCE/EXHIBITION CENTER

Background:
A Detroit meeting tradition for more than 30 years, Cobo Conference/ Exhibition Center is one of the largest convention facilities in the United States. In 1988, a $225 million expansion allowed Cobo to span more than 17 acres of downtown Detroit and offer 700,000 square feet of exhibit space.
The "new" Cobo has 84 meetings rooms, which can be used separately or linked to the exhibit floors via 26 escalators.

Named for the late Albert E. Cobo, mayor of the city from 1950-1957, Cobo Hall has served millions of convention and tradeshow delegates. Tens of millions more have attended public shows. The name was changed to Cobo Conference/Exhibition Center in 1987 to reflect the facilities multiple uses.

What's Special:
Every January, the international automotive community converges on Cobo to participate in the annual North American International Auto Show.

The fourth People Mover station in Cobo Conference/Exhibition Center enables conventioneers to gain access to the facility from any one of the stations on the 2.9 mile track of the transit system.

Extra Special:
Cobo is the site of the Michigan Sports Hall of Fame and one of Detroit's most recognizable figures, a statue of Joe Louis, resides at the facility's main entrance.

Details:
