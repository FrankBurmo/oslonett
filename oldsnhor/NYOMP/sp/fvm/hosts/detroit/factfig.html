    Facts and Figures - 4
    Major Industry:
    Internationally known for automobile manufacturing and trade, the world headquarters for General Motors Corp., Ford Motor Co., Chrysler Corp. and Volkswagen of America are located in Metropolitan Detroit. The area ranks as a leader in the production of paints, non-electrical machinery and automation equipment in pharmaceutical, rubber products, synthetic resins and garden seed. National and international corporations headquartered here include Kmart, The Budd Company, Stroh's Brewing Company, American National Resources and Federal Mogul. These companies help employ more than two million Detroiters.

    Employment by industry (top five):

    • Manufacturing, durable goods 418,657
    • Retail Trade 363,666
    • Health Services 196,955
    • Educational Services 165,643
    • Finance, insurance and real estate 130,118

    Airports:
    The Detroit area is home to two major airports: Detroit Wayne County Metropolitan Airport and Detroit City Airport.
    Detroit Wayne County Metropolitan Airport is the 12th largest airport in North America.

    Located 18 miles southwest of downtown, it is a major international business and leisure travel hub. The airport offers more than 100 non-stop destination national and international flights across the world.

    All major domestic airline carriers and three international carriers offer service from the airport.

    • Annual number of passengers: 22.8 million
    • Scheduled takeoffs and landings per day: 1,200

    Detroit City Airport, which is located 10 miles from downtown Detroit, offers limited commercial flights to Indianapolis, Cleveland, Akron and Chicago.
