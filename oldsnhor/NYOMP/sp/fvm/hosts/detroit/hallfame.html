MICHIGAN SPORTS HALL OF FAME

Background:
Founded in 1954, the Michigan Sports Hall of Fame is a non profit organization designed to maintain a public awareness of the excellence of performance and civic contributions of Michigan's finest athletes.
The Hall of Fame is housed in Cobo Conference/Exhibition Center along the south corridor. The attraction contains photos and plaques commemorating the achievements of outstanding Michigan professional sports figures. Each year, a "class" of members are inducted into the hall via a state-wide sports writers ballot election.

Extra Special:
The Michigan Sports Hall of Fame holds an annual induction banquet at Cobo Center and in 1994, six newly elected members joined the 169 Michigan Sports legends inducted since 1954.

Details:
The Michigan Sports Hall of Fame is located in Cobo Conference/Exhibit Center,
One Washigton Blvd., Detroit, MI 48226. The Hall is open 9 a.m.-5 p.m. or contingent on Cobo's extended convention hours. Admission is free.
