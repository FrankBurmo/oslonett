MEXICANTOWN FIESTA

Background:
Gather in Mexicantown for a fiesta that celebrates Detroit's authentic Hispanic tradition. For food, music and dancing with a Mexican flair, this is the one of the summer's hottest festivals.

What's Special:
Visitors can dance in the streets and try the salsa, mambo or more traditional folk dances. Ethnic cantinas and outdoor cafes serve Mexican, Puerto Rican and Cuban cuisine. Spectators will hear strolling musicians and see colorful costumes. The Mexicantown Fiesta offers rich culture and spicy fun.

Details:
The Mexicantown Fiesta is free and is located in the heart of Detroit's Mexicantown, on Bagley and 21st Streets, two blocks north of the Ambassador Bridge.
The 1994 Mexican Town Fiesta is scheduled for June 17-19.
