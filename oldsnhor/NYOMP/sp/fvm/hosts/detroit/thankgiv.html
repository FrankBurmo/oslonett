THANKSGIVING DAY PARADE

Background:
The Michigan Thanksgiving Day Parade is an annual tradition that takes place in the heart of downtown Detroit. Now entering its 68th year as a Detroit holiday institution,
the parade annually warms the hearts of more than one million Detroiters who attend the event. The event includes a grand marshall and local TV and radio broadcasts.

What's Special:
The parade begins at 9 a.m. each Thanksgiving. It features 40 floats, 20 helium balloons, 25 marching bands and more than 1,000 costumed marchers. As the grand finale, Santa Claus welcomes in the holiday season.

Extra Special:
Each year, hundreds of volunteers join the Friends of Parade Association to assist in parade activities -- float building, parade marshalls, clowns and giant caricatures.

Details:
The procession marches down a 2.5 mile stretch of Woodward Avenue from Mack Avenue to Jefferson Avenue.
