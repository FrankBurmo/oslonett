Sand and Sea

Just an hour from Washington is the Chesapeake Bay and its delicate ecosystem. The resort towns Rehobeth, DE, Ocean City, MD and Virginia Beach, VA, are three hours away. Assateague Island National Seashore is a 37-mile long barrier island extending to Chincoteague Island in Virginia. The island's wild ponies are believed to descendants of horses shipwrecked long ago.

Trails

C&O Canal National Historic Park
P.O. Box 4, Sharpsburg, MD 21782
(301) 739-4200
Stretches 184 miles from Georgetown to Cumberland, MD. In the summer, enjoy the mule-drawn barge trips from either Georgetown or Great Falls, MD. The wide towpath alongside the canal is open to bikers, joggers and hikers. Parking at Great Falls only.

Rock Creek Park
5000 Glover Road NW
(202) 426-6832
A rustic expanse of 1,754 acres surrounding Rock Creek and extending north to the District line. The park includes Pierce Mill, Art Barn gallery, nature center, riding stables, tennis courts, a golf course, 30 picnic areas, playing fields and an extensive network of trails for walking, jogging and cycling.

Theodore Roosevelt Island
Potomac River, accessible from the northbound lane of the George Washington Parkway, just beyond Arlington Memorial Bridge. (703) 285-2598. This serene island, accessible by a footbridge from the parking lot, is a wildlife refuge with nearly two miles of trails. A 17-foot bronze statue of President Theodore Roosevelt, early champion of wildlife and nature conservation, can be seen in Statuary Gardens. Open daily 8 a.m.-dusk. Tours conducted by request.
