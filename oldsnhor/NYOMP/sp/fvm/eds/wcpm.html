Project Management

In addition to application development and systems integration, EDS is providing project management for World Cup USA 1994 in several key areas.

  • EDS is the largest provider of personnel to the World Cup technology staff.

  • EDS personnel have lead the deployment of local telephone services at each venue.

  • EDS personnel are leading the technology efforts at major venue sites.

  • EDS personnel have directed the installation of critical network resources.

  • EDS personnel are managing the daily operations of the network.
