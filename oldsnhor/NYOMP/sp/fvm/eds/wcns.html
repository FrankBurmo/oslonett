World Cup News Service

The World Cup News Service (WCNS) is a source for statistical and biographical information for all World Cup tournaments since the first World Cup in 1930. The principal user-base for WCNS is the press. Print and broadcast journalists at each venue will have immediate access to historical statistics and official results of World Cup 1994. In addition, past news articles and soccer-related announcements from around the world will be available online.

The 1994 Results, Statistics and Biographies section of WCNS will track the progress of each team through every game of the 1994 World Cup. Spotters at each game will enter all pertinent statistics as they occur, including goals, assists, penalties and substitutions. This information, being stored in the central database, will be immediately accessible to all other workstations. In addition, certain key statistics, such as goals scored, will trigger the sending of messages to broadcast booths at the stadiums, insuring that broadcast journalists have up-to-the-minute results information from other venues.

WCNS will include a fully-automated query facility for accessing detailed statistics from all previous World Cup tournaments. All official FIFA records from every World Cup tournament can be accessed based on tournament, team, player or statistic e.g. the best scorer, the most tournaments won by a team, the highest scoring game in a tournament,etc.

In addition to WCNS, the official FIFA record book will also be available around the world via the Compuserve network. This subscription-based service will permit access to the World Cup database from virtually any personal computer equipped with a modem and communications software and access to a telephone network. This online service will present in a textual format the same statistical information available graphically through WCNS.

The News and Announcements section of WCNS provides background information for all aspects of world soccer, based on published articles in newspapers or other news services. This information is available in as many as four languages: English, French, German and Spanish.

The Media Logistics system provides support for the press who will be in attendance at World Cup USA 1994 games. Media representatives are identified to the system by having information about them entered into the database. Seats or sections at each stadium may be reserved for use by the press through the system. In addition, other press requirements including telecommunications equipment, accomodations, and travel may be specified in the system.
