Event Management Applications

The Event Management Applications (EMA) developed by EDS for World Cup 1994 include all systems needed to effectively execute the World Cup games.

  • Accreditation: Officials, dignitaries, media and security personnel will be provided with badges to assist World Cup security with access control.

  • World Cup News Service: provides jounalists with biographies, results and statistics from every World Cup tournament including 1994 results as they become official.

  • Inventory, Staffing and Protocol: Complete information management for all aspects of World Cup operations.
