Consulting

The development, refinement and coordination of strategic business plans and initiatives in conjunction with a client's senior management team to support the client's business direction, impact business performance, and improve operating results.

  • Business Planning--development and articulation of business mission and vision, goals, objectives, critical success factors, strategies, programs, and measures necessary to achieve and maintain improved business performance.

  • Business Design and Reengineering--identification, development, and optimization of the processes and activities that support the business direction, strategies, structure, and infrastructure.

  • Resource Planning--definition, development, leverage, mobilization, and optimization of the internal and external resources required to improve business performance.

  • Change Management--application of management processes to transition from an existing environment to one that meets specific business objectives.

Systems Development

The creation, migration or improvement of systems to meet specific business needs or take advantage of changes in the technology market.

  • Functional Specifications--identification of system requirements and a design of the capabilities and functionality necessary to meet business needs.

  • Applications--creation or modification of systems and related support to perform according to specifications.

Systems Integration

The process of acquiring, integrating and installing all hardware, software, communications and related support components.

  • Technology Selection--identification, acquisition, customization and installation of hardware, software and communications components that will function together effectively to support the business applications.

  • Interface Creation--building or modification of interfaces and other capabilities to allow different application components to function together effectively.

  • Implementation--transition to the operation of a new business environment, including training and benchmarking.

Systems Management

The ongoing management and operation of information technology components. Systems management may involve resources ranging from specialized system applications to the customer's entire information technology function, including facilities and personnel.

  • Computing--operation, maintenance and enhancement of centralized or decentralized computing infrastructures, including intelligent workstations and corresponding systems software.

  • Communications--operation, maintenance and enhancement of the telecommunication infrastructure, which may include wide area networks (WANS) and local area networks (LANS), transmission media (satellite, microwave and terrestrial) and communication media (voice, data, and image).

  • Applications--operation, maintenance and enhancement of a portfolio of systems used to support business functions and processes.

  • Data--the organization, maintenance and protection of data, including the logical and physical distribution environment, access methods and security required by the business.

Process Management

The ongoing responsibility for direction and operation of one or more business processes within an enterprise.

  • Resources--acquisition, deployment and utilization of people, facilities, technology, support functions and supplies.

  • Integration----linking business processes, including development of interfaces with related processes and applications.

  • Performance----responsibility for the fulfillment of business processes, including accountability for performance measures.
