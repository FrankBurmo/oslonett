Directions to the Los Angeles Venue: The Rose Bowl

From the North:
From the north San Fernando Valley, take eastbound Foothill Freeway, (Interstate 210). Exit at the Berkshire-Oak Grove or Arroyo-Windsor off-ramp. Proceed south to parking areas.

From the South:
From downtown Los Angeles, take the Golden State Freeway, (Interstate 5) north to the Glendale Freeway, (Route 2), then north to the Foothill Freeway, (Interstate 210) east. Exit at the Berkshire-Oak Grove or Arroyo-Windsor off ramp and proceed south to parking areas.

From the West:
From the San Fernando Valley, take the eastbound Ventura Freeway, (Route 134) to the Glendale Freeway (Route 2), then north to the Foothill Freeway, (Interstate 210) east. Exit at Berkshire-Oak Grove or Arroyo-Windsor off ramp and proceed south to the parking areas.

From the East:
From the San Gabriel Valley, take the westbound Foothill Freeway, (Interstate 210). Exit at Lincoln-Washington, Arroyo-Windsor or Oak Grove-Berkshire off ramp and proceed south to parking areas.

Special Information:
Handicapped parking is available in Lot B, accessible from the Seco-Mountain exit off the Foothill Freeway, (Interstate 210).
