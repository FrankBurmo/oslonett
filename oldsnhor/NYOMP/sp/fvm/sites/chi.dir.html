Directions to the Chicago Venue: Soldier Field

DIRECTIONS TO THE STADIUM:
Stadium parking is limited and spectators are urged to take bus transportation or walk from downtown on game days. Look for details on stadium access local newspaper, television and radio.

Chicago Transit Authority (CTA), (312) 836-7000, provides a rapid transit train from O'Hare to the Loop every 8-12 minutes (every 30 minutes from 1-5 a.m.); one-way fare for the 35-minute trip is $1.50; and operates rapid transit from Midway Airport to the Loop (Orange Line). The 20 minute trip is $1.50. CTA also provides weekday rush hour bus service from Midway to downtown.

From the North:
Take southbound Lake Shore Drive (US41). Exit at westbound Randolph Street, then south on Columbus Drive to Monroe Drive and the Grant Park Monroe Drive Parking Garage, or continue on Lake Shore Drive to the stadium.

From the Northwest:
Take eastbound (Interstate 90/94) southbound Kennedy Expressway to Ohio Street exit, Ohio Street east to Fairbanks/Columbus, and south on Columbus to the Grant Park Monroe Drive Parking Garage. Traveler's Advisory: Construction will be in progress on the Kennedy and may increase travel times.

From the West:
Take eastbound Eisenhower Expressway (Interstate 290) to eastbound (Interstate 90/94) Dan Ryan Expressway southbound to northbound (Interstate 55) (Stevenson Expressway) to southbound Lake Shore Drive (US41). Exit Lake Shore Drive at 31st Street to McCormick Place Parking.

From the Southwest:
Take Stevenson Expressway northbound (Interstate 55) to southbound Lake Shore Drive (US41). Exit Lake Shore Drive to 31st St. exit and McCormick Place Parking.

From the South:
Take northbound Lake Shore Drive (US41) to 31st Street exit and McCormick Place Parking, or continue to the stadium. OR: Take northbound Dan Ryan Expressway (Interstate 90/94) to northbound Stevenson Expressway (Interstate 55) to southbound Lake Shore Drive (US41). Exit Lake Shore Drive at 31st Street to McCormick Place Parking.

From the Central Area:
Use east-west streets to Columbus Drive or Lake Shore Drive (US41) and south to Soldier Field.

Pedestrian Access Routes:
Michigan Avenue (both sides) from Oak Street to Van Buren Street, Harrison Street, or 11th Place. There are pedestrian overpasses over the Illinois Central Railroad right-of-way at all three locations, connecting with Columbus Drive. OR: Columbus Drive (both sides) from Monroe Street to Roosevelt Drive. There are signalized pedestrian crossings at Roosevelt Drive across both Lake Shore Drive and Columbus Drive. The Chicago Department of Transportation is evaluating these locations for the installation of pedestrian bridges. OR: Lake Shore Drive (both sides) from Grand Avenue to Soldier Field. A pedestrian tunnel at approximately 1200 South provides access to Solder Field.

Special World Cup Parking signs will be in place to help motorists locate all parking facilities.
