  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Eight
    • Record: 7-14-4
    • Best Result: 1986: Fourth Place.

    Belgium was among the pioneers of organized soccer in Europe. The Belgium Soccer Association was formed in 1895. In spite of the fact that there was great interest in the early leagues, soccer was mainly thought of as a pastime.

    Belgium played its first international match against France in 1904. But, it was not until 1977 that full professionalism was introduced in Belgium and the goal of competing with the best was finally reached. It qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup with a 0-0 draw against the Former Republic of Czechoslovakia on November 17, 1993, in Brussels.

    Belgium has attended four World Cup Finals since 1970 including the 1986 tournament where it finished fourth.
