  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Five
    • Record: 0-10-6
    • Best Result: 1986: Lost to Mexico in second round.

    Popular legend has it that soccer was introduced in Bulgaria in 1894 by Georges de Regibus, a Swiss physical education teacher. The Bulgarian National Sports Federation was founded in 1923, which included the Football Federation. It was not until the Soviet takeover in 1944 that large funds were made available for facilities and coaching. This increased Bulgaria's interest in soccer.

    The national team proved successful in Olympic competition at Melbourne in 1956, as well as Mexico in 1970. Bulgaria qualified for each of two World Cup finals in the 1960s and the first two finals of the 1970s. Its effort in the World Cup finals in 1986 advanced it to the second round for the first time in history. Qualification for the 1994 FIFA World Cup came when Bulgaria beat France, 2-1, on November 17, 1993, in Paris.
