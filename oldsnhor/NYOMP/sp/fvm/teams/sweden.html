    The oldest Swedish club was formed in Gothenberg in 1887, a city that still remains the center of Swedish soccer today. The swedish Sports and Athletic Association also was formed in Gothenburg in 1895.

    The first national game was played in July 1908, seventeen years before a national league was introduced. Sweden took fourth place in the 1938 World Cup, and has done well since that time, attending seven other World Cup finals. Sweden was assured its place for the 1994 FIFA World Cup before its 1-1 draw with Austria on November 10, 1993, in Austria.
