  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Eight
    • Record: 13-12-7
    • Best Result: 1950: Finished Fourth.

    Soccer came to Spain in 1873, when English and Scottish workers at the Rio Tinto and Tharsis mines introduced a new game called football. The first club was formed in 1880, the "Huelva Recreation Club". Most of the clubs still in existence today were formed by 1910. Spain joined FIFA in 1904, and the Real Federacion Espanola de Futbol was formed in 1913.

    The national team did not play its first game until 1920. Spain held an excellant international record until the outbreak of its civil war in 1936. During the Spanish Civil War and World War II, Spain's international soccer game nearly came to an end. After World War II, soccer began to grow again. Spain has qualified for eight World Cup finals and qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup when it beat Denmark, 1-0, on November 17, 1993, in Seville.
