  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Two
    • Record: 0-3-0
    • Best Result: 1930: 4-0 losses to Brazil and Yugoslavia.

    Bolivia was introduced to soccer by Leoncio Zuaznabar in 1896. The national league was formed in La Paz in 1914, with the formation of the Federacion Boliviana de Futbol in 1925. Bolivia's youth footbal academy, Tahuichi, has made a name for itself worldwide. The academy takes boys off the streets by placing them in a stable environment. It stresses a good education and an enlightened plan of personal development. The Tahuichi Academy is highly regarded for its artful and beautiful style of play. It was formed 15 years ago by Roland Aguilera.

    Bolivia's national debut came in 1926 when it played in the South American Championship. It was outscored 24 to two in four matches. When the following year produced similar results, participation was shelved until 1946. Since then, Bolivia has capitalized on its advantage of practicing and playing soccer at 12,000 feet above sea level. Bolivia qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup with a 1-1 draw against Ecuador on September 19, 1993. This is the first trip to the finals through the qualification process.

    Bolovia has attended two World Cup finals by invitation, once in 1930 and again in 1950.
