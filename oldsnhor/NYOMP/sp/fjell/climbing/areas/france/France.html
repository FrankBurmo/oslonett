Rock Climbing Areas In France

Buoux

Location:

7km S of Apt; 57km SE of Avignon; 36km E of Cavaillon in the vaucluse. The crags are situated in the village. It is only allowed to climb on the crags facing south near the river.
Kind of Rock:
Limestone.
Height:
100m.
Season:
Climbing is possible all year around although in mid-summer it can get to hot and in mid-winter it can get too cold.
Bolted:
Buoux is very well bolted. Other gear you can leave home since it is hardly possible to put is somewhere.
Camping sites:
It is not allowed to camp in Buoux. There are a lot of camping sites nearby. There is a camping site in Apt, and also a very nice "camping municipal" (= cheap) at Bonnieux (close to the crag).
Topo:
A topo can be bought in Cavaillon at "Inter sport" or in Apt.
Grades:
The easiest routes are in the grades 4b, 4+ and 5a; Most of the routes are in the 6b/c and above range (although there are a number of 6a's).
Other comments:
This is one of the best crags in France.

Ceuse

We spent over 2 weeks last June climbing at Ceuse. It's superb; really one of the best limestone areas in France. It's hardly polished compared with Buoux or Verdon. The best camping is at Camping Les Guerins along the road about a mile east of the crag trailhead. It's run by a friendly farmer. He would come by everyday and chat with us. We couldn't, however, understand much of what he said and just nodded in agreement. It costs about $15 a night depending on whether you have a car, how many people, and how many tents. He also sells the guide "Grimper dans les Hautes Alpes" for about $20. You almost need a car to get up to basecamp and then down to Gap, the nearest large town, for essential supplies like beer and camembert. The nearby town of Sigoyer, about 3 miles downhill, has a good bakery for morning bread and croissants du chocolat and accommodations. The trail up to the cliff gets fairly tiresome after a week of walking up there. Take the right hand trail that goes up the ridge east or right of the nursing home. It winds up through the trees at a fairly gentle angle, but still gains about 2,000 feet of elevation in 2-3 miles. The left or west trail goes straight up the hillside and is very steep and eroded. It makes a good shortcut for coming down in the afternoon. Best place to access the cliff is at the Secteur Cascade on the western end of the crag. A huge spring of delicious, frigid water cascades out of the cliff to a couple pools. This is a good place to fill your water bottles and cool down after the tiring trek up. The best climbs for the hardman or woman is at the Secteur Cascade--long overhanging pulls between pockets and huecos. Bring a 200-foot rope for all climbs. The right side of the Secteur Cascade has some good warmups-- Ananda 7a, Medecine douce 6c+, Women 7a+. Des trous encore des trous direct is a good 6b route. Immediately right are more good and popular routes. Further down the cliff (to the east) is the Secteur Berlin, with lots of hard technical Patrick Edlinger routes. Makach Waloo 7c+ is excellent. At Secteur demi lune are some great 6a and 6b routes--Harley Davidson 6b+, Papyrus 6a+, Katina 6a, and Carte noire 6a. Just right is Carte blanche 8a+ or 5.13c, very overhanging technical route that Ian did his fourth try. At the far end of the cliff where the climbing ends on the northeast flank (the rest of the cliff is off-limits to preserve its unique flora which is where plants from the Alps meet plants from the Mediterrean) is the very overhanging Bibendum 7b. It's well worth doing for the spectacular lower-off. Lots of other great nearby areas also--try Orpierre for lots of moderate and easy climbs, plus the campground has a pool (You need Speedo trunks to swim here). Also Devoluy to the north with bolted 2,000-foot limestone sport routes.

Cheers--Stewart

near Brignolles in the south of france
Kind of Rock:
limestone
Height:
50 m
Season:
all season
Bolted:
y

you can find it in the magazine GRIMPER mars 95

5b-c to 8b-c

comments : I discovered this site in April 95 and I returned there in the end of september 95. I fell in love with this crag.It's a great pleasure to climb there: the routes are athletic and very esthetic.just an advice: on week end many climbers meet on this crag. so go on climbing on week day.

Le Kromtahl

Location:
Marlenheim (Alsace)
Kind of Rock:
Grit
Height:
20 m
Season:
All but Winter
Bolted:
Yes
Topo:
There is a small one but out of date.
Grades:
From F5c to F8b
Other comments:
In this crag, one find the hardest routes of Alsace with : - Le Tango des Grabataires F8a - De la Terre a la Tune F8a - Armelle au pays de big bacs F8a - Le Theatre du No F8a+/8b - Le Monument Hysterique F8b (unrepeated, first ascent : JM Thrin-Thieu) But there numerous routes between F6b+ and F8a. All grades are well represented.. Totally, there are quite 80 routes.

Orgon

Location:
10km W of Cavaillon; 50km S of Avignon.
Kind of Rock:
Limestone.
Height:
8m to 30m high.
Season:
Climbing is possible all year around although in mid-summer it can get to hot and in mid-winter it can get too cold.
Bolted:
The orgon is very well bolted
Camping sites:
There is a camping site between the crags. At the moment this camping is not accessible for climbers, I don't know wheather this will change in the future. There are several camping sites about 10km from Orgon.
Topo:
A topo can be bought locally in Orgon.
Grades:
There are routes in all grades.
Other comments:
The latest news (dd 24 of may 1995) half of the cracks - the whole of the right hand side as you look up the valley - are closed. The left hand side and the Falaise du Canal crags are still open though. It is a very nice area with quite short routes. There is also a section called "Section du canal" with lots of hard routes; Information of this section can also be found in a Dutch magazine called "Limits" September 1994. This area is suited for beginners and, therefore, quite crowded.

Orpierre

Location:
50km E of Avignon.
Kind of Rock:
Limestone.
Height:
10m to 100m high.
Season:
Climbing is possible all year around although in mid-summer it can get to hot and in mid-winter it can get too cold.
Bolted:
It is very well bolted. Especially for beginners there are lots of routes with distance between bolts of 1m to 2m.
Camping sites:
There is a camping sites in the village.
Topo:
A topo can be bought locally in the climbing shop or in the local bar.
Grades:
There are routes in all grades.
Other comments:
This is a very nice area with a lot of routes in the grades 5 and 6. In summer when it is hot you can climb in the morning and evening and use the swimming pool of the camping site of the village during the hottest time of the day. This area is suited for beginners and ,therefore, quite crowded.

Saffres

Location:
50km NW of Dijon. Go from the village up along the hill; when you see a parking place, there is this field where you can park. From the parking place there is a road to the crags.
Kind of Rock:
Limestone.
Height:
8m to 30m high.
Season:
Climbing is possible from April to October; Before and after this time it can be too cold and too rainy.
Bolted:
Saffres is very well bolted.
Camping sites:
There is a place, where it is allowed to camp underneath the crags along the road. This place has cold water and a toilet. They sometimes come to collect money for the camping once and a while.
Topo:
A topo can be bought locally in Saffres.
Grades:
There are about 150 routes in all grades.
Other comments:
The next village where you can do some shopping is 10km from Saffres.

Verdon

Location:
In the canyon of Verdon. The climbing center is at La Palud.
Kind of Rock:
Limestone.
Height:
Up to 300m high.
Season:
Climbing is possible all year around although in mid-summer it can get to hot and in mid-winter it can get too cold.
Bolted:
The Verdon is not always very well bolted, so you have to bring your gear. Especially this is true for the routes at "L'Escales"; Bolts are only placed at spots where it is hard to place other gear. Some other areas are well bolted.
Camping sites:
There are a lot of camping sites nearby.
Topo:
A topo can be bought in La Palud. It is advisable to buy one.
Grades:
There are routes in all grades.
Other comments:
This is one of the best crags in France. One of the best crag there is called "L'Escales". This crag is 300m high, and most of the routes are reached by rappel. Be careful to take the right route, since else it can be very hard to get back. Don't leave any valuable things in the car, since car thiefs are waiting for you.
