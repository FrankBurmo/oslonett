Finale Ligure

Location:

Finale Ligure, 40Km to the West from Genoa, Northern Italy
Kind of Rock:
Limestone with holes
Height:
~100m
Season:
All year
Bolted:
Y
Camping sites:
Tolerated under Monte Cucco
Topo:
"Finale" by A.Gallo available at "Rockstore" in Finalborgo
Grades:
1500 routes from 4 to 8b+
Other comments:
All the routes in the Topo with three stars! comments : Finale is an area with about 30 crags ranging from 10m to 250m of height and with routes for everyone, for the most part correctly bolted (cemented rings and spits). The climbing style is technical and physical at the same time but always enjoyable and the variety of the site is so great that Finale is ideal for a one week holiday or more. There are some very important routes like Hyaena, 8b+, and Ombra ,8b+, which are part of the history of extreme climbing ('80s) but in general Finale is the ideal site for the medium climber (6a-7a). A last word: if you can, don't miss it!
