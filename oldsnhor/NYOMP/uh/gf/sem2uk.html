A Summernight's Dream

International Theatre Festival, Porsgrunn, June 4-11, 1995

Cristina Castrillo : Memory

Cristina Castrillo is one of the founders of the famous Libre Teatro Libre in Argentina. Later, she settled in Switzerland where she founded Teatro delle Radici in 1980. Currently, she travels all over Europe as teacher, actor and director. As indicated by the seminar's title, Castrillo's focus is the use of the actor's own memories as a tool. When the actor's work is rooted in «personal rememberances», he or she ventures into unmapped personal territory. The seminar consists of physical and vocal work.
