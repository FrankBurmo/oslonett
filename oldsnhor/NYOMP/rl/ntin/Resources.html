Information Resources Within The Norwegian Travel Information Network (NTIN)

NTIN provides a lot of views into its huge information base. Readers may select views depending on what they are looking for. One view is the regional view, where the best place to start is the clickable map of Norway. You select the area you want to visit on the map. Inside each area and town there are pointers to the individual information resources for that area.

The above view is well suited if you are looking for information about a specific area of Norway. If, instead, you are looking for general information on Norway, you may select the national view. Here you will find many of the same information categories, but the granularity is not so fine as within the regional view.

Within some regions and towns you may be able to "zoom in" to even deeper levels of detail!
