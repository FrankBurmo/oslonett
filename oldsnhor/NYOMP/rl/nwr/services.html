White water rafting (Sjoa river)

Day trip: experience a full day of unforgettable rafting. For first time rafters we offer a class 2-3 day trip on the lower Sjoa. Our trips have made the crystal clear Sjoa river famous all over the world. A tasty warm lunch is served during the rafting adventure.

2 day trip: Our overnight trip includes two great days on the river. You may choose to spend the night in our wilderness camp, or if you prefer, you can stay in a modern cabin with complete facilities available. The trip includes a three course barbeque dinner, a hot lunch both days and breakfast on the second day. We arrange our overnight trips for novice rafters (class 2-3) and experienced rafters (class 3-4).

NWR's rafting school: An intensive six day course designed for those interested in becoming a riverguide or just want to learn more about rafting.

NWR's rafting safari: Call for details.

Rock Climbing

NWR will teach you how to climb in our 12 meter high climbing tower. Then we take you out to try real rock climbing. You will also learn how to abseil down the mountain side. We use different sites depending on the participants skills and wishes. This activity suits everyone who is looking for a new experience and adventure.

Season: May - October

Fishing

Would you like to catch a 4 kilo trout while visiting Norway? Jotunheimen offers several excellent lakes and rivers to catch big chars and mountain trout. NWR guides will show you how to prepare the fish and have a feast while sitting by the lake. During the winter NWR offers ice fishing - don't miss it!

Season: Summer (June - August), Winter (December - March)

Trekking

From NWR's Rafting Centre we can reach 3 famous national parks, Jotunheimen, Dovre and Rondane. Here you will find Galdhøpiggen and Glittertind, Norways highest mountains. Trekking in these areas can definately be the highlight of your vacation in Norway. Don't forget your camera and binoculars.

Season: May - October

Here is how to order!

Grotting

Caves are made by underground rivers. When exploring dark caves it feels like time is non-existent. The sharp light from your headlamp and funny sounds from the hidden river makes your imagination run wild. We will explore the Dummedals caves in Bøverdalen and Kåsihole in Rondane.

Season: Juni - October

Here is how to order!

Canyoning

See the deep norwegian canyons, the many waterfalls and steep walls. Summertime NWR visits Bergdøldalen, a wild and remote canyon which for some parts is only a few meters wide but very, very deep.
Your climbing skills will be put to a test here.
Wintertime we visit Åsengjuvet on skis or snow shoes. All waterfalls are frozen up and covers the canyonwalls like white/blue curtains. If we are lucky we can get a good look at some of the elk staying here during the wintertime. This is an adventure not to miss.

Season: The whole year

Here is how to order!

Glaciering

NWR invites you to challenge the Glacier of Fairytales (Svelnosbreen) and Smørstabbreen, both situated in Jotunheimen National Park. These blue/white glaciers offers a spectacular adventure. A tasty lunch will be served while sitting between high towers and deep blue cracks. NWR's glacier crew will help provide all the equipment and the safety needed. Glaciering with NWR could very well be the highlight of your vacation in Norway.

Season: May - October

Elk safari

This Elk Safari offers a very special adventure. We will visit Jotunheimen, an important winter grazing area for several hundred elk. Equipped with snowshoes or skis we can come very close and experience this beautiful and wild animal in its natural habitat. Warm lunch is served while sitting around the sparkling open fire.

Season: December - February

Here is how to order!

Small game hunting

NWR offers 3000.000 acres, a guide and well trained dogs. It's fascinating to experience how the dogs work together with the hunter (you). You can hunt hare, woodland birds and the mountain grouse.
A tasty lunch is served out in the bush around the open fire.
In September and October the mountain is dressed in bright red, orange and yellow. It's beautiful.
