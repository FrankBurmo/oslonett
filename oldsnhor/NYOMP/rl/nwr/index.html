Norwegian Wildlife & Rafting AS
Scandinavias leader in the rafting and wilderness industry!

Established in '84, Norwegian Wildlife & Rafting AS (NWR) is the pioneer of Norwegian rivers. Our knowledge, safety, equipment, service and experience make us Scandinavias leader in the rafting industry. Every year more than three thousand satisfied clients have joined our highly qualified guides for a safe journey down the glittering waters of the Sjoa river. NWR's experience is your safety.

We offer all these services.

  • White water rafting (Sjoa river)
  • Rock Climbing
  • Fishing
  • Trekking
  • Grotting
  • Canyoning
  • Glaciering
  • Elk safari
  • Small game hunting
