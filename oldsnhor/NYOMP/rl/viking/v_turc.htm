Tour C. Wilderness adventure and Greenland one your own.

The area around Kangerlussuaq (Søndre Strømfjord) varies from round grassy hilly parts with idyllic fjords to foaming aolume of water, deep rifts and impressing and tremendous glacier falls. Both the fjord and most mountain lakes are rich with fish and reindeers and flocks with musk make the area very exiting. The area is an eldorado, for fishermen, hunters, hikers and for those who like mountain - biking.

Day by day.

1 To Kangerlussuaq - Preparations, instructions and packing.

2 By foot in the valley of Sandfluktdalen. The Glacier camp is set up close to the tremendous glacier front.

3-6 With starting points at the Glacier camp, we offer the following:

Day tours, in a group or on your own, photographing wild nature and animal life, hunting (see in the price list about hunting), fishing, canoeing at the lake right beside the camp (canoes free to borrow), two days tour in the tremendous ice fall at the ice cap.

7-8 We walk for two days along a mountain area north of Kangerlussuaq, to the fjord. From there, we us boats to the fjord Umivit. The Fjord Camp is set up.

9-12 With the starting point at the Fjord Camp we offer the following:

Two days tour to the lake Iluliartoq where the glacier calves in the water, we pass through a fascinating canyon and a small, but very rich fishing lake. Day tours, in a group or on your own. Hunting (the area is full with hare and birds). Fishing in lakes and in the fjord (many sea-trouts). Canoeing on the fjord.

13 By foot back to Kangerlussuaq.

14 Optional activities. We recommend the following:

Rent tricycles, helicopter sightseeing over the glacier, go 1 1/2 day to Nuuk (Godthåp)

15 Return to Copenhagen / Oslo.

Participants of The Wilderness Adventure must have camping experience. The tour suits both the experienced outdoor people, those who like forest / mountain tours and families with children. Those who want to participate in the two days excursions from the two camps, must be in relatively good physical shape. Parents with small children can also attend both 2-days tours. We arrange two 2 days' tours from both camps.

All participants participate in setting up and operate the camps.

Greenland on your own.

We can divide Tour C in two: One week in one of the camps, and one week on your own.

Price and terms of travel.

The days on your own can for example be used as your activities in Kangerlussuaq- districts or go by plane / helicopter to Sisimiut (an idyllic city where one meets true Greenland culture) or to the capital city of Nuuk (Godthåb)

Experience this fantastic exiting country with the splendid arctic nature, rich animal life, pleasant people and enormous inland ice You don't need to be a professional adventurer to experience Greenland`s wilderness, but you must enjoy camping and like splendid and isolated nature.
