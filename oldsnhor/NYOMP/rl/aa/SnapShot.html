Arctic Explorer mooring on West-Spitzbergen, in a natural harbour called «Sally Harbour».

Arctic Explorer - in our fourth season, stands for expeditions with good friends. This popular series of programs had a repeated sale of 60% to previous participants last season, and when presenting the 1996 plans for new explorations, we hope they will reflect recognition for those of you who have been requesting programs in these areas.

Main exploration area in 1996, is Helgeland with its fjords, mountains, glaciers, impressive coastline and islands. 8 separate tours are scheduled, see expedition list for 1996 for prices and further details.

We also have augmented the whale safaris, primarly to observe the Sperm Whale, but also to do thorough research on the Fin Whale, whose reappearance has been discovered by Arctic Explorer projects.

Constant new exposing of stone age culture findings above the Polar Circle, make us feel excitement by looking into an unpredictable history. We therefore continue the programs where we have a closer look on the cave paintings & engravings.
