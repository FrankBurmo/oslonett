The Oslo Package

The Oslo Package includes hotel accommodation, breakfast and the Oslo Card. The Oslo Package costs from NOK 295,- per person in a double room per night. The prices are low, but standards are high. Choose among 32 hotels in all categories. Most hotels are situated close to the centre of town within easy access of the tourist attractions. All the hotels are close to the tram or bus stops or underground or train stations.

A good, hearty breakfast to set you up for the day is included in the prices. We can promise you that you will need it if you are planning to see the whole of Oslo ! Children sleeping free of charge in an extra bed will naturally also include breakfast.

No extra costs for up to two children under the age of 16 sleeping in extra beds in their parents room. Children also receive their own Oslo card.

In addition, we can offer bargains on train tickets from most points in Norway and Sweden to Oslo. Tickets to theatre, shows and sports events are being arranged upon request.

The Package is available all weekends (Thursday - Monday) throughout the year, as well as every day in the period from June 12th - August 20th 1995.

To book the Oslo package, Contact Your travel agent. The package may also be purchased over the counter at the tourist information centres at Vestbanen and Oslo Central Station.
