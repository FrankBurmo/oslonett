Facts about Oslo

Oslo was founded in 1048 AD. In the year 1624 a terrible fire destroyed most of the city which was then rebuilt by King Christian IV and named Christiania. The city reverted to its original name of Oslo in 1925.

The geographical centre of the city is Høgåsen, a hill north-east of Sognsvann lake in the Nordmarka forest. The highest point is Kyrkjeberget in Nordmarka 630 meters (1890 feet) above sea level.
