A Chronological History of Oslo

1048              Oslo is founded by Harald Hardraade.


1299              King Haakon V builds Akershus Fortress.

approx. 1349 - 50 The Black Plague.

1397              The Calmar Union: Denmark, Sweden and Norway  united.

1536              Norway becomes a Danish province, through a royal
                  marriage.

1537              The Protestant  (Lutheran)  Reformation

1624              Major  city fire. Oslo is moved and rebuilt to the
                  area behind Akershus Fortress and named Christiania
                  (after the Danish King Christian 4th.

1697              Our Saviour's Church (Oslo Cathedral) is consecrated
                  in Christiania.

From 1800         Start of the golden years for timber trade.

1811              The royal King Fredriks University is founded in Oslo.

1814              Christiania becomes the capital. The Norwegian
                  constitution signed at Eidsvoll. Norway comes into union
                  with Sweden.

1837              Municipal self-government is introduced in Norway.

1848              The Royal Palace is completed.

1854              The first railway opened from Kristiania to Eidsvoll.

1894              First Electric street car.

1898              First suburban tram line to Holmenkollen.

1905              The Union with Sweden  is dissolved. Norway becomes an
                  independent nation.

1925              The city changes its name from Kristiania to Oslo.

1939              Oslo Airport (Fornebu) is opened.

1948              Major extension of the city.  

1950              The new City Hall is  inaugurated.

1952              The Olympic Winter Games in Oslo.

1959              Inauguration  of The Norwegian Opera House.

1963              Opening of the Munch Museum

1966              The New metro system is opened.

1977              Inauguration of  the Oslo Concert Hall 

1982              World Ski Championship of  Nordic events,  is
                  arranged in Oslo.

1990              "Fjell-linjen" tunnel is opened, leading the main
                  traffic under the city.

1993              The Oslo Peace Agreement
