Accomodation

A wise old adage says that where there is room for the heart, there's room to stay. In Oslo at any rate there's never any problem finding a room for the night with a total of 12.500 hotel beds. But then the city has also been called "the town with the big heart". So where in this myriad of hospitality should one check in? The choice is large and varied, and it is good to know that hotel standards are very high everywhere. Note that hotel prices vary according to the time of year and the days of the week. Check for favourable summer and week-end rates! See also the Oslo Package for reasonable weekend and summer rates. The package includes hotel acoomodation, breakfast and the Oslo Card.
