By boat

Båtservice Sightseeing A/S,

Rådhusbryggen 3,
N-0l05 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 20 07 15,
telefax +(47) 22 41 64 10.

All tours depart from Pier 3 in front of the Oslo City Hall. Ordinary tickets available from Tourist Information counter at the Norway Information Centre.

Mini-Cruise;
50 min. boat ride which gives you an interesting view of the harbour and the city, the ancient fortress of Akershus, and picturesque islands in the inner part of the Oslofjord.

Departure daily every hour on the hour from 10.00 a.m. - 8.00 p.m. in the period May 19th to August 23rd.

Fjord-cruise;
2 hours tour for tourists wishing to see more of the summer life, with beaches & boating. Refreshments available onboard.

Depart daily 10.30 a.m., 01.00 p.m., 03.30 p.m., 05.45 p.m. in the period May 1st to September 20th.

Fjord-cruise with lunch buffet;
2 hour Fjord Cruise, with stop over at restaurant Najaden to enjoy the famous lunch buffet.
Daily departure 10.30 a.m. in the period May 1st to September 20th.

Evening fjord cruise incl. maritime buffet;
2 hours Fjord Cruise, then ashore at restaurant Najaden to enjoy the grand maritime buffet.
Departure daily ex. Sundays 05.45 p.m. in the period June 23rd to August 15th.

Grand Tour of Oslo;
Ideal combination of fjord cruise and sightseeing by coach, including one hour lunch. Visits to the Polar Ship Fram Museum, the Kon-Tiki Museum, the Norwegian Folk Museum, the Viking Ships Museum, the Vigeland Sculpture Park and the Holmenkollen Ski Jump.
Departure daily 10.30 a.m. in the period June 1st to August 30th.

A summer night on the Fjord
Visit to a private garden by the waterfront, where there will be a taste of Norwegian specialities. Coffee and cakes served on the way. A 3 hour tour with the midsummer spirit.
Departures: July 3, 10, 17, 24, 31 & August 8 at 6.30 p.m.

By bike

Tour 1,
Every day of the week, min. 4 people: Bike and boat in the Oslo fjord area (Oslo-Nesodden-Oslo) Round trip by bike on the charming Nesodden peninsula. Ferry back and forth to Oslo. Lunch included at Paviljongen restaurant, Nesodden. About 8 hours.

Tour 2,
Friday, min. 4 people: Bygdøy-Frognerparken sightseeing. (Vestbanen-Frognerparken-Bygdøy-Vestbanen) A round trip to the highlights Frognerparken and Bygdøy. After a short visit to the sculpture park, we continue on winding roads on the beautiful peninsula of Bygdøy. Stop for lunch a visit at the Kon Tiki museum. About 3,5 hours.

Tour 3,
Every day of the week, min. 4 people: Short-Tour of Nordmarka (Oslo-Frognerseteren-Skjennungen-Oslo) By bus or subway to Frognerseteren. On to Skjennungen by bike, to enjoy a snack and the beautiful view. All the way back to Vestbanen on quiet roads by bike. We make a short stop in Frognerparken. About 4 hours. .

Tours 4,
Every day of the week, min. 4 people: Nordmarka overnight. (Oslo-Stryken-Kikut-Oslo) We take the train to Stryken, and bike through the forest. No traffic, beautiful scenery, peaceful and quiet. Overnight at the Kikut Inn in the middle of the forest. Next day we try canoeing on the lake of Bjørnsjøen before biking back to Oslo. About 34 hours

Tour 5,
Friday, min. 4 people: Nordmarka touring (Oslo-Stryken-Kikut-Oslo). By NSB train to Stryken in Nordmarka. Riding through the forest on quiet gravel roads. Traditional Norwegian lunch at the Kikut Inn. About 9 hours.

Booking: one day in advance in most hotels, tourist information or directly to Den Rustne Eike Sykkel-Tur og Utleie, Vestbaneplassen 2, N-0250 Oslo, tel.: (47) 22 83 72 31, telefax (47) 22 83 63 59.
