Museums and Sights
-in the Surrounding Area

Asker Museum, Valstad Collections

Kirkevn. 68, N-1364 Hvalstad,
tel.: +(47) 66 79 00 11

An artists home with painting exhibitions and exotic garden.

Blaafarveværket, (Cobolt blue works)

N-3340 Amot In Modum,
tel.: +(47) 32 78 49 00

100 km west of Oslo. The Royal Blaafarveværk (Cobolt Blue Mines) was established in 1772. The mines are open to the public. The original factory buildings are preserved. Each summer a retrospective exhibition of famous Scandinavian artists. Nearby, the famous Haugfossen - the highest waterfall in eastern Norway.

Bærums Iron Works and Oven Museum

N-1353 Bærums Verk,
tel.: +(47) 67 13 00 18

Shops, workshops and exhibitions in idyllic surroundings in the old iron works from 1610. The Oven Museum displays approx. 60 ovens which illustrate the history of the oven, its styles and types throughout a period of almost 300 years. Pots, functional and ornamental items from our grandparents day.

Eidsvoll building

Eidsvollminnet, Carsten Ankersvei,
N-2074 Eidsvoll Verk,
tel.: +(47) 63 95 13 04

80 km. north of Oslo. A manor where Norway's constitution was written and signed by the new National Assembly in 1814.

Hadeland Glassworks

N-3520 Jevnaker,
tel.: +(47) 63 31 10 00/63 31 05 55

The oldest existing glassworks in Norway, in activity since 1770. Scandinavia's largest Glass Museum. See glassblowing demonstrated in the workshop. Handmade glass at reasonable prizes in the gift shop. Restaurant.

Høvikodden / Henie-Onstad Art Centre

N-1311 Høvikodden, tel.: +(47) 67 54 30 50, telefax +(47) 67 54 32 70

Museum of international modern art donated by figure skater Sonia Henie and her husband Niels Onstad, with comprehensive permanent and Ad-Hoc exhibitions. Display of figure skater Sonia Henie's prize collection.

Oscarsborg Fortress

N-1440 Drøbak,
tel.: +(47) 64 93 50 87

Built in 1853 and named after King Oscar I of Sweden and Norway. During the invasion of Norway on 9 April 1940, the German warship Blücher was sunk by shots fired from Oscarsborg.

Tregaarden's Christmas House

Torget, N-1440 Drøbak,
tel.: +(47) 64 93 42 60

Scandinavia's only all-the-year-round Christmas House with its own postmark.
