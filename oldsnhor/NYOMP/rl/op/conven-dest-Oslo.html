OSLO - CONVENTION DESTINATION

Oslo Promotion A/S, Oslo Visitors and Convention Bureau

Oslo Promotion is a non-profit making organization with a two-fold objective to market Oslo as an international convention city and to help convention officials in the planning of their events.

The Bureau provides free information on Oslo as a venue for meetings and conventions. In addition the Bureau offers its experience and expertise through a wide range of services, all of which are free, like coordination of site inspection visits for organizers, advice on social programs, assistance in choosing suitable convention facilities, accomodation and convention suppliers as required etc.

Oslo - the Capital of Norway - is situated in the heart of Scandinavia, surrounded by magnificent scenery from the fjord to the forested hills. The center of Oslo is small and compact, and most hotels, meeting facilities, shopping areas, and entertainment are within walking distance.

The political climate in Norway is stable, and our crime rates are low. Our clean environment, with fresh air and clean water, is also an important virtue.

Oslo has become a popular destination for international conventions and an increasing number of meetings within art, culture, science, medicine, economics and international politics show Oslo's competitiveness within this field.

General situation - Hotels

Oslo has undergone a tremendous change over the last 4-5 years, and offers now up to date accomodation in all categories, ranging from first-class to budget hotels, student accomodation, hostels and private accomodation.

45 hotels with a total of 5543 rooms ( 10.575 beds).

In the area around Oslo another 10 hotels have appx 1000 rooms (2000 beds).

Congress Centers

Oslo is today a well equipped conference city, and has hotels and meeting facilities for up to 7.000 persons.

Technical equipment

Oslo has several companies that rent out technical equipment, like installations for interpretation, systems for polling, equipment for video-taping and transmission etc.

Interpreters

Well trained conference interpreters are available.

Professional Congress Organizers (PCO's)

Technical assistance (printing, registration, etc) is available through local PCO's (Professional Congress Organizers), who are both knowledgeable and experienced.
