Telematikksystemer AS

A knowledgebased company within communications

Telematikksystemer is a company within the Upnet-group which has its headquarter in Stockholm. Upnet is positioning itself to become the leading internetworking company within the Nordic countries. In Norway, Upnet has more than 50 employees within internetworking, with a turnover in excess of 100 mill. NOK.

Telematikksystemer's primary business idea is to increase customers competitiveness by supplying services and products related to integrated and intelligent network solutions. To meet these goals Telematikksystemer has built a unique base of knowledge in relevant technologies. Telematikksystemer's ambition is to be considered as a strategic partner within communications by its customers, by contributing to maintaining the customers communications cost at a minumum though still meeting the defined requirements. In addition to supplying cost effective network solutions, Telematikksystemer have large involvements in operational support of customer networks. This involvement has given us considerable experience with equipment supplied by Telematikksystemer, as well as other manufacturers' equipment. As a result Telematikksystemer has gained a solid experience in PC/PC-networking, minimachines, mainframes and PABXs in integrated networks.
