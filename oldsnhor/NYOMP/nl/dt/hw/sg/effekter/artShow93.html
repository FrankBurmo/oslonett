CyberArt, WeirdArt, ChickenFart...

Silicon Graphics Artshow 1993

The following pieces were done for the 1st Annual SGI Employee Artshow. The criteria was that all pieces had to have been generated by an SGI workstation... and you couldn't have your mother do it for you. Those were about the only rules so the playing field was wide open. These images were done mostly late at nite so none of them really have any profound philosophical meaning. But heck, when I was finally conscious the next morning, it was a trip to see what was conceived the night before and what midnite oil could do to your brain.
