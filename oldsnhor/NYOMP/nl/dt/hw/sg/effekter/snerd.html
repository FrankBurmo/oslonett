Webster Definition for "SNERD"

1. snerd \'sn*rd\ n [ME, fr. OE; L nerdum, Gk eirein to sa] an individual who is able to see those doggon' SIRDS (Single Image Random Dot Stereograms) in less than 0.5 seconds. Often nerdy and seen standing next to you at the mall secretly laughing at you. Usually have bloodshot eyes.
origin: SIRDS + nerds = SNERDS.
