NEWS bulletin March 28 1995:
Two day courses in geo-mechanics, including one day well integrity and one day basic user training of the PFAS software, will be given to a Norwegian Oil Company, first time in April 1995.

NEWS bulletin March 14 1995:
ITC will offer Internet training courses on three levels, user, expert user and developer from May 1995.

NEWS bulletin February 15 1995:
Version 2.0 of the inversion technique in PFAS is under development and will be introduced in September 1995. The new features will include automatic analysis of large data sets, filed simulation facilities, shear stress as input parametre as well as automatic generation of rose diagrams, based on the statistics from the results.

NEWS bulletin January 17 1995
ITC will present the paper "Experience, status and future plans with an IWCF level Computer Based Training and Field Tool system" at the IADC well control conference in Milano 7-9 June. This will be the first show of the IWCF trainer under Windows and the CBTmaker under Windows.
