In-situ Stress Determination and Evaluation at the Tampen Spur Area.

Jørgensen, T. , Bratli, R.K., "In-situ Stress Determination and Evaluation at the Tampen Spur Area.", Rock stresses in the North-Sea, NTH Trondheim 13-14 Feb. 1995.

Summary

In a report done exclusively for Saga Petroleum a.s in Norway, the in-situ stress field around the Tampen Spur area in the Norwegian North Sea has been determined and evaluated, using the "In-situ Stress Inversion Technique" [1],[3]. The report from this study is in Norwegian and this brief English summary gives the results and conclusions of the study. The data has been provided by Saga Petroleum a.s from its well database. Because some of the fields are operated by others than Saga Petroleum a.s, scarcity of data from these fields resulted in exclusion of some of them from the determinations of the in-situ stress field orientation.

The inversion showed that the regional trend of the stress field was reflected in the upper parts (Tertiary and Cretaceous), but is rotated in the lower, Jurassic parts. A similar rotation is also noted from break-out analysis.

The authors of this report invite operating companies in general and particularly operating companies in the North Sea to join forces in similar studies for other areas, where other methods for in-situ stress determination are used (Breakouts Analysis, Differential Strain Curve Analysis (DSCA), Anelastic Strain Recovery (ASR), etc.).
