OVNSIM (Furnace Simulator)

This was the kickoff-project for founding Computers and Learning A/S, and it yielded many useful results. The software resulting from the project was intended for use in vocational training of skilled workers to better their understanding of ..smelteovner.. . The success of this project resulted in considerable activity in utilizing 'CAL' (Computer Assisted Learning) in vocational training in Norway. The project was funded by the Federation of Norwegian Process and Manufacturing Industries.

PAPIRSIM (Paper Simulator)

The pulp and paper industry in Norway was also interested in utilizing PCs in vocational training, and started their own development activity while also both evaluating and buying some of the best available software from sources abroad. The Papirsim project went on for five years, and a considerable amount of courseware was developed. Most of this courseware was tailored to production and production equipment at the largest pulp and paper plants in Norway, and is therefore not available for external use. The project was started with financial support from the Norwegian government and continued by the industrys own vocational training school 'Treforedlingsindustriens Yrkesopplæring'.

SEMSIM and CEMENT (Cement Simulator)

In cooperation with the Norwegian cement manufacturer Norcem in Brevik, Norway, CANDLE has put a considerable amount of work into the projects SEMSIM and CEMENT. The first part of the project (SEMSIM) was financed by the Norwegian government, and when this funding ended in 1991, another project part called CEMENT was started in 1992 in which CANDLE worked with both Norwegian Norcem A/S and Swedish Cementa AB who financed the project jointly. The resulting courseware 'CEMENT' is now in use both at Norcem and Cementa, and cement-manufacturers in other countries want to have it translated to other languages.

In both SEMSIM and CEMENT animations are used as the main pedagogical tool. The animations are used to illustrate and explain how dynamic processes in ovens and machines work. A common factor for all these processes is that they are visually inaccessible in real life. The animations show in simple and intuitive ways what goes on.

PRO2SIM (Production Process Simulator)

Pro2Sim consists of animated simulations of an industrial mixing and boiling process. It was developed by CANDLE in cooperation with the Norwegian 'Næringsmiddelindustriens Landsforening'. The three steps of weighing, heat-treatment and storage management may be simulated separately or combined, and an economic model calculates the bottom line of one week of simulated production. The program is available free of charge in Norway, and can be copied freely as long as the copyright notices are not removed. Exists in Norwegian only.

Aluminium Elektrolyse (Aluminum Electrolysis)

This course presents some aspects of the production of aluminum by electrolysis. It was developed by CANDLE in cooperation with Hydro Aluminium Sunndal, which is a large Norwegian aluminum plant. The courseware is avaible free of charge in Norway, and can be copied freely as long as the copyright notices are not removed. Exists in Norwegian only.
