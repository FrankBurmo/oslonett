Consulting and development services

As described elsewhere in this presentation, Computers and Learning A/S has successfully developed several packages of CAL (Computer Aided Learning) software since 1988, and is currently one of the most experienced such developers in Scandinavia.

Computers and Learning A/S as a company keeps a relatively low profile and does not advertise anywhere else outside these WWW-pages. Our main assets are our knowledge and expertise and our customers satisfaction.

In addition to developing software for educational use in schools and industry, we also do consulting work on various related subjects, such as user interfaces, strategies in educational use of information technology etc.
