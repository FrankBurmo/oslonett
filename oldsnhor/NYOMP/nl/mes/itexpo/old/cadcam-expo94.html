The CAD/CAM market is growing rapidly, and the expected growth in turnover for hardware and software in the period up to 1997, is a full 15%. The planning of the most important and only event for this market in Norway, is well on the way. Cad/CAM EXPO'94 will run from August 30th to September 1st, in Oslo Spektrum. By putting AutoCAD EXPO under the CAD/CAM-umrella, we will gather all of the Norwegian CAD/CAM environment in one event.

User oriented subjects linked to lectures, are keywords for the conferences, well taken care of by a technically broad, and solid committee. The whole specter of computer assisted tools, both on the design-side and on the production side, will be focused upon. We encourage the exhibitors to supplement the technical full meeting activities with their own seminar sessions, linked to their own products, and their own technical competence.

Target Visitors

Consultants, CAD/CAM Managers and users in building and constructions, offshore, engineering industry, mechanical industry and productions, architects, engineers and designers.

Target Exhibitors

CAD/CAM vendors, harware vendors, AutoCAD distributors, application developers, trade solutions, consultants/servicees, communications/networks.

Practical Information

You can choose between an unfurnished or a furnished stand. An unfurnished stand includes separation walls, front curtain, cleaning and garbage removal. A furnished stand has, in addition to the above padded walls by your choice of color, work-desks, shelves for brochures, furniture, sign for the stand, electricity sockets and spotlights.

Rent of an unfurnished stand is NOK 13.950 per booth. For a furnished stand, the rent is NOK 22.900 per booth. Enrollment fee is NOK 3.000. Prices do not include VAT.

Last date for enrollment is June 20, 1994.
