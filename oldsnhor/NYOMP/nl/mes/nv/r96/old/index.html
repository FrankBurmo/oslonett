Reiseliv '96 is the 9th annual travel trade fair in Oslo, Norway. The fair is the gateway to the booming Norwegian market, where the travel industry benefits from the public's increased disposable income, as well as a national economy on the upswing. Reiseliv is the place to meet and do business with customers, suppliers and colleagues in the travel industry, as well as the general public.

Over the last eight years, Reiseliv in Oslo has manifested its position as Norway's premier travel and tourism fair, and one of the most important meeting places in Scandinavia for the trade. And for your guaranty of quality, Reiseliv has become a member of ETTFA (European Tourism Trade Fairs Association). Every January, the major players in the travel industry meet with existing and potential key customers, suppliers and businesspartners at Reiseliv.

In addition, national and international travel organizations select this fair as the venue for their meetings, seminars and conferences, thus emphasizing Reiseliv's position as the industry's meeting point.

At Reiseliv '95, 7.700 trade visitors attended Reiseliv, a 10 percent increase from 1994. They represent, in addition to travel agencies, tour operators and transportation companies, important professional buyers from Norwegian business and government organizations.
