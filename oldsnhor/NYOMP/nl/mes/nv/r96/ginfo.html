General Information

Type of fair: Trade and public fair
Dates: 11. - 14. January 1996
Trade days - all day Thursday 11th as well as Friday 12th January until 1400 hours.
Place of event: Sjølyst Exhibition Centre, Oslo - Norway

NOK 30,- Children under 14 years and pensioners
Organizers: Reiseliv is organized by the Norwegian Trade Fair Foundation in collaboration with The Norwegian Tourist Board (NORTRA).

Reiseliv is a member, and act according to the rules of European Tourism Trade Fairs Association (ETTFA) and Scandinavian Fair Control (SFC).
