Computers, peripherals and software

Office and Computerisation'96 aims to present the latest information technology products and future solutiios to industry and commerce in Norway. Modern technology opens new opportunities, and Norwegian business executives will be at the show to find their way around a major market and discover the opportunities available. Make sure your company is there to assist inquiring visitors hungry for news. A broad-based exhibition offers people a complete overview is clearly the most effective approach to a large field. Our aim is to secure even better representation from IT firms at Office and Computerisation '96, and we would like to extend an invitation to everyone with something to offer in such areas as:

Software * Technical data processing * Networks/communications * Multimedia/graphics/virtual reality * Hardware/peripherals * Data security * Expertise/training * Desktop publishing

System software is the basis for all computerisation, and we will be focusing on:

Windows '95 * Windows NT * DOS * Macintosh * OS/2 * UNIX and others
