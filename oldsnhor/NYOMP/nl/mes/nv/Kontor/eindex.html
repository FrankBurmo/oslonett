Office & Computerisation'96 invites your company to exhibit at Norway's largest show in its field from 9-13 September 1996.

This event is a collaboration between the Norwegian Office Machine and Equipment Dealers Association (KDL) and the Norwegian Trade Fair Foundation. It aims to present a broad spectrum of products and services in the field.
As the only forum in Norway to provide customers with an overall view of the sector, Office and Computerisation attracts some 40 000 potential buyers. So it obviously pays to be there. And participation yields results. Exhibitor judgements of previous shows speak for themselves.
  • 98% made new contacs during the show.
  • 90% were extremely or very satisfied with Office and Computerisation '94.
  • 91% were extremely or very satisfied with the number of visitors.
  • 89% were extremely or very satisfied with the professional standard of visitors.
  • 95% were extremely or very satisfied with the professional standard of the show.
  • 64% intended to exhibit in 1996, 36% did not know, but nobody said they would definetely stay away.
The show in 1994 was ranked by exhibitors as significantly better than competing arrangements in Norway. In 1996 we have the following categories.
  • Computers, peripherals and software
  • Office furniture and environment
  • Office machines and equipment
  • Telecommunications
