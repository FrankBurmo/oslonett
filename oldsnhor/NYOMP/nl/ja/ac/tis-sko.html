Tekniske utviklingsmuligheter

Som tekniker får du anledning til å gå på en rekke seminarer/ presentasjoner i Norge - for å følge med på det nye som kommer på markedet. I tillegg har Andersen Consulting en rekke tekniske spesialkurs som avholdes i Chicago eller Nice. Nedenfor følger noen smakebiter:

  • Object Week: Seminar with outside Object Technology experts - like Grady Booch - and Andersen Consulting experts. Focus on the newest in Object Technology, architectures and methodology.

  • Multi-User Systems Workshop: Focus on supplying the firm with technical knowledge in areas of architectures, database technologies, case technologies & migragtion to C/S environments. Provides an opportunity to share firm experiences in the use of enterprise system tech, and to review trends in enterprise system technologies.

  • Object Modeling School: Object Modeling School prepares object analysts for an object technology engagement. The focus of the school is on the creation of object models within a simulated case environment.

  • Object Tech C++ Prog for C Programmers: This five-day vendor-led course will focus on the fundamentals of C++ programming. More specifically participants will idenify the advantages of the object-oriented approach to systems building and gain practical experience with object-oriented development, tools and techniques.

  • Enterprise Information Architecture Workshop: To provide a forum for sharing enterprise information architecture engagement experiences. Focuses on leading edge technologies that have a significant impact on systems architectures.

  • Emerging Technologies: Emerging Technologies workshop reviews a mixture of current and future advanced technologies. Technologies will include object oriented programming, knowledge based systems, constraint based reasoning, case based reasoning, image systems, and hypermedia.

  • Hypermedia workshop: Purpose of this workshop is to provide an awareness & overview of the latest multimedia technology. It focuses on technology used by our internal consultants on engagements, as well as technology being used by clients. In addition, workshop discusses ideas for possible uses on client engagements.

  • Network Solutions Workshop: Objective is to enhance ability to deliver end-to-end solutions in a changing computing environment. Scope includes current & future trends, analysis of relevant engagement experiences and discussionof issues.

  • Principles of Technical Architecture: Five day engagement simulation emphasis: prepare participants to function in the role of a technical architect. Work in teams to develop a technical architecture. Resources on-line knowledge support system.

  • Network Architecture School: Purpose of the school is to prepare participants to design & install telecommunications systems to meet business needs. Primary focus is on network design & management of telecommunications systems. In addition the school examines the role of telecommunications in systems integration.
