Kings Bay Kull Compani AS

The Kings Bay Kull Compani AS is a state-owned limited company, answerable to the Department of Energy and Commerce. The company owns Ny-Ålesund and about 300 km2 of land around Kongsfjorden. The company's income comes from its hotel, messroom, cafe, shop, airport, quay and visiting cruise ships. In addition Kings Bay Kull Company construct buildings which are leased to scientific research organisations.

From Ny-Ålesund there are flights to Longyearbyen three times a week in July and August, twice a week in May, June and September, and once a week during the winter. In June, July and August there are also boats from Longyearbyen and a regular weekly boat service from Tromsø.
