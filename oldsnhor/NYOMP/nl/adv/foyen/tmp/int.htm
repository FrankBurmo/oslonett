International expertise - local services

Advokatfirmaet Føyen & Co Ans is one of Norway's 10 largest law firms and forms part of an international group of attorneys practising under the Føyen name. The firm currently serves small and medium-sized companies as well as large corporations and enterprises, multinationals, individuals, associations, organizations and public bodies.

The Føyen group offers legal services in five countries. Clients requiring international legal assistance can enlist the services of the local Norwegian-based attorneys or apply directly to the non-resident attorneys. Clients receiving assistance from non-resident attorneys may apply at any time to the responsible attorney in this country.

Whenever legal problems arise in other countries, Føyen & Co. Ans can be of assistance in finding the right law firm for the case in question, also in countries where we do not operate directly. We are continually improving our international network of well-reputed law firms and contacts.
