Company profile

The law firm Føyen & Co Ans has special competence in the fields of electronic data processing, telecommunications and information technology, in addition to the fields of building and contracting law, real estate law and property development. The majority of the attorneys in the firm have their main area of expertise in one of these specialist fields.

Føyen also handles a large volume of international assignments, particularly in England and in the United States.

We provide legal assistance in most areas of commercial law, including company law and strategy, financial and taxation questions, law governing duties and indirect taxes, banking and monetary institutions, competition, copyright, offshore, bankruptcy, labour law, etc. The building up of competence in relation to EEA and EU law has been a key issue for the law firm's attorneys. Through collaboration with the international law firm Masons in London, Føyen & Co. Ans has established a base in Brussels which will strengthen the company's competence appreciably in this connection.

In recent years the firm has handled numerous assignments for Norwegian and non-resident clients in connection with the acquisition of listed and non-listed companies, acting on behalf of buyers as well as sellers.

Settlement of disputes and serving as counsel in the courts represent a large part of our assignment volume. The firm is engaged in cases before the courts and before arbitration tribunals within the entire range of the law.
