Welcom Software Technology provides professional project management solutions

The Company

Welcom Software Technology is the international leader in professional, PC based project management solutions. Our aim is to provide quality software and associated services for organisations for which serious project management is a critical business requirement.

Established in 1983 by a team experienced in the disciplines of professional project management, Welcom's specialists have extensive experience working with over 10,000 customers worldwide who rely on project management as a core business function.

Welcom Software Technology's integrated products are functional and versatile solutions for managing both individual and enterprise wide project environments.

Headquartered in Houston, Texas, USA, the Company employs over 100 people and worldwide revenues for the year ending 1993 exceeded $10 million. Welcom has marketing and support offices located in Houston, London, Paris, Munich and Washington DC, with distributors in Norway and throughout the rest of the world.

Welcom's Products

Open Plan is the leading international high end project management software, combining professional project management functionality with the accessibility of the desktop PC. It provides extensive planning, resource and cost management facilities for single or multiple projects and uses the Xbase language and database as an integral part of the system. Open Plan runs on PCs and local area networks (LANs).

Cobra (Cost Breakdown Application) is a comprehensive cost management and performance measurement system. Initially designed to rigid US Department of Defense cost/schedule control system criteria, Cobra also offers proposal, funds and subcontractor management. Easily customised without laborious programming effort, Cobra can be configured to match the cost management needs of individual organisations. Cobra offers seamless integration with Open Plan, providing a coherent framework for ongoing control of detailed cost and performance analysis.

Opera is an extension to Open Plan designed to analyse and quantify the risks associated with uncertainties in project costs and activity durations.

WELCOM CASE STUDIES

OIS GIVEN A CLEAN BILL OF HEALTH

The Department of Health wanted to combine the implementation of a new Office Information System (OIS) with the relocation of 1,200 staff from London to Leeds.

The DoH used Welcom's Open Plan to handle the complex task of planning and scheduling the OIS implementation and evaluating the potential resourcing difficulties of the relocation.

PA Consulting Group was responsible for overall programme management, using a standard Open Plan template. The proof of the success of the OIS was that it was delivered on time and within budget.

GO WITH THE FLOW

South West Water plc is involved in a 10-year, 2 billion capital investment programme designed to improve water services and bathing waters in the South West. The programme includes over 1,600 schemes, ranging from a 100,000 sewage treatment works to a million marine project.

SWW's Project Services department has developed a system based on Welcom's Open Plan to handle a complex range of information and reporting procedures within the programme. Open Plan's menu and reporting functions have also provided a model for the development of SWW's bespoke cost management tool.

SCOPE FOR CHANGE

Courtaulds Engineering set up a three-year, 1 million project called SCOPE (Software and Computer Operations Project for Engineering) with the aim of realigning the company's IT systems to match its prime business activity of project management.

Based on Welcom's Open Plan and Cobra project and cost management systems, SCOPE incorporates a blend of packaged software and bespoke applications to cover all aspects of Courtauld's business and project management needs.

PROJECT MANAGEMENT PASSES THE ACID TEST

Acetic acid is a chemical found in a wide range of consumables, from food preservatives to plastics. At BP Chemicals' Hull plant, the largest acetic production complex in Europe, essential maintenance needs to be carried out quickly and efficiently to ensure production downtime is kept to a minimum.

MB Services, which carries out this maintenance, uses Welcom's Open Plan to manage the various complex procedures involved. Resourcing for each shutdown is planned and monitored using Welcom's Open Plan, and daily reports are produced to keep those involved up to date.

PRACTICE MAKES PERFECT

Hospital construction and refurbishment is part of a capital programme worth 350 million being undertaken by West Midlands-based The Landmark Practice.

Landmark is one of the largest multi-disciplinary consultancies in the UK, offering services ranging from estates' development to interior design. To help manage these services, Landmark uses Welcom's Open Plan to plan, summarise and report on thousands of activities within its capital programme.
