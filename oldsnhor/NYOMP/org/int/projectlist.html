Ongoing projects

Below are an overview of the mailinglists regarding ongoing projects at IPMA. If you want to join one of the lists, follow the description at the bottom of the page.
certification
Open for general information regarding certification matters in IPMA.

globnet
Open workgroups for all projectmanagers regarding global professional corporations.

pmed
Open for general information regarding education matters in IPMA.

pmed-group
Closed channel for members of the education project team.

pmqa
Open for general information regarding quality assurance in PM matters in IPMA.

pmqa-group
Closed channel for members of the quality assurance in PM project team.

pmresearch
Open for general information regarding PM reaserach matters in IPMA.

pmresearch-group
Closed channel for members of the PM reaserach project team.
If you want to join one of these lists, do as described below:
  1. You must send an email to the address: majordomo@oslonett.no, with empty subject-field.
  2. Put the following line in the body of the mail: "subscribe listname", where listname is one of the listnames certification, globnet, pmed, pmed-group, pmqa, pmqa-group, pmresearch or pmresearch-group.
If you need some help, you can contact John H. Heier at IPMA.
