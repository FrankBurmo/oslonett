SIG groups

Below are an overview of the IPMA mailing lists for SIG groups. If you want to join one of the lists, there is an description at the bottom of the page.
contract
Open for specific interest group SIG, contract management.

cost
Open for specific interest group SIG, cost control

pharmacy
Open for specific interest group SIG, pharmacy

plan
Open for specific interest group SIG, planning

risk
Open for specific interest group SIG, risk management

telecom
Open for specific interest group SIG, PM in telecom
If you want to join one of these lists, do as described below:
  1. You must send an email to the address: majordomo@oslonett.no, with empty subject-field.
  2. Put the following line in the body of the mail: "subscribe listname", where listname is one of the listnames contract, cost, pharmacy, plan, risk or telecom
If you need some help, you can contac John H. Heier at IPMA.
