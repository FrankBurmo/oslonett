Overall Focus

On behalf of the INTERNATIONAL PROJECT MANAGEMENT ASSOCIATION, The Norwegian Association of Project Management is proud to present the programme for INTERNET'94, The 12th World Congress on Project Management.

At the Congress, we will focus on the new dimension of leadership facilitated trough the methodology of project management.

Management Through Projects gives us not only a tool for exact specification of an undertaking in terms of definition an specification of the resulting deliverables, also we have the tools necessary to plan and control the course of action to meet the specified goal within defined time and resource constraints.

A key success factor is to make the vision and goal clearly understood by all involved parties, thus creating extraordinary enthusiasm and diligence amongst the members of the project team.

This is of particular importance in today's society where the very rapid changes in the technological, social and political environment constantly alters the ground rules on which industries and organizations are basing their operation.

No one can avoid this process, and as a consequence we can all benefit from learning to master the project methodology and thus realize the ultimate message of this Congress:

Dynamic Leadership Through Project Management

What do we mean by this statement?

Dynamic leadership is associated with a great number of qualities such as charisma, clear vision and goals, assertiveness, ability to think and act fast, and with adaptability.

Of course, introduction of a project oriented working method can not and will not give a leader or an organization these traits. We do not think that anybody can be a dynamic leader just by using project management methods.

Our aim is to try and demonstrate how project management can lead to a process where these qualities are visualized and gradually integrated into the operating procedures of a corporation of organization.

We think that a talented leader using project management tools can harness his organization to reach its objectives faster, with less resources and with greater precision.

The most significant benefit is that this management method removes much of the ambiguity and confusion that can inevitably lead to a bad professional and social environment,

Our logo, symbolizing an organization as a ship being safely led through rough waters and into the port by the lighthouse can be compared to the reality of the Norwegian coastal population in its struggle to build a modern country with the highest standard for economic and ecological development, health and social security.

We welcome you both to a professional and Nordic experience here in Oslo - in the same type of atmosphere that lead to the Middle East Peace Initiative in this very city.
