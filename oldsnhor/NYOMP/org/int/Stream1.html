Stream#1: Enhanced Value Generation

A primary Goal for the Dynamic leader

Session 1.1 Projects
Key to increased Value Generation.
Time: Thursday June 9, 1400 - 1800 hrs.

The session will focus on the key benefits of the project approach. This is the primary strategic goal for the champions of tomorrow. It will demonstrate the advantages of Management by Projects within a changing environment. The dynamic dimension is a dominant element and must be met with a proactive and visionary approach. 

Session 1.2 Management of Change
Strategic dimension for enhanced value generation.
Time: june 10, 0830 - 1200 hrs.

Management of Change is becoming more and more necessity for maintaining the competitive edge of your business. This session will highlight the processes related to management of change and its importance for establishing a winning culture. Management of Change should be included in the strategy and business plan of your enterprise in order for you to meet the needs of a continuing change process. 

Session 1.3 The Importance of the Project Organization
Time: friday june 10, 1300 - 1630 hrs.

The session aims to establish a new set of requirements for individuals working within the organization of the future in which increased employee commitment, co-operation and flexibility are fundamental for success. Emphasis will be on how a project organization can achieve this state of evolutionary process which is necessary for optimal value generation. 

Session 1.4 Make the most out of existing resources
Time: Saturday June 11, 0900 - 1230 hrs.

Enhanced value generation simply means more effective use of resources, whether they be people, equipment or natural resources. The process of increasing value generation are affected by our ability to structure and stimulate the creativity in people and organizations. In this session, we intend, through examples and theoretical models, to illustrate some of these effects.
