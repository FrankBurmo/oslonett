HOLMENKOLLEN ALL-ROUND, SUMMER AND WINTER.

Few sports arenas can equal Holmenkollen's combination of top sports, cultural events and recreation. The facilities around the jump are alive, summer and winter. Holmenkollen is all-round, for all.

JUMPING ARENA

The Holmenkollen Ski Jump, the jewel of the facilities is 103 years old. Yet it has been rebuilt 14 times, and is now a completely modern jump, with porcelain inrun tracks. The jump holds one unbeaten Winter Olympic record: 101.400 spectators watched the jump meet there during the 1952 Olympic Winter Games in Oslo.

Holmenkollen Ski Jump current hill record is 126,5 metres. Holmenkollen Jump facts: Vertical distance, outrun to top platform :121 metres.

CROSS- COUNTRY ARENA

The cross-country arena is made for activity, with course interchanges and a race organization building. Every year, the world's best cross-country ski racers meet here, as do 35,000 other racers and countless thousands of recreational skiers and walkers. The arena is the starting point and the finish of an extensive trail network.

HOLMENKOLLMARSJEN AND CHILDREN'S DAY

Skiers throng through the Nordmarka forest, on their way to a common goal; Holmenkollen. Their abilities vary, but they are together, in the Holmenkollmarsjen Citizen's Race. In the arena more than 6,000 children from 4 to 12 years old, enjoy their own events in the Holmenkollen Children's Day. They are all Holmenkollen racers.

A TOURIST ATTRACTION.

Holmenkollen is not only a sports arena, but also a tourist attraction that includes a magnificent view from the 60 metres tower. 417 metres above the fjord below. Some 62 millions tourists visit the arena every year, making the arena Norway's most visited.

THE SKI MUSEUM.

The Ski Museum at Holmenkollen is the world's oldest museum dedicated exclusively to ski equipment. It opened in 1923 and was integrated into the jump tower in 1952. In 1983, it was relocated in a new rock cavern building adjacent to the tower, and now has extensive exhibitions, including the gear used on Nansen's and Amundsen's polar expeditions, models of the Holmenkollen jump and displays of the royal family on skis.

SUMMER ARENA

Skiing remains a winter sport, but Holmenkollen is also a summer arena offering a spectrum of activities including cross-country running, orienteering, mountain biking, roller skiing and swimming, as well as concerts and entertainment programmes.

SUMMER CONCERTS

The Oslo Philharmonic Orchestra, under the baton of Mariss Jansons and prominent guest conductors, turns Holmenkollen into a summer festival, attended by 20,000 and seen direct on TV world-wide.

"CHANCE IT "

If you would like to fall in the water in front of 30,000 spectators and countless TV viewers, try it in "Chance it", the summer Holmenkollen entertainment programme featuring madcap cycles and boats. In the event, challenge, entertainment and fun are the prime ingredients.

OSLOMARKA

No other capital city can match Oslo in recreational facilities. Holmenkollen and the new Tryvann Ski centre are the gateways to the Nordmarka forest preserve, with alpine ski hills, ski jumps and 2,400 km of well prepared cross-country ski trails. And in the summertime the area is ideal for walking, cycling, fishing, swimming and other activities. The Skiforeningen lodges are open year round.
