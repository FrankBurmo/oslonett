Feedback on the NEUR-WWW service

Based on our experiences from the 1994 Winter Olympics, it is evident that a great number of people appreciate live events brought to them through the Internet and the World Wide Web.

The Norwegian European Union Referendum has of course a far smaller target group than the Winter Olympics(!), but we would like to collect statistics on how this service is used by Norwegians abroad and others. This material will be presented to the Department of Foreign Affairs and other governmental affiliates to educate them on how the Internet can be used to reach Norwegian citizens abroad and other people interested in what's happening in Norway.

So, as a courtesy to Oslonett, we kindly ask you to submit the below form, and at a minimum fill in your email address (if your browser didn't supply it) and check the appropriate radiobutton to indicate your nationality. If you feel for it, feel free to write a few lines too.

If you prefer to use email, use the address neuv@oslonett.no

Thank you for your time and feedback!
