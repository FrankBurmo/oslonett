OfficeNET . . . our virtual office is at your command

Picture yourself in this situation in the not too far distant future . . .

John Brown runs Just-In-Time Circuits (JCI), a small company that sells contract manufacturing services. John spends his money wisely to keep his company profitable -- that means he keeps overhead low. JCI's three-person sales team is really working hard to win new business, but they badly need new promotional literature. John has heard that OfficeNET can help, and best-of-all, the work can be done on a contract basis without having to hire an employee.

John types a quick e-mail to OfficeNET and faxes in his current literature with marked-up changes. Soon, an OfficeNET technical associate is hard at work. Two days later his new service data sheets are return e-mailed to John for review. A few minor changes are required that John calls to discuss. The next day his final proofs are electronically sent to the printing house.

JCI's full-color, glossy data sheets are ready to help sell the company's services within a week. The sales team is delighted and so is John. OfficeNET is his kind of company.
