BSDI Internet Server V2.0 - Powerful Internet and WWW Gateway for PC Platforms

 [logo]

Powerful BSDI Internet Server software makes it easy to setup and configure an Internet gateway and World Wide Web home page. The BSDI Internet Server includes bulletproof networking (TCP/IP, SLIP, and PPP), Internet servers and clients, EZ-config setup utilities, the X Window System, administration and monitoring software - everything required for connecting to the Internet as a full peer. Can function as an Internet router.

The BSDI Internet Server recently received INFOWORLD's Top Score Award. A 16-user configuration is priced at $995.00 and includes 60 days of installation support and a money-back guarantee.

BSDI is the commercial supplier of the state-of-the-art operating system, Internet, and networking technologies originally developed at the Computer Systems Research Group at the University of California, Berkeley. Leading computer scientists from the University of California's CSRG founded Berkeley Software Design, Inc. (BSDI) in 1991 to unleash the full commercial potential of the BSD Operating System.
