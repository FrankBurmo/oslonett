  2.0 WHAT IS NETSURFER DIGEST?
  
   Netsurfer Digest is your guide to interesting news, places, and
   resources online. Each week it brings you short, crisp newsbytes,
   notices, and reviews designed to provide an informative and
   entertaining snapshot of the vast wired world. Periodically you can
   also expect to see special longer features, a letters to the editor
   section, and possibly special bulletins about significant breaking
   stories (e.g. imminent death of the Internet).
   
  3.0 HOW MUCH DOES NETSURFER DIGEST COST?
   
   Netsurfer Digest is free. However, we presume our readers are mature
   enough to understand that in the real world of bills and taxes a
   business such as this can't survive without sponsors. Thus you can
   expect to see a trade supplement show up in your mailbox, no more then
   twice per week, and no larger then the average issue of the Digest.
   
   The goal is to deliver this material in a non-intrusive and clearly
   marked manner on the assumption that you are interested in who is
   offering what online. Read it at your leisure or delete it, it's part
   of the package and both we and the sponsors will be interested in your
   reaction to it.
   
  4.0 HOW IS NETSURFER DIGEST DELIVERED?
   
   Netsurfer Digest is delivered via E-mail directly to your mailbox. The
   Digest can reach you at any E-mail address which is capable of
   receiving mail from the Internet.
   
  5.0 IN WHAT FORMATS CAN I GET NETSURFER DIGEST?
   
   Netsurfer Digest comes in one of two formats, plain ASCII text, or
   HTML formatted text suitable for viewing with any World Wide Web
   browser. The HTML version has live links to all the sites named in the
   text so that with a simple click of a mouse you can connect to the
   site you have just been reading about.
   
   NOTE: You do not have to be connected directly to the Internet in
   order to enjoy the benefits of the HTML version. Any HTML browser,
   such as Mosaic or Lynx, can be used to read Netsurfer Digest in a
   nicely formatted style, much superior to boring old ASCII text.
   
   WE STRONGLY ENCOURAGE YOU TO SUBSCRIBE TO THE HTML VERSION.
   That way you can take advantage of various neat features we
   are working on for future versions of the digest.

  6.0 HOW DO I SUBSCRIBE TO NETSURFER DIGEST?
   
   Send all subscription requests to: nsdigest-request@netsurf.com
   
   To subscribe to the HTML version send the following command in the
   BODY of the message:

     subscribe nsdigest-html
   
   To subscribe to the TEXT version send the following command in the
   BODY of the message:

     subscribe nsdigest-text

   To unsubscribe send one of the following commands in the BODY of the
   message:
   
    unsubscribe nsdigest-html
    unsubscribe nsdigest-text
   
  7.0 HOW CAN I CHANGE MY SUBSCRIPTION FROM TEXT TO HTML FORMAT?

   Easy. Simply send the following to nsdigest-request@netsurf.com in 
   the BODY of the message:
 
    unsubscribe nsdigest-text 
    subscribe nsdigest-html

   If you want to switch from HTML to TEXT just reverse the order like so:

    unsubscribe nsdigest-html
    subscribe nsdigest-text

  8.0 HOW CAN I CHANGE MY DELIVERY ADDRESS?

   It's easy. Simply send an unsubscribe command from your old address
   and then send a subscribe command from your new address.

   Note that our subscription software automatically reads the address
   from the "From:" field of your E-mail message, so the subscribe
   and unsubscribe commands must come from the appropriate addresses.

  9.0 WHY DO YOU BOTHER MAILING THE HTML VERSION?
   
   Why not just put up the Web server? Well, there are really two reasons
   for this.
   
   First, the Web clients such as Mosaic and others make very nifty
   universal readers for all of our customers, even those without direct
   access to the Internet. Since we like our product to look good this
   seems the obvious solution to go with.
   
   The second reason is that we do not want our WWW server to be a choke
   point for our readers. Why have thousands of readers trying to hook up
   to our machine and potentially wind up in a giant traffic jam, just to
   link to an unrelated site they read about in the Digest? From the
   standpoint of reliability and bandwidth usage it makes sense to mail
   out HTML source.
   
  10.0 HOW COME I DID NOT GET MY LAST ISSUE OF NETSURFER DIGEST?

    We've been doing this long enough to know that there are a number of
    problems which crop up over and over again either due to factors
    beyond our control or due to our dizzy growth rate. 

    On any given mass mailing we get between 5%-10% bounced E-mail.
    This means our software tried to deliver to your address but could
    not. Some of the reasons we've seen why this happens:

      - Your machine or mailing software was down when we tried mailing.
      - Your file system was full.
      - Your address has changed.
      - The link to your site was down somewhere along the delivery chain.
      - Our mailing software starts acting up for utterly unknown reasons
        (well, usually it's because we've been mucking with our machines).
      - A conspiracy involving aliens, Elvis, and the Trilateral Commission.
      
    If all else fails you can always stop at our FTP or WWW sites to
    get the latest issue of Netsurfer Digest:

      http://www.netsurf.com/nsd/index.html
      ftp://ftp.netsurf.com/pub/nsd/

  11.0 HOW CAN I UNSUBSCRIBE TO NETSURFER DIGEST? 

    It hurts us to have to tell you how to do that. It really does. If it
    comes to this it means we have failed you, our gentle readers. If you
    unsubscribe there will be wailing and gnashing of teeth, and someone
    will throw themselves on the point of their mouse pad. So don't. Just
    tell us how to get better.
    
    Mail nsdigest-request@netsurf.com with one of the following in the 
    BODY of the message:
    
        unsubscribe nsdigest-html 
        unsubscribe nsdigest-text
