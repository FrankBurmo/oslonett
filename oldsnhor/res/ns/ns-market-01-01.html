Back Cover Blurb

Cyberspace is booming. Each month, millions of people are discovering the power of the Internet, online services, and corporate email systems. With this power comes responsibility.

People who wouldn't dream of burping at the end of dinner post offensive messages to international forums. Middle managers inadvertently send romantic email messages to the company-wide email alias. People at computer terminals forget that there are real live people on the other end of the wire. Topics are lost in noise, feelings are hurt, reputations are damaged, time and bandwidth are wasted.

There's no longer an excuse. This book brings etiquette to the bustling frontiers of cyberspace. In a series of entertaining essays, the author establishes the do's and the don'ts of communicating online, from the Golden Rule to the art of the flame, from the elements of electronic style to virtual romance. Accessible to both network wizard and clueless newbie, this is the first book to offer the guidance that all users need to be perfectly polite online.

CAPITALIST PIG - Business Game Tests Your Entrepreneurial Savvy

Capitalist Pig, the business simulation game for Windows and Macintosh, lets you succeed in business without risking it all. In the sound-and-animation filled game, you choose a product, line up capital, hire employees, and you're on your way to big bucks or bankruptcy.

The fun is backed by economic statistics from the twentieth century, and financial statements are always on hand, so you can learn economics and business as well. You're constantly making decisions: hire or fire? advertise? what about taxes? Your advisors (lawyer, accountant, economist, PR agent) give you sound advice, but will you follow it? Meanwhile, employees embezzle funds, fire breaks out, and a terrorist runs rampant.

Used in business classes and for management training, it's one game you've got to try.

PROGRAMMING GETS FASTER WITH CAUSE

If you're a programmer, you owe it to yourself to look at CAUSE, a programming tool from Pluma Software. Cause lets you create programs in hours instead of weeks because it has no language or syntax to learn.

With a graphical interface, create objects and link them together with steps. Resulting programs can be compiled to stand-alone. Add-on products allow networking, debugging, and pre-built external resources. Built-in relational database lets you assign indexes, and cross-reference items, indexes, and files.

No royalties for single-user distribution. MS-DOS v2 is cross-compatible with MAC v2; MAC v4 has all the bells and whistles, including full MAC interface.

Zocalo Engineering

Local and Wide-Area Network Design and Installation

Zocalo Engineering specializes in the construction of robust networks of interoperating Macintosh and UNIX computers, providing full-service design and installation of AppleTalk and IP local- and wide-area internetworks. Zocalo Engineering's expertise is concentrated in the following areas:

  • Network Planning. Zocalo's planning approach provides the network services you need now as well as the flexibility to grow into future needs, including electronic mail, file sharing, centralized backup, security, and remote access.

  • Cable Plant Design. Zocalo can provide a blueprint for the implementation of an AT&T PDS and EIA/TIA 568-compliant voice and data network in your workspace.

  • Installation. Zocalo's team of experienced cable plant installers can wire a new building to suit, retrofit an old one, or repair an existing cable plant quickly and reliably.

  • Cable Plant Certification. Zocalo can ensure that your installed wiring meets the EIA/TIA and ANSI standards applicable to the class of network you intend to use.

  • Internet Feeds. Zocalo also provides full-time high-speed Internet feeds in Northern California, giving customers access to worldwide information resources.

  • AppleTalk Tunneling Services. Zocalo's tunneling services allow customers to connect remote offices for a fraction of the cost of leased lines or packet-switched services.

  • Support and Training. Zocalo provides on-going support for your network and training for your users and in-house network administration staff.

Zocalo Engineering employs network architects of the highest repute, who publish regularly in periodicals like Network World, MacWEEK, MacWorld, and Connections, and sit on the standards-setting bodies of the Appletalk Networking Forum and the Apple Network Managers Association.
