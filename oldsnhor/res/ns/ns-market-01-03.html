The Internet Press Conference - An Emerging Form of Electronic Publishing

Zaballos & Associates Logo

At Zaballos & Associates, we're intrigued about an emerging form of electronic publishing - the Internet Press Conference.

Using the World Wide Web, FTP, IRC and other nifty Internet technologies, we imagine accompanying the media - virtually and interactively - for direct access to, and interaction with, the news event.

Join us at the UniForum '95 Conference in Dallas, Texas on Wednesday, March 15 at 10:30 a.m. where we'll explain and demonstrate an Internet Press Conference during the panel - "Integrating UNIX and the Mac: The Convergence of Open Systems and Electronic Publishing", co-chaired by Steven Beedle and Zee Zaballos, Principals of Zaballos & Associates and joined by Rob Kolstad, Ph.D., President, Berkeley Software Design, Inc., John Bryant, Vice President, JADE Multimedia, Inc, and Jason Levitt, Senior Technology Editor, Open Systems Today/Information Week.
