Oslonett Inc., Oslo, Norway

Oslonett Inc. is a Norwegian company providing Internet and Unix services.

Public access, dial-up Internet

Oslonett provide a public access UNIX system with full Internet connectivity. Online services include SMTP mail (with numerous gateway possibilities), Usenet News, Telnet, FTP, Gopher, WAIS and World Wide Web. We are running a local Gopher server (gopher.oslonett.no:70) as well as a local World Wide Web server (at www.oslonett.no:80) with a lot of information and pointers to Internet meta resources. For your convenience, there is a searchable WAIS index for easy lookup in the Web server.

For prices and general info on how you to use the Oslonett public access dial-up Internet, please send mail to our mailresponder: info@oslonett.no.

Oslonett Marketplace

Our WWW server contains miscellaneous «business links» as well as local material. Probably most interesting to outsiders, is our service called the Oslonett Marketplace. English versions are automatically made available for connections from outside Norway. For information on how you can advertise your product and services in the Oslonett Marketplace, please contact us at one of the addresses given at the bottom of this file, but first look here.

Commercial use of the Internet is still in its infancy in Norway, and Oslonett's mission is to promote the use of Internet services, and to show the industry how Internet access can be used as a competitive edge.

Consulting services

We also provide traditional consulting services to the industry, with focus on UNIX, Network Management, LAN technology and heterogeneous System Management.
The 18 shareholders are all professional computer scientists (M.SC and DR.SC) with an exceptional amount of experience within the above mentioned fields. The shareholders are mainly people from the following three institutions :

  • Department of Informatics, Univ. of Oslo
  • Norsk Regnesentral («Norwegian Computing Center»)
  • USIT («Center for Information Technology Services»)
