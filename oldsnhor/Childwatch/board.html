Childwatch International - Executive Board

The activities of Childwatch International are initiated and supervised by an Executive Board of 12 experts from within the field of child research and who are associated with key research institutions at global, regional and national levels:

  • Ferran Casas (Spain) President
  • Lisbet Palme (Sweden) Vice-president
  • Judith Ennew (UK)
  • Ivar Frønes (Norway)
  • James Garbarino (USA)
  • Noboru Kobayashi (Japan)
  • Gertrud Lenzer (USA)
  • Per Egil Mjaavatn (Norway)
  • Vitit Muntarbhorn (Thailand)
  • Philista Onyango (Kenya)
  • Helmut Wintersberger (Austria)
  • Eugenia Maria Zamora (Uruguay)
