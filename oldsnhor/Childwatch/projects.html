Childwatch International - Activities

Through a series of various activities, with a holistic and inter-disciplinary approach, Childwatch International works to achieve its objectives in close cooperation with national, regional and global research organisations as well as with intergovernmental organisations such as UNICEF, UNESCO, UNHCR and others.

Childwatch activities include:

  • disseminating information about current developments in child research;
  • establishing a database and facilitating that members of the network can communicate through e-mail and electronic conferences;
  • organising conferences or symposia where experts on selected themes are brought together to analyze existing knowledge for action, or defining new areas for urgent development;
  • initiating and participating in international research projects on children and undertaking consultancies and programmes;
  • assisting the development and strengthening of regional and national child research centres, groups and networks;
  • supporting the coordination of the regional and international development activities of existing associations and institutions involved in child research;
  • establishing and maintaining a resource pool to draw from when knowledge is needed and new research required, as well as assistance to strengthening infrastructure for child research;
  • seeking funds and sponsorship from donors and other organisations to support the above activities.
