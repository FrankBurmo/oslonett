Childwatch International - Objectives

Childwatch International will:

  • promote knowledge of, and improvement in, child research throughout the world;
  • promote an inter-disciplinary approach to child research and a holistic understanding of children;
  • provide channels for exchange of information and knowledge between researchers addressing issues relating to children's rights and well-being;
  • promote more effective dissemination of the results from child research to the general public;
  • facilitate the development and strengthening of capacity for research for children in countries that are not served by appropriate national or regional research institutions;
  • identify experts or institutions to undertake research or studies on specific child issues, and to facilitate and stimulate development of projects in areas of priority;
  • encourage and support a dialogue between researchers and decision-makers on the development of strategies for the implementation of the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child and other international agreements and instruments;
  • cooperate with intergovernmental-, non-governmental-, and national organizations to support their work for the benefit of children, initiate activities that can meet their needs for research, and stimulate further international cooperation.
