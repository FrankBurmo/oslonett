Childwatch International - Network

Childwatch International is a global research network consisting of institutions, associations and individuals involved in research on issues relating to children and children's development and well-being. Members of the network include:

  • Centre for the Study of the Child & Society, Glasgow
  • Centre International de l'Enfance, Paris
  • Centro de Estudios del Menor, Madrid
  • Child Development Centre of China, Beijing
  • European Centre, Vienna
  • Consortium on Children, Families & the Law , USA
  • The Consultative Group on Early Childhood
  • Department of Child Ecology, the National Children's Medical Research Center, Tokyo
  • Department of Educational Psychology, Kenyatta University, Nairobi
  • Family Life Development Center, Cornell University, Itacha
  • Fundación Paniamor, Costa Rica
  • Institut de l'Enfance et de la Famille, Paris
  • The Norwegian Centre for Child Research, Trondheim
  • Tata Institute of Social Sciences, Bombay
  • Universidade Santa Ursula, Center for Research on Childhood, Rio de Janeiro
