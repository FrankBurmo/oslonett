Directions to the San Francisco Venue: Stanford Stadium

PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION INFORMATION:

CALTRAIN:
Caltrain runs between San Francisco and San Jose. It will be making a special stop at the Stanford Stadium Station for the games. The station is located within walking distance of the stadium. For more information, please call 800-660-4287.

SAMTRANS:
SamTrans buses will be shuttling people from the Daly City BART Station, the San Francisco International Airport and Peninsula points to CALTRAIN stations. For more information, please call (800) 660-4287.

TRANSPORTATION AGENCY (TA):
TA will provide 15 minute bus service along the El Camino Real between San Jose and Palo Alto, with extended trips to Menlo Park every 30 minutes. Other TA routes connect with both (line 22) and CALTRAIN providing access to the stadium. For more information, please call 408-287-4210.

GOLDEN GATE TRANSIT:
Golden Gate Transit buses service the Transbay Terminal where passengers can connect with SamTrans (line 7F) departing every 30 minutes to the stadium. For more information, please call 415-332-6600.

BART:
A rail system linking San Francisco and the East Bay. On game days, fans may take BART to the Daly City Station and catch a SamTrans bus to the stadium. BART trains will leave downtown San Francisco for Daly City starting at 6:02 am and run every 7 minutes until 7 pm and then every 20 minutes until closing at midnight. For more information, please call 415-788-BART.

MUNI:
The San Francisco Municipal Railway serves all of San Francisco with buses, light rail and cable cars. For more information, please call 415-673-MUNI.
