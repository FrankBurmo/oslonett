Directions to the PARK-AND-RIDE for the Orlando Venue: Citrus Bowl Stadium

PARK AND RIDE SHUTTLE
LYNX, the regional transit system for the Orlando Urban Area, will provide shuttle service to the stadium from three easily accessible locations: Central Florida Fairgrounds on West Colonial Drive (SR 50) is the primary location; International Drive at Republic Drive serving the southwest and attractions area; and Ben White Raceway at the intersection of (US 441) and Lee Road (SR 435) for the northern suburbs and Winter Park hotels. Many area hotels will offer chartered bus service directly to the games or to one of these three Park-and-Ride lots.

The shuttle service will be provided to game ticket holders only, and the round-trip fare for the shuttle service will be $10 per person. For advance purchase of round- trip tickets, contact LYNX at (407) 841-8240. Buses will run continuously from three hours before kickoff to three hours after the game is over.

AROUND THE STADIUM
There will be no public parking available in the area adjacent to the stadium. Streets surrounding the stadium will be closed to general public vehicles and accesible only to those having special permits. Charter bus parking will be available. For information, contact the City of Orlando Parking Bureau at (407) 246-2154.
