  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Five
    • Record: 8-6-6
    • Best Result: Runner up in '74 and '78.

    The Netherlands was one of the forefathers of organized soccer. Its football association was formed in 1889. By 1900, there were many clubs playing soccer in the Netherlands.

    In 1957 the Dutch created their first national league and approved professionalism. The Netherlands' international schedule began to increase in the 1960s and by the 1970s it seemed that Dutch soccer was on the rise. However, the Dutch team failed to qualify for both the 1982 and the 1986 World Cup tournaments. They made their comeback when they qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup by beating Poland, 3-1, on November 17, 1993, in Poznan.
