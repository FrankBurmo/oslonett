  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Five
    • Record: 3-6-3
    • Best Result: 1990: Lost to Ireland in second-round.

    Romania formed The Federatia Romana de Fotbal in 1908, the first Balkan state to do so. It then formed the larger Federation of Romanian Sports Societies in 1910. Soccer's popularity diminished in Romania with the advent of World War I.

    Romania played its first international game in 1922. It was one of only four European teams to travel to the first World Cup in 1930. Romania has attended four World Cup finals since that time. It qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup when it beat Wales, 2-1, on November 17, 1993, at Cardiff.
