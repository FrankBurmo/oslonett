    • Previous Appearances: Six

    • Best Result: 1954: Lost to Austria in quarter-finals.

    Football has been played in Switzerland sincd 1869. Its first club was formed in 1879, and in 1895, the Swiss Football Association was formed. Switzerland was one of the founding members of FIFA, and gained recognition after the FIFA headquarters were established in Zurich in 1932. Fifty years later, the founders of UEFA decided to move its home to Berne, Switzerland.

    The first national team game was played in 1905 against France. Switzerland won a silver medal in the 1924 Olympics and made it to the quarter-finals of the 1934 and 1938 World Cup. It has attended six World Cup finals since the tournament began. Switzerland qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup by defeating Estonia 4-0 on November 17, 1993, in Zurich.

    Switzerland hosted the World Cup finals in 1954, staging the games in Basle, Berne, Geneva, Lausanne, Lugano, and Zurich.
