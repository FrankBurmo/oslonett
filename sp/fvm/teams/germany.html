    • Best Result: Tied for record with 3 titles:'54, '74, 90.

    Germany had been playing soccer for more than 20 years at the turn of the century, but it was not until 1908 that a proper national team was instituted. The first national game was played against Switzerland in Basel.

    Germay was removed from FIFA in 1946. The newly formed West German and East German Federations were eventually admitted. West Germany formally entered FIFA in 1950, and it is this association that continues to play under the name of Germany. The East German association was disbanded when reunification came in 1990.

    Germany has appeared in five of the last seven World Cup finals, a phenomenal record that no other country can match. Germany won its first World Cup title in 1954, its second in 1974 and is currently ranked second in the world. Germany's victory in the 1990 tournament in Italy automatically qualifies it to enter the 1994 FIFA World Cup.
