  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Two
    • Record: 1-4-2
    • Best Result: 1990: Lost to Cameroon in second-round.

    The city of Barranquilla was the first place soccer took hold in Colombia. It was in 1948 that the introduction of professionalism took place. The league competition experienced a complete reorganization in 1968, replacing the simple European-style league with a complicated structure. A new Federacion Colombiana de Futbol was formed in 1971.

    Colombia qualified for its first World Cup tournament in 1962 and didn't qualify again until the 1990 tournament in Italy. Qualification for the 1994 FIFA World Cup came when Colombia beat Argentina, 5-0, on September 5, 1993, in Buenos Aires.
