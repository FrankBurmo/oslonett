  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Fourteen
    • Record: 44-11-11
    • Best Result: Tied for record with 3 titles - 1958, 1962, 1970.

    Brazil will always retain a special place inthe hearts of soccer fans around the world. Brazil's popularity and success came from its supreme soccer skills. The golden age of Brazilian soccer came between 1950 and 1970. It was blesssed with so many players of outstanding talent that in the six World Cup tournaments of the era, Brazil reached the final on four occasions.

    Success in the South American Championships has been limited for Brazil's national team. Brazil entered the first World Cup in Montevideo, Uruguay in 1930. It was knocked out by Yugoslavia in the first round. Since that time, Brazil has entered and qualified for every World Cup tournament held. It has been the only country to do so. Qualification for the 1994 FIFA World Cup came on September 19, 1993, when they beat Uruguay, 2-0, in Rio de Janeiro.

    Brazil was the first country to win the World Cup title three times, a feat since matched by Italy and Germany. Following its third title, Brazil was awarded permanent custody of the World Cup trophy, and a new trophy was created for use in subsequent World Cup tournaments. Brazil is currently the top ranked soccer team in the world.
