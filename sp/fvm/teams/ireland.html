  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: One
    • Record: 0-1-4
    • Best Result: 1990: Lost to Italy in quarter-finals.

    Ireland's early years in soccer showed that the game was strongest in the north. It was not until 1986 when Jack Charlton became the manager that Ireland's national side began to enjoy success in soccer.

    Charlton modified the Irish team as its manager. He was able to change not only its tactics, but its attitude as well. Charlton inspired the team and as a result, Ireland qualified for the 1988 European Championship. Shortly thereafter, it qualified for its first World Cup final, traveling to Italy in 1990. Ireland qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup with a 1-1 draw to Northern Ireland on November 17, 1993, in Belfast.

    Ireland has finally arrived on the European soccer scene and continues to be a national team on the rise.
