    The sport of soccer was first introduced to Czarist Russia in 1879, when Englishmen taught the game to students in St. Petersburg. Now, more than a century later, Russia is once again an independent nation. Russia originally joined FIFA in 1912, but the USSR supplanted that membership in 1946. Following the collapse of the USSR, FIFA declared Russia to be the direct successor to the Soviet membership in the world body.

    Soviet soccer has achieved significant success through the years, attending the World Cup finals seven times. Following Russia's independence and its reinstatement as a FIFA member, the Russian Football Union moved quickly to get a national team on the field so that it could prepare for the World Cup qualification matches. In its first four qualifying matches, the Russians beat each opponent without surrendering a goal. It was assured a place in the World Cup finals even before its 0-1 loss to Greece on November 17, 1993, in Athens.
