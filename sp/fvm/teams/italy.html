  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Twelve
    • Record: 31-11-12
    • Best Result: Tied for record with 3 titles: '34. '38, '82.

    Italy's strong interest in soccer began in the late 1800s when many clubs were formed in the north. Italy's first national game was played against France in 1920. The "single league" system that is present today, started in 1930.

    The 1930s was an impressive decade for Italy. It won the 1934 and 1938 World Cup titles, as well as taking a gold medal at the 1936 Olympics. The tragic airplane crash that occurred in 1949 killed all the team members on board. Italy had a tough time recovering from the loss. Its post War performance was evidence of this. After playing in many World Cup finals, Italy pulled itself together to win its third title in 1982. Qualification for the 1994 FIFA World Cup came with a 1-0 win over Portugal on November 17, 1993, in Milan.

    Italy has hosted the World Cup twice since the tournament started, 1934 and 1990. Italy is currently ranked third in the world.
