Public Transportation

Due to its small geographic size, Boston is an excellent walking city. The Massachusetts Bay Transit Authority (MBTA), known as the ``T,'' provides excellent direct service to just about anywhere in Metropolitan Boston.

The four main lines, Blue, Green, Red and Orange, all run into the downtown area and out to different neighborhoods. For reference in this publication, there is a T map on the fold-out map of Boston.

One good tip on following the maps on the trains is the term ``inbound,'' which means the train is heading towards the Park Street T stop, the heart of the city. ``Outbound'' refers to trains heading out from the Park Street T stop.
Fares are $.85 locally and $.85-$2.00 for zone changes. The T starts running at 5:00am (except Sunday - 6:00am to 12:45am). The last trains leave downtown Boston at 12:45am.

The T's Visitor Passport gives you unlimited travel in Boston for one day ($5), 3 days ($9) or 7 days ($18), as well as discounts on popular attractions. T passes are available at the GBCVB Information Center on Boston Common and at the Prudential Center or call the T Customer Service Center at (617) 722-3200 for Visitor Passport sales locations or travel information.

For specific information on routes and schedules call the MBTA at (617) 722-3200.

MBTA Commuter Rail

Sometimes called the ``Purple Line,'' it extends from downtown Boston to as far as 60 miles away. One can find the commuter rail at four subway stops: North Station, off the Green line and Orange line; South Station and Porter Square both off the Red line; and Back Bay Station off the Orange line. For information call (617) 722-3200.

Bus Service

Greyhound Bus service is located at 720 Atlantic Avenue, South Station, Boston, (800) 231-2222. The ticket office is in the South Station Amtrak area.

Peter Pan Bus Lines is located at 555 Atlantic Avenue, diagonally across the street from South Station. (617) 426-7838.

Air Service

Logan International Airport, 1-800-23-LOGAN, is just two miles outside the city center. There are several convenient public transportation options to the airport from downtown Boston and suburban locations. Travelers can ride the MBTA Blue Line to Airport Station and connect to all terminals via free Logan shuttle buses. Taxis are readily available within the city and at the airport. There are approximately 40 airlines that serve Boston. Call your travel agent for details.

An Airport Water Shuttle ferries passengers between Rowes Wharf on Atlantic Avenue in downtown Boston and Logan. Logan provides free shuttle bus transfers to and from the Logan dock and all terminals. Weekday departures every fifteen minutes 6:00am to 8:00pm; Sunday departures every 30 minutes noon to 8:00pm. No service Saturdays, New Year's Day, July 4, Thanksgiving, or Christmas. Service on national holidays follows a Sunday schedule. One-way fare: adults $8.00; senior citizens $4.00; children under 12 free.

The Braintree Logan Express provides daily nonstop express bus service to and from Logan Airport and the South Shore. It's conveniently located opposite the South Shore Plaza on Forbes Road in Braintree. Fare: $5.00 one way. Children under 12 ride free.

The Framingham Logan Express provides daily nonstop express bus service to and from Logan Airport and Framingham. It is located at the Shopper's World Mall, off Route 30. Fare: $8.00 one way Mon-Fri, $5.00 one way Sat & Sun. Children under 12 ride free.

The Woburn Logan Express provides daily nonstop express bus service to and from Logan Airport and Woburn. It is located at Exit 36 Washington Street off Route 128 Mishawum Road. Fare: $5.00 one way. Children under 12 ride free.

Logan's 1-800-23-LOGAN ground transportation hot line provides round-the-clock schedule information on these and other transportation alternatives to and from Logan.

Taxi Service

Taxicab service to and from the airport and within the city is easy to obtain and a convenient way to travel. Taxicab stands are located throughout the city or hail a cab on the major streets. Taxicabs in Boston cost a minimum of $1.50 and $.20 for each 1/7 mile thereafter, plus all toll fees and an additional $1.00 for cabs originating at Logan International Airport.
