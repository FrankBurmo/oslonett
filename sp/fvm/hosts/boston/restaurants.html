Boston Dining

From lobsters to linguini, Greater Boston's dining experience is as varied and as colorful as the city itself. Once the home of baked beans, Boston's dining options are as extensive and enjoyable as any city in the world.

An enjoyable way to orient oneself with Boston's dining scene is through neighborhood offerings.

  • The North End, also known as "Little Italy," boasts Italian cuisine which rivals Italian home cooking.
  • The Back Bay offers everything from trendy American nouvelle cuisine to long established ethnic restaurants.
  • Chinatown, bordering the Midtown Cultural District, offers a wide variety of oriental cuisines.
  • The city's centuries-old fishing industry on the waterfront is unequalled in the diverse seafood selections it has to offer.
No matter what part of Boston or beyond, there is no shortage of culinary choices to suit all tastes in this world-class city. Bon appetit!
