San Jose Accommodations

The City of San Jose has approximately 4,800 guest rooms, and some 10,000 guest rooms throughout the region. A total of 1,449 guest rooms are within a three-block radius of the San Jose McEnery Convention Center. San Jose's full-service convention hotels include: The Fairmont, Hotel De Anza, Holiday Inn-Park Center Plaza, Hyatt San Jose, Le Baron Hotel, Radisson Plaza Hotel, Red Lion San Jose, San Jose Hilton & Towers, and the Hotel Sainte Claire.
