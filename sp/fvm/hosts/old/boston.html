Population: 575,000

Stadium Facts: Built in 1970, with a capacity of 61,000. Foxboro Stadium is located halfway between Boston, MA and Providence, RI, approximately 20 miles from downtown Boston.

June/July weather: average temperature is 70.5 degrees F/21 degrees C.

Attractions: Boston dates back to the American Revolution and is a fabled cradle of American liberty. Boston grew as a harbor town and is still one of the nation's finest ports. The city is now a center of banking and finance. Tourists can visit Boston Common, Faneuil Hall, the Swan Boats, and the U.S.S. Constitution. More than 20 colleges and universities are located in Boston, including Harvard University, the oldest college in the United States.
