Population: Bay Area: 6 Million

Stadium Facts: Built 70 years ago, the Stanford Stadium sits peaceful on the campus of Stanford University in Palo Alto, 35 miles south of San Francisco. Seating Capacity: 80,000+.

Weather: Despite California's scorching reputation, San Francisco is blessed with a temperate marine climate and enjoys mild weather year-round. Temperatures seldom rise above 70°F (21°C) or fall below 40°F (5°C).

Information: Surrounded on three sides by the Pacific Ocean and San Francisco Bay, San Francisco's compact 46 square miles (125 sq. km.) crowd the tip of the San Francisco Peninsula. ``The City'' has a population of 723,959; nevertheless it looms large in the imagination as the hub of the Greater Bay Area. The nation's fifth largest metropolitan region registers a population of 6 million and earns its sobriquet ``Everybody's Favorite City'' by hosting over 13 million visitors, conventioneers and business travelers each year.
