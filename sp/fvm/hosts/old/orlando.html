Few Tourist destinations in the world have the assets of Orlando, Florida. Disney World, Epcot, Universal Studios, MGM Studios, and Sea World, among other attractions, all have helped cement the Central Florida city's reputation as the place to go on vacation.

Orlando. It's a top choice of travelers of all ages from all over the world. And once you've been here, it's easy to understand why! The city comes by its magic naturally nature has blessed us with beautiful countryside and practically perfect vacation weather all year round.

Long an agricultural center, Orlando blossomed anew with the opening of Walt Disney World in 1971. The excitement has grown non-stop ever since. Vacationers thrill to the fantasy of The Magic Kingdom and the technological wonders of EPCOT '94. They're captivated by the mysteries of Earth's marine life at Sea World of Florida and the natural glories of Cypress Gardens. There's the big-screen magic and back-lot excitement of Universal Studios Florida and Disney-MGM, old-fashioned fun at Church Street Station, and a continuing out-of-this-world fascination with Kennedy's Spaceport USA.

And, for those who prefer to spend their leisure time indulging in less structured activities, Orlando is equally appealing. The area offers golf on nearly 100 excellent courses. Tennis, water skiing and croquet abound and, for a real change, check into hot-air ballooning, horseback riding and uniquely relaxing motor home tours.

Vacationers can always enjoy the quiet countryside and pristine lakes, in virgin hardwood forests or in primeval wetlands. Or you can spend an afternoon fishing or canoeing on miles of cool, clean rivers. All in all, it is an adventure you never outgrow!
