Orlando Areas of Interest

Natural Florida
Allow time to venture "off the beaten path" and enjoy the natural beauty of the area. Watch live gator wrestling, then take a breath-stopping bungee jump or hot air balloon ride. Then spend an afternoon horseback riding, fishing, or take a guided boat tour through a scenic chain of lakes. Try a canoe trip along the unspoiled Wekiva River or a pontoon boat ride or riverboat cruise along the historic St. John.

Orlando Science Center
"Hands-on" science center, planetarium shows. 407/896-7151

A World of Orchids
The world's largest permanent exhibit of flowering orchids. 407/396-1887

Sea World of Florida
Marine life park featuring live shows. Home of Shamu the killer whale. 800/327-2424

Cypress Gardens
Explore breathtaking gardens and the new butterfly conservatory. 800/237-4826.

Busch Garden, Tampa Bay
African theme zoological park. Exciting thrill rides, live entertainment, exotic shows, and Kumba, one of the world's largest and fastest roller coaster. 813/-987-5082.

Disney MGM Studios Theme Park Walt Disney World Resort
The most popular movie and television studios in the world where Disney magic bring your favorite movies to life. 407/824-4321

EPCOT Center Walt Disney World Resort
Disney's dream of two dazzling worlds, Future World and World Showcase. 407/824-4321

Magic Kingdom Walt Disney World Resort
Over 40 attractions and seven lands featuring Adventureland, Frontierland, Tomorrowland, Main Street U.S.A., Mickey's Starland and Fantasyland. 407/824-4321

Kennedy Space Center/Spaceport USA
Showplace for the United States space program/home of the space shuttle. 407/452-2121

U.S. Astronaut Hal of Fame
Memorabilia of Mercury and Gemini astronauts and their historic missions. Home of the U.S. Space Camp. 407/269-6100

Malibu Grand Prix
Scaled Indy and Formula One style racing. 407/351-4132

Universal Studios Florida
Over 40 rides, shows and attractions including back to the future ride. 407/363-8000
