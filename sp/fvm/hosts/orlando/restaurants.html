Dining

Return to Orlando At-A-Glance
From sushi to steak, from pasta to grits, Orlando's restaurants are as much a part of the magic as our attractions. The area is home to some of the world's most respected and talented chefs. And the dishes you'll discover here are among the most diverse in the country.

On the following pages, you'll find information on restaurants grouped by cuisine. You'll find offerings that range from Old World continental to wildly New Wave, in settings for breakfast, brunch, lunch, supper, dinner, tea and everything in between.

You can make the most of your Orlando visit by indulging your taste buds in some real Florida specialties & try Cuban one day and classical Southern cooking the next. And for a treat that comes complete with stories to tell the folks back home, try gator tail or gator 'nuggets real Florida-only fare. And don't forget Florida's world-famous citrus!

Orlando's superb weather offers another special dining opportunity. Outdoor dining in a number of settings from dockside at a yacht club to seating aboard a real paddle wheel steamboat on a dinner-time river cruise.

Along with old favorites, area restaurants are always testing new foods and recipes & just one more reason to come back again and again.
