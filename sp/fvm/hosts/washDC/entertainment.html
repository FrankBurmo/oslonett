Theater & Stage

American Theatre Project At 8Rock
1920 Martin Luther King Jr. Ave. SE, Washington, DC 20020
(202) 678-7625. (202) 678-7625 (tape)
Six theatrical productions (matinees available). Admission; senior citizen and student discounts available. Metro: Anacostia.

Arena Stage
Sixth Street and Maine Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20024
(202) 488-3300
Three-theater complex presents an entertaining mix of classics, comedies and musicals. Admission. Handicapped accessible seating and parking, audio narration, listening assistance devices and sign interpretation available with advance notice. Closed selected holidays. Metro: Waterfront.

Carter Barron Amphitheater
Rock Creek Park, 16th Street and Colorado Avenue NW
(202) 426-6832
A 4,000-seat outdoor theater.

Discovery Theater
Arts and Industries Building, 900 Jefferson Drive SW, Washington, DC 20560
(202) 357-1500 (voice and TTY for the hearing impaired)
Live dramas, musicals and puppet shows for children. Show times: Tuesday-Friday 10 and 11:30 a.m., Saturday 11:30 a.m. and 1 p.m. Admission. Metro: Smithsonian.

Ford's Theatre
511 10th St. NW, Washington, DC 20004
(202) 347-4833, (202) 347-5599 (TTY for the hearing impaired), (800) 899-2367
Box office open Monday-Friday 10 a.m.-curtain, Saturday-Sunday noon-show time. Signed performances, audio description performances and audio enhancement devices available. Metro: Metro Center, Archives-Navy Memorial.

George Washington University Lisner Auditorium
730 21st St. NW, Washington, DC 20052
(202) 994-6800
A recently renovated 1,500-seat facility offering popular performers of jazz, rock, international and classical music concerts, comedy, dance and opera.

John F. Kennedy Center For The Performing Arts
2700 F St. NW, Washington, DC 20566
(202) 467-4600. (800) 444-1324
Six magnificent theaters present drama, dance, music, comedy, film and commissioned works. Home of the National Symphony Orchestra, Washington Opera and the American Film Institute. Sign interpreted and audio-described programs and performances, TTY available. Metro: Foggy Bottom-GWU.

Lincoln Theatre
1215 U St. NW, Washington, DC 20009
(202) 727-6600
See African-American Points of Interest section.

National Theatre
1321 Pennsylvania Ave. NW, Washington, DC 20004
(202) 628-6161. (800) 233-3123
One of America's oldest continually operating theaters features national tours of Broadway favorites, pre-Broadway shows and American premieres. Admission. Hearing amplification available; narrated performances on occasion. Metro: Metro Center.

Shakespeare Theatre
450 Seventh St. NW, Washington, DC 20004. Box office:
(202) 393-2700, (202) 638-3863 (TTY for the hearing impaired)
New 447-seat theatre located in the heart of the Seventh Street arts district between the White House and U.S. Capitol. Admission. Fully accessible for patrons with disabilities. Metro: Archives-Navy Memorial.

"Shear Madness"
Kennedy Center
2700 F St. NW, Washington, DC 20566
(202) 416-8290, (202) 467-4600 (tape)
The comedy whodunit that lets the audience play armchair detective. Now in its sixth year at the Kennedy Center. Show times: Tuesday-Thursday 8 p.m., Friday 9 p.m., Saturday 6 and 9 p.m., Sunday 3:30 and 7 p.m. Admission. Signed performance available. Metro: Foggy Bottom-GWU.

Studio Theater
1333 P St. NW, Washington, DC 20005
(202) 588-5259
Helen Hayes Award-winning contemporary theatre featuring off-Broadway-style plays premiering in Washington. Admission. Hearing devices, wheelchair lifts available. Closed Monday-Tuesday. Metro: Dupont Circle.

See listing in section.

Warner Theatre
1299 Pennsylvania Ave. NW, Washington, DC 20004
(202) 628-1818. Box office: (202) 783-4000
This former movie palace has been beautifully restored with the same elegant neoclassical decor displayed on opening night in 1924. Presents such headliners as Frank Sinatra, Tommy Tune, Mikhail Baryshnikov, Prince, the best of Broadway and the Washington Ballet performance of "The Nutcracker." Open daily, except Christmas.

Wolf Trap Foundation for the Performing Arts
1624 Trap Road, Vienna, VA 22182
(703) 255-1868, (703) 255-1860 (tape)
America's only national park for the performing arts, this outdoor theatre features performances ranging from pop, symphony and musical to dance and comedy. Performance times vary. Admission to performance; no charge to visit park area. Parking available. Metro: West Falls Church.
