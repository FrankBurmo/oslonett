January

Martin Luther King Jr.'s Birthday Observance
Jan. 17.
Local choirs, guest speakers and military color guard salute King's memory. (202) 619-7222.

Chinese New Year Parade
Late January
H Street between Fifth and Eighth streets NW. The colorful celebration includes firecrackers, lions, drums and dragon dancers. (202) 724-4091.

April

National Cherry Blossom Festival
April 3-10
Washington's historic monuments are framed in springtime splendor when more than 6,000 Japanese cherry trees bloom in late March/early April. For parade tickets, call (202) 728-1137. For general information, call (202) 737-2599.

White House Easter Egg Roll
April 4
Children ages 8 and under, accompanied by an adult, gather on the south lawn for this traditional event. Eggs and entertainment provided. (202) 456-2200.
Duke Ellington Birthday Celebration, April 30, Freedom Plaza, 13th Street and Pennsylvania Avenue NW. Come celebrate this native Washingtonian's contribution to American music. (202) 331-9404.

May

Goodwill Embassy Tour
May 14
Various Washington embassies open their doors to the public to benefit Davis Memorial Goodwill industries. Admission; reservations required. (202) 636-4225.
Memorial Day Ceremonies, May 30, Arlington National Cemetery, the Tomb of the Unknowns. Services in Memorial Amphitheater feature military bands and a presidential keynote address. (202) 475-0856. Also ceremonies at Vietnam Veterans Memorial and U.S. Navy Memorial. (202) 619-7222 and (202) 737-2300.

July

Festival of American Folklife
July 1-4, 7-10
National Mall
More than one million visitors attend this annual festival of American music, crafts and ethnic foods. Sponsored by the Smithsonian Institution's Office of Folklife Programs. (202) 357-2700.

DC Free Jazz Festival
July 2-4
Freedom Plaza, 1300 Pennsylvania Ave. NW
Top national and international jazz performers in free concerts. (202) 783-0360.

National Independence Day Celebration
July 4
Events begin at 10 a.m. with the annual National Archives Program. At noon, the National Independence Day Parade follows a route down Constitution Avenue past many of the historic monuments on the Mall. At 4 p.m., a variety of free entertainment is available at the Sylvan Theatre on the Washington Monument grounds. At 8 p.m., the National Symphony Orchestra performs on the west steps of the U.S. Capitol. At 9:15 p.m., a spectacular fireworks display over the Washington Monument ends the day in "capital" style! (202) 619-7222.
