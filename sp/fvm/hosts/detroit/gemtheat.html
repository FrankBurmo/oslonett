THE GEM THEATRE

Background:
The Little Theater was founded in 1928 by women of the Twentieth Century Club who wanted to offer an "uplifting influence on their community." Now known as The Gem Theatre, this 450-seat house boasts a rich history of sophisticated film showings, plays and musical theater. Over the decades, The Gem Theatre went through several changes until the re-opening in 1992 after an 18-month, multi-million dollar restoration. It is the newest addition to the rapidly developing Theater District and the pride of Detroit show-goers. With its gold filigree ceiling and original wall panels of intricate, colored patterns,
The Gem sparkles with life.
Intimate cabaret seating, a vividly colored interior and the opportunity to sip cocktails and munch on hors d' oeuvres during the show, all add to The Gem's elegance. The main level features cabaret seating, while the lighting fixtures and theater seats, 1920s antiquities, were salvaged from the Ambassador Theatre, a movie palace in St. Louis.

What's Special:
The Gem offers unique broadway-style shows, comedy acts and other productions.

Details:
