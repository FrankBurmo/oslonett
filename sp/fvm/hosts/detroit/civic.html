LEADERS IN BUSINESS AND DEVELOPMENT


Free enterprise has played a dominant role in Detroit from the earliest days of the French fur trader to the thriving manufacturing center of Detroit.
There are many civic and business organizations in Detroit that have interest and influence on city affairs and government.
