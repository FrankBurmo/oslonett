THE DETROIT INSTITUTE OF ARTS

Background:
Ranked fourth largest among the nation's fine art museums, The Detroit Institute of Arts (DIA) opened in 1885. With more than one billion in art and facilities, it stands as the largest municipally-owned museum in the nation. The museum attracts more than half a million visitors annually to view some of the finest works by Rembrandt, Picasso and Matisse. Overall, the museum houses approximately 55,000 creative works throughout the museum's 11.5 acres and 100 galleries. Among its renowned works are French Impressionist, German Expressionist, American and Flemish paintings, Italian, African, Asian, Native American, Egyptian and 20th-century art.
What's Special:
Some highlights of the Institute's collections include: the four-wall mural, "Detroit Industry" by Diego Rivera; Van Gogh's "Self Portrait"; Joos Van Cleve's "Adoration of the Magi"; John James Audubon's "The Watercolor Paintings for the Birds of America"; and the nation's largest collection of historic puppets.

Extra Special:
Works by famous African-American artists such as Romare Bearden and Detroit artist Charles McGee are on permanent display. The museum also has part of its extensive African art collection on display. Additionally, the DIA annually hosts "Bal Africain," a black-tie fundraiser for the museum's Friends of African and African American Art, the primary support for both collections.
A museum gift shop and two restaurants, the Kresge Court Cafe and the American Grille, enhance the visitor's experience.
In 1993, the DIA unveiled its new Information Center, where visitors can view an 11-minute orientation film hosted by the museum director. Outside the theater are enlarged floor plans and information showcasing the permanent collection and the location of various works.
Activities:
The DIA features many special events and shows including the Detroit Film Theatre's weekly series of avant garde and foreign productions.

Details:
The Detroit Institute of Arts is located in the Cultural Center at 5200 Woodward Ave., Detroit, MI 48202, (313) 833-7900. DIA hours are Wed.-Sun. 11 a.m.-4 p.m., Sat. and Sun. 11 a.m.-5 p.m. Admission is voluntary.
