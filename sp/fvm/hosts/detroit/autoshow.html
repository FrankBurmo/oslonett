NORTH AMERICAN INTERNATIONAL AUTO SHOW

Background:

Well known as the "Motor City," Detroit is also home to one of the world's largest auto shows -- the North American International Auto Show. Now entering its 81st year, the show is sponsored by the area's Auto Dealers Association and has the distinction of being the only North American Auto Show sanctioned by the prestigious Organisation Internationale Des Constructeurs D'Automobiles in Paris, France.
What's Special:

Auto manufacturers and coach builders annually display approximately 750 cars and trucks. Showgoers get first looks at new models, production cars, prototypes and concept cars. In 1994, 33 models made their worldwide debut at the show.
The show attracts more than 600,000 visitors and 3,500 media from around the world. Held in Cobo Conference/Exhibition Center, the Auto Show occupies an area equal to 11 football fields and continues to set new space and attendance records.
With domestic and foreign exhibitors, the auto show has a true international flair. Nameplates range from Detroit's own Big Three to BMW and Toyota. Media, automobile buffs and car buyers from around the world have made the North American International Auto Show a celebrated event in the city.
Extra Special:
The Detroit Auto Dealers Association sponsors a black-tie Charity Preview, held the night before the public opening of the show. A record 11,236 people attended the extravaganza in 1994, raising $1.4 million for area charities.
