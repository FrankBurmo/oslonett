SECOND CITY

Background:
In September 1993, the Second City -- Detroit Comedy Theatre opened in the Theatre District next to the Fox Theatre. Seven actors carry on Second City's rich comedy tradition incorporating local humor and timely political and social issues into satirical sketches.
The Second City, which has its roots in improvisation and ensemble acting, has been at the vanguard of intelligent humor for more than 25 years. Formed by students at the University of Chicago, the theatre has been home to some of the brightest talents in comedy.
Unlike stand-up comedy, The Second City concept is translated by a group of actors who enliven an empty stage with topical comedy sketches. Using few props and costumes, punctuating scenes with original music, the ensemble creates a slice-of-life environment, lampooning modern life -- political, social and cultural.
Detroit is the third metropolitan Detroit area to be home to the cutting edge of comedy theater. There are three theaters in the Chicago area and one in Toronto. In addition, there are four touring companies that perform throughout North America.
The Second City has nurtured the talents of some of the most gifted entertainers in North America. They include: Alan Alda, Ed Asner, Dan Ackroyd, John Belushi, John Candy, Chris Farley, Valerie Harper, Tim Meadows, Catherine O'Hara, Gilda Radner, Joan Rivers, Martin Short, Jerry Stiller, Ben Stiller, and George Wendt.

What's Special:
Second City houses a 350-seat theatre; a 350-seat restaurant named Risata; a 50-seat bar on the street level; a dual-level rooftop terrace to accommodate 200 people, and a store featuring Second City merchandise on the lower level.
