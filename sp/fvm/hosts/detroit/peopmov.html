DETROIT PEOPLE MOVER

Background:
Since its first run in August 1987, the Detroit People Mover is now a major source of transportation for people located in the Central Business District. Covering 2.9 miles in 15 minutes, the People Mover is an elevated computerized rail transit system stopping at 13 stations throughout downtown Detroit. It links the city's major office buildings, hotels, restaurants, Cobo Conference/Exhibition Center and shopping districts.

What's Special:
More than two million dollars worth of artwork debuted in August 1987 when the Detroit People Mover opened, with each piece reflecting the character of the neighborhood where the station is located. Regional, national and international artists were invited to create proposals for each station, with tile, mosiac and other durable materials given preference. Four of the pieces were handcrafted at Pewabic Pottery, Detroit's historic ceramics studio. People Mover riders can view the majority of the artwork from the trains.

Details:
The People Mover can be accessed from any one of the 13 stations, including Cobo Conference/Exhibition Center, Joe Louis Arena, Renaissance Center and Trappers Alley. The system is operated by the Detroit Transportation Corporation, 150 Michigan Avenue, Detroit, MI 48226, (313) 962-RAIL or 313-224-2160.
Cost per ride is 50 cents. Exact change or a purchased token is required. System hours are Mon.-Thurs., 7 a.m.-11 p.m.; Fri., 7 a.m.-Midnight; Sat., 9 a.m.-Midnight and Sunday, noon-8 p.m.
