MONTREUX DETROIT JAZZ FESTIVAL

Background:
The largest free jazz festival in North America, the 15th annual edition of this Detroit classic takes place September 1-5, 1994. The event showcases local, regional and international jazz artists at more than 100 free, open-air concerts. The 1994 festival will feature a world-class selection of jazz masters. A record 800,000 jazz fans turned out for the 1992 Festival.

What's Special:
The Festival is held annually in Hart Plaza, a multi-use facility located on the riverfront in downtown Detroit. The sister festival to the prestigious Montreux Jazz Festival in Switzerland, the event is designed to promote Detroit as a vital and prosperous urban center and preserve the city's rich jazz community.
The finest Michigan high school and college jazz bands and ensembles also perform. Scholarships are awarded to the top students by the International Association of Jazz Educators.
The Festival is also heard internationally via a National Public Radio (NPR) broadcast hosted by WDET and WEMU, two area NPR stations.

Details:
