  • Will there be Videos of the game results?

    Unfortunately, there will be no videos of the game results on this server. We did not get approval from the parties to be to distribute the videos via this server. We are making arrangements to have still images of the key plays of the game in the Results section.

  • What kind of system is providing this information?

    The server providing the World Cup 1994 Mosaic Pages was provided by Sun Microsystems, Inc. The system is a SPARCserver 1000 running Solaris 2.3.

  • How long did it take to build the information that is available on this server?

    A team of people from Sun Microsystems started constructing the pages the first part of May 1994. After receiving approval from World Cup USA and FIFA, EDS, and Sprint, members of Sun's World Cup Mosaic Team; Darrell Sano, Carl Meske, Will Shelton, Hassan Schroeder, Jim Remmell, Shane Sigler, and Regina Schumann, assisted by Judy Lindberg, Nanoshka Johnson, Phil Parkman, Dotty Carey, Jim Oliver, Anne Alexander, Billie Markim, and Andrew Neuschatz of Infobahn/San Francisco, began assimilating the necessary information to construct the World Cup Mosaic Pages.

    Darrell Sano, along with the Mosaic Team, first defined the overall structure and framework based on the goals and communication objectives of this project. Darrell designed the look and feel of the WorldCup pages and created the images you see throughout the server, worked from information given by the various Host City World Cup representatives. Carl Meske took the masses of data and assimilated them into the HTML files that are viewed via Mosaic. With the help of Regina, Jim, Judy, Andrew, Nanoshka, Billie, Dotty, Anne, Jim and Will, the content and information was put together and made available by June 6, 1994.

    During the games, Hassan Schroeder will be making the Game Results changes via automated crontab scripts and from data received from the various venues over the WCNS network. These changes will be propagated to the various servers throughout the world, including Tokyo, Norway, UK, and Germany, the location of mirror servers of the World Cup Mosaic pages.
