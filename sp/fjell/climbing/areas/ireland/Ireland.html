Dalkey

Location:
About 10km south of Dublin city centre, served well by bus (No. 8) and local rail (DART).

Kind of Rock:
Granite.

Height:
Mostly 20-30m, a few of 50-60m and 10-15m.

Season:
April to October - Best mid-summer.

Bolted:
Definitely not! All natural protection.

Camping sites:
None in Dublin at all - best to stay in hostels in/near town. Ask at tourist office. Price guide: IRstlg5-8 a night single.

Topo:
Excellent guide book available in Dublin climbing shops, The Great Outdoors or Bramac; price IRstlg9.

Grades:
Many routes of all grades, particularly VS-E3 (french 4-6c).

Interesting Routes:
All those with *** in the guide book.

History:
The quarry is the remains of a hill used to build the main ferry port in Dublin during the early 1900's. Climbing only really took off during the 70's and 80's.

Recreation: The climbers always frequent McDonaghs pub in Dalkey village (10 mins walk form crag) afterwards. Typical sociable Irish pub.

Other comments: Some of the harder routes tend to be poorly protected; ask local climbers for advice. The lower grades (up to E2) tend to be well protected. Many of the climbs are like extended boulder problems.
