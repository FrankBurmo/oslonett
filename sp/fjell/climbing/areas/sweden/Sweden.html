Rock Climbing Areas In Sweden

A very nice describtion with a lot of climbing areas can be found in this article here, written by Per Calleberg (per.calleberg@sto.pharmacia.se).

Haggsta

Location:
South of Stockholm. 20 min with the subway to Masmo, and then 20 min walk, plan in the topo. Or by car.
Kind of Rock:
Granite.
Height:
Up to 35 m.
Season:
Free climbing about apr-sep if warm weather. Aid all seas on and possibly ice in jan.
Bolted:
Some routes are, mainly over UIAA VI, but generaly NOT.
Camping sites:
A field for free in front of the crag.
Topo:
Stockholmsforaren, ca 200 SEK; sold in town, i.e. at Tierra, Flemminggatan.
Grades:
All. Up to IX.
Interesting Routes:
Yes.
History:
Long, started in the 30:s.
Other comments:
Close to a cemetary.
For more info you can ask Mikael Hedin (micce@grub01.physto.se)

Kullaberg

Location:
In the southwest of Sweden 40km northwest of Helsingborg on a small peninsula. The easiest way to get to Kullaberg is by car, but it is also possible to catch the bus from Helsingborg to Molle and walk the last 2 km. Most climbs in the area can be reached by a few minutes walk from the car.
Kind of Rock:
Granite, a bit eroded in some places but mostly good quality.
Height:
10-40 m.
Season:
The middle of April to the end of September. The best months are June, July and August. Can get quite cold in the other months, especially if it's blowing from the Sea.
Bolted:
Some of the harder climbs are bolted, but mostly you have to place your own protection. Mostly its easy to place good protection.
Camping sites:
A good camping site just outside the village of Molle a few km from Kullaberg. Camping is NOT allowed on Kullaberg itself. There are also some hotels in the little village of Molle, as well as a few Restaurants and Shops.
Topo:
"Klattring pa Kullen, nord- og Sydkyst" published by "Dansk Bjergklub" in Denmark. Can be bought in all climbing shops in the area, for example "Naturkompaniet" or "Outside" in Malmo.
Grades:
From UIAA II to VIII. Most of the routes are in the V to VI range.
Interesting Routes:
A absolute must is Dr Strangelove (VII-), Kullamannens dorr and Freak Brothers (VIII-), Gubbamalen.
Other comments:
One of the few really good climbing areas in the southwest of Sweden. Kullaberg is a small rocky peninsula rising steeply out of Oresund and is one of the few rocky areas in an otherwise flat landscape. The area is a wildlife reserve and it's well worth a visit even if you don't intend to climb. Kullaberg is also the local climbing area for many Danes, Denmark is just a few kilometers away by boat. All in all there are some 300 routes in the area, well worth a visit if you are in the neighbourhood. For more information contact:

Valberget

Location:
In the south east of Sweden in Blekinge, the area lies about 20 km north east from the town of Karlshamn just outside a small village called Granefors. The way from Granefors to Valberget is a bit hard to explain, the easiest way to get there is to ask someone in Granefors for directions, they are often quite helpfull. Another alternative is to ask the tourist information center in Karlshamn for a map and directions. It's quite hard to get to the area without a car, the easiest way is to take the bus from Karlshamn to Granefors and walk the last few km.
Kind of Rock:
Granite of a very good quality. The climbing is a mix of face climbing and crack climbing on vertical to slightly overhanging rock. There are also a quite impressive roof, and a few slab climbs.
Height:
5-45 m.
Season:
The middle of April to the end of September. Some of the routes are climbable even when it rains because of the overhanging rock.
Bolted:
All the existing routes are very well protected with good and solid bolts and many have anchors at the top for easy lowering off. Bear in mind the area has a big potential for quite a few new, very hard, climbs so there are many possible lines not protected by bolts.
Camping sites:
The best alternative is to camp on top of the "hill" where the routes are. Its also possible to camp under the big roof if its raining. The nearest camping ground is in Karlshamn.
Topo:
No topo at the moment, perhaps in the beginning of '95. Get in touch with the local climbing club for information. Grades:
UIAA IV to VIII. Most of the routes are in the VI to VII range.
Interesting Routes:
Try the big roof, quite a challenge, if you got the strenght (VIII-).
History:
Climbing activity in this part of Sweden has been quite low until just recently ('91-). This rock was discovered by climbers who recognised its potential just a few years ago. Most of the harder routes has been set up in '92-'94.
Other comments:
This rock offers about 40 bolted routes with potential for many more. There are many other small areas in the vicinity of Karlshamn with great potential for new and very hard routes.
