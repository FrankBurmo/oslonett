Final result

Hi you all, who showed interest in the WILD COUNTRY Flexible Friends Case:
-------------------------
This is the final report:
-------------------------

The thing seems to be much less dramatic than in the first case:

What has happened:

An american dealer returned a Wild Country Flex Friend: one of the steel 
tubes, where the cable is fixed on both ends (on one is the sling and 
eye, on the other the friend levers), could be pulled off by hand. The 
defect piece probably slipped through the quality control cycle, not 100% 
finally manufactured. A letter to all american dealers (to be found at 
http://www.wu-wien.ac.at/usr/h86/h8653026/flexfriend.html, and as 
facsimile GIF at http://www.wu-wien.ac.at/usr/h86/h8653026/FLEXIBLE.GIF) 
called back all friends which match two criteria:

a) a specified batch number:
Z15 to Z52
A01 to A52
301 to 352
401 to 452
501 to 522

All Flex Friends with two-digit-batch numbers or no batch numbers at all 
are correct.

AND

b) All Flexible Friends of these series have hexagonal swages at both 
ends. If  one of the swages is round, it is a defect and seriously 
hazardous tool.

Caution: Earlier Friends (there are three generations, all in whole) also 
have round swages. So please check the serial number and the swages.

All in all, it seemed to be a single incident. From the call-back-letter
(which was released mid August) until now, no damaged friend was returned.
Also, a large share of these batches was sold in the USA.  Anyway, noone
can tell where they ended up. 

These friends are of pretty recent production. So if your Friend is 
bought longer than 3 years ago, it is not affected (and will have correct 
round swages). Also, if you find a round-swaged Friend in a shop, it need 
not be wrong, it may be an old one. Check the serial number.

You can be perfectly sure to have a defect Friend, if it has ONE 
hexagonal and ONE round swage.

All damaged friends are returned for free. Others NOT.


You can find more detailed information on the Web Page

http://www.wu-wien.ac.at/usr/h86/h8653026/flexfriend.html (this is the 
letter, which was distributed).

If you speak German: There is a complete article at 

http://www.wu-wien.ac.at/usr/h86/h8653026/flexfriend-d.html

This mail will also be posted into rec.climbing and de.rec.climbing.

I hope all this can ease the panic. Anyway, have a short look on your 
climbing belt before you start again next time,
