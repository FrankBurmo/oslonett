Tivoli Rock

Address:
Strangenhaeuschen 21
52070 Aachen
--49 241 15 77 47

How to get there:
by car: Leaving the High way at Exit Wuerselen.
a) Coming from Netherlands: cross the lights, go straight ahead, you will see the gym after 100 meters to your left side.
b) Coming from Cologne, Liege or Duesseldorf: same exit, turn right. At the first crossing turn left. 100 meters later you will see the gym to your left side.
by public transport: ??
Height/climbing area:
The wall has a climbing area of about 600 square meters. The biggest part of it is made of the Entre Prise structured system. The rest is made out of the usual stuff used for climbing walls. The maximum height is 16 meters that includes several roofs (6 meters).
Hours of opening
Monday to Friday from 8 am until midnight. Saturday and Sunday from 8 am until 22.30 pm
Other comments:
The Introduction into belaying and making knots is obligatory for beginners.
