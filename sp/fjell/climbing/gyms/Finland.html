Olympia stadion (Olympic Stadium)

Address:
Helsinki
How to get there:
by car:
by public transport:
Height/climbing area:
Width 4.5m
11m vertical then 13m very overhanging (below a staircase)
Total height 17m
3 routes (not for beginners),
concrete.
Hours of opening
Every day 9-20.
Closed in the event of track&field competitions or other special events.
Other comments:
To get in you need a key from a caretaker.
Free of charge.
No heating.
