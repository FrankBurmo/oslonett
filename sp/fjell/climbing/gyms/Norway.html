Climbing Gyms In Norway

Haakons Hall

Lillehammer

How to get there:
by car: Approx. 190 km. (2 h drive) north from Oslo on route E6.
by public transport:Train from Oslo.
Height/climbing area:
20 meter high, 9 meter wide, 7 routes, indoor wall.
Hours of opening:
0900-2100 (1000:2100).
Other comments:
Dynamic wall (Entreprise) with structures imitating natural rock, plus the ordinary grips. The angle of the wall can be varied between (+10)-(-60) degrees. The wall is composed of two vertical main sections, and each section falls in three subelements. The angle of all the elements can be determined indepentdently, making the wall a very flexibel instrument.

Questions concerning the wall can be e-mailed to ta@ostforsk.no.
