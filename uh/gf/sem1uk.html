A Summernight's Dream

International Theatre Festival, Porsgrunn, June 4-11, 1995

Zygmund Molik : Voice and Body

Zygmund Molik has for more than 25 years been one of the leading actors of Jerzy Grotowski at his famous Teatr Laboratorium in Wroclaw, Poland. The plays in which he has performed include «The Constant Prince», «Akropolis» and «Apocalypsus cum Figuris». Performances that toured all over the world. The influence of Grotowski on contemporary European theater can hardly be overestimated.

Molik's main focus is the voice, how to liberate the voice, the concert between voice and body, and uncovering the immense possibilities of the human voice. This is the first time Zigmund Molik conducts a seminar in Norway.
