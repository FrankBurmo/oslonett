A Summernight's Dream

International Theatre Festival, Porsgrunn, June 4-11, 1995

Anna Zubrzycka og Gregorz Bral : The Living Actor

Anna Zubrzycka and Gregorz Bral are both rooted in the esteemed Teatr Gardzienice in Lublin, Poland. Many regard Teatr Gardzienice as one of the most exciting and innovative theaters to come out of Poland in the last decade. The company has cooperated with a number of well known theaters, including The Royal Shakespeare Company. This seminar aims to develop the physical and vocal potential of each participant. Through physical training, rythmic chanting and group improvisation, habits and conventional patterns are challenged.
