A Summernight's Dream

International Theatre Festival, Porsgrunn, June 4-11, 1995

Maria Cassi og Leonardo Brizzi : The Comedian's Song Workshop

Maria Cassi was trained as a classical singer. Recently she has had great success in Italy with her comical theatrical concertos accompanied by pianist Leonardo Brizzi. Their highly unusual and entertaining style has secured them fixed spots in Italian radio and television. The pair is very popular on the European festival scene. Their seminar is based on mimical and musical techniques of improvisation. The comical element is not necessarily the choice of material, but the way the material is treated and performed.

We can assure that the singing at «The Comedian's Song Workshop» is not replicated anywhere else.
