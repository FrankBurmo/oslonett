PRESS RELEASE FROM SCHIBSTED

The film laboratories in Sweden, Norway and Denmark are preparing for international competition

The development of digital techniques and integration of film and video will require substantial investments in the film processing industry. None of the laboratories in Scandinavia have attained sufficient profitability to justify the necessary investments, due to a small market size and stronger international competition.

The shareholders of the Scandinavian laboratories, Sveriges Television AB in Stockholm, Nordisk Film AS in Copenhagen and Schibsted Film AS in Oslo, have therefore decided to adjust the capacity to a level that gives sufficient long-term profitability.The agreement entered into by the three companies creates the basis for maintaining technically advanced laboratories in the three countries.

The agreement entails that the three companies jointly will own a holding company, Skandinaviske Filmlaboratorier Holding AB, with equal shareholdings of 1/3 each. The holding company will own the laboratories FilmTeknik AB in Solna (Sweden), SWELAB Filmlaboratoriet AB in Stockholm, Johan Ankerstjerne AS and EuroLab AS in Copenhagen. At the same time Nordisk Film will become a shareholder in FilmTeknikk AS in Oslo, at the same level as Schibsted Film. Norsk Film will continue as a 9% shareholder.

Plans for coordination and rationalization of the activities will be elaborated immediately.

The purpose of the agreement is to secure modern, competitive and profitable film laboratories in the three Scandinavian countries.

Oslo, 14 December 1995
Schibsted A/S
