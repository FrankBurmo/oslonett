3d Quarter 1995

The positive development in the Norwegian economy continued also in the 3. quarter of 1995, but at a lower growth rate than previously. Consumer demand is still growing strongly, whereas the growth in housing starts has stopped. The 1996 National Budget anticipates lower growth in 1996 compared to 1995, but at the same time emphasizes that the continued strong performance of the economy can lead to price- and wage pressures.

The demand for advertisement space in Aftenposten and VG grew by 16.4 % and 8.7 % respectively on a year-to-date basis. Advertisement revenues showed growth of 11.2 % and 12.5 % respectively.

The market for casual sales newspapers is becoming increasingly tough, with strong competition from new media, particularly television. VG's circulation, which showed signs of stagnation during the first 6 months of the year, continued to slip in the 3. quarter compared to the corresponding quarter in 1994. The year-to-date circulation of approximately 370,100 copies compares with 380,400 last year. Compared to 1993 the circulation level is stable. Sunday circulation continues to grow.

Aftenposten's circulation has developed in a satisfactory manner, with a year-to-date circulation of 279,900 copies compared to 278,100 copies last year.

The high level of activity within the group's companies as well as group project teams continued in the 3. quarter. The effort to position the group within the rapidly growing market for electronic publishing services led to the acquisition of Oslonett AS on September 1. Oslonett is the leading supplier of Internet-based services to the consumer market, with a 50 % market share. The newly acquired company will be provided resources to expand its know-how and capacity, and a new on-line service is planned for release at the end of the year. Through this acquisition and ensuing activities, Schibsted has positioned itself as one of the leading actors within this market in Norway. Attractive partners are being sought both in Norway and Scandinavia so as to set up a strong content-based group. Both Aftenposten and VG have developed their own Internet based applications, and these have been well-received in the market. The quarterly report is also available on Internet (http://www.oslonett.no/schibsted/).

In October an agreement was reached between Aftenposten and Kinnevik to establish a jointly held company Ð IMEDIA Norge AS Ð that will sell information surveillance services. The company will compete with the Bonnier-held company Norsk Argus, but will aim at exploiting existing and new technologies to provide customers with a superior product.

On September 19. TV 2 was given the economic and operational responsibility for TV Pluss AS. With this agreement, TV 2 was able to supply local TV channels with an alternative to the illegal re-broadcasting of TV Norge, which had to be terminated on October 1. TV 2's local-TV set-up - called Norgeskanalen - is based on TV+ as the network channel, and an agreement entered into with a number of local TV channels (currently 14). This network solution has a good chance of being awarded the national local-TV network. TV Pluss is included as a wholly owned subsidiary until September 19, thereafter the results are included in TV 2's accounts. The 3. quarter accounts do not include the effect of a potential later share sale.

In October the newspaper group A-Pressen purchased 22 % of the shares of TV 2 from Vital. Schibsted is of the view that a stable ownership structure with a Norwegian majority strengthens the channel's position.

Aftenposten is a 30 % owner in a new company that has applied for the local-TV concession to be awarded for the Oslo area from 1996.

The local transit authority - Oslo Sporveier AS - is expected to disclose the conditions which it will set for awarding the right to distribute a free daily newspaper within the public transport system. The newspaper publishing concept is expected to resemble closely that of Metro Ð the free daily newspaper recently launched in Stockholm. Schibsted and 5 other parties have expressed an interest in bidding for the distribution rights. The launch date is set to early 1996.

The group's organization will be changed according to changes taking place in our primary markets, as well as a consequence of choice of strategic direction. The organization model presented in this report is altered compared to previously, and reflects among other things a greater degree of interaction between group companies that traditionally have operated in distinct markets.

Financial highlights

The group's revenues at the end of the 3. quarter were NOK 2,471.6 (2,226.5) million, an increase of 11.0 %. The operating profit was 315.6 (292.5) million, an increase of 7.9 % compared to last year. The Schibsted Film companies contributed with an operating profit of NOK 5.2 (-20.3) million, while TV Pluss is included with an operating loss of NOK 53.1 million.

Net financial items were NOK 64.9 (22.9) million. Associated companies contributed positively to financial items with NOK 41.4 million. The group's net profit increased from NOK 231.9 million to NOK 273.2 million, an increase of 17.8 %.

The profit for the 3. quarter itself was somewhat lower than last year. This is due primarily to the negative contribution from TV Pluss, as well as higher newsprint costs of approximately NOK 18 million in the 3. Quarter.

At September 30. the EPS is calculated at NOK 3.95 (3.35), whereas the CFPS is calculated at NOK 5.67 (5.46). The group's equity at the end of the quarter increased to NOK 1,615.4 million from NOK 1,320.5 million last year, giving an equity ratio of 64.6 %. The group's interest bearing debt is only NOK 12.4 million.

Investments

Investments to date total approximately NOK 216.million. In addition to the acquisitions during the period, investments in building refurbishments have been undertaken at Aftenposten, and both newspapers have invested in new production technology.

Ownership structure

The ownership structure has remained stable through the year, with only minor changes in the shareholder structure. The share price at the start of the 3. quarter was NOK 79, while it traded at NOK 73 at the end of the quarter. The Oslo Stock Exchange All Share Index increased with 5.3 % during the quarter.

Future prospects

Growth expectations are positive, but moderate. Continued growth in total advertisement expenditure is expected also for the 4. quarter. The uncertainty with respect to the newspapers' profits is tied to the development of VG's circulation in the 4. quarter. The decision by Oslo Sporveier regarding publishing of a new free daily paper is also anticipated with considerable expectation.

The TV and film activities will in the 4. quarter be impacted by the authorities' decision regarding the local TV network, as well as the concession for the Oslo area. The further development of Oslonett and its growth within the electronic publishing sector will be the most important activity within the multi-media area.

Oslo, November 2, 1995
