leserbrev:  An important feature of SMTP is its capability to relay mail across <br> transport service environments. A transport service provides an <br> interprocess communication environment (IPCE). An IPCE may cover one <br> network, several networks, or a subset of a network. It is important <br> to realize that transport systems (or IPCEs) are not one-to-one with <br> networks. A process can communicate directly with another process <br> through any mutually known IPCE. Mail is an application or use of <br> interprocess communication. Mail can be communicated between <br> processes in different IPCEs by relaying through a process connected <br> to two (or more) IPCEs. More specifically, mail can be relayed <br> between hosts on different transport systems by a host on both <br> transport systems. <br>
