Meet the travel trade!

Visitors will meet more than 900 exhibitors representing products, services and destinations in over 120 countries all over the world, and from all parts of Norway.

With new exhibiting countries and more participants at the national pavilions, the 9th annual travel trade fair in Oslo will be one of the most important meeting places for the travel trade, and the first to go in Scandinavia in 1996!

Find new contacts for future business: 68% of the trade visitors established new business relations at the fair. Select your contacts, and use Reiseliv as a "work-shop" for doing business.
