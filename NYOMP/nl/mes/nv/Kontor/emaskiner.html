Office machines and equipment

Installing modern office equipment obviously represents an effective way to tackle today's competitive markets. A lot is happening in this area at present, with new opportunities and integration. A photocopier, for instance, is no longer "just" a copier. Office and Computerisation'96 aims to be a complete exhibition that embraces most of the solutions available for the modern office in 1996. The show will accordingly present Norwegian industry and commerce with the latest developments in office machines, equipment and accessories, including:

Photocopier * Audiovisual equipment * Copying and reproduction equipment * Stationary * Post room systems and equipment * Telephone and telefax systems * Time registration * Printing equipment * Office supplies

The market is large, and encompasses numerous products. But there are also many customers, so make sure you are at Office and Computerisation'96 to secure yur slice of the cake.
