A decision support tool for well integrity on a World Wide Web (WWW)

Bratli R.K., Stene, F., Aadnoy, B.S. , Giljarhus, S., Fagereng, S., Bautz, S., "A decision support tool for well integrity on a World Wide Web (WWW)", IADC Asian Well Control Conference 1994, 1-2 Dec. 1994, Singapore.

Summary

This paper will present a decision support system for well planning, and follow-up of well integrity (safe operating mud weight window) during drilling operations. PFAS, short for Planning and Field Application Software, is a Windows based modular system, using Internet as the communication network for data exchange and technological support. Large amounts of data are collected during drilling operations to ensure primary well control, and to reduce the risk of well stability problems. However, after the well is drilled and completed, the drilling and well data is often not easily accessible, mainly due to poor data storage and retrieval systems. By using a decision support software system, like PFAS, these data may be more readily available for display and use in future planning, thus providing a more complete set of data for our prognosis. The paper also brings up the discussion of the future role of drilling contractors and the concept of integrated services. Appendix-A shows two field cases while Appendix-B presents a novel technique for establishing a model for the horizontal stress field.
