TECHNICAL SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT

For the development and maintenance of SESAM, our integrated FEM-based structural analysis system for offshore and ship structures, DNV sesam is looking for a dedicated person to join our team at Høvik.

MS Windows Technology

The main activities and responsibilities will be within software architecture, user interface systems and data communication.

We are looking for a dynamic person with education and experience from one or several of these areas:

  • Programming on MS Windows 3.1 and NT
  • Visual C++, OLE, COM and MFC
  • C/C++ programming
  • Object Oriented methodology
Interest in computers and programming is required, and experience in development of engineering software is desired.

In our evaluation of candidates we will emphasise result orientation, interest in understanding the client's needs, and the ability to communicate well and work closely with people. A positive attitude and sense of humour are a must.

DNV Sesam can offer a flexible and open working environment among highly qualified people.
