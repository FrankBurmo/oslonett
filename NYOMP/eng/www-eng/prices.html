The cost of announcing at Oslonett's webserver

All prices below are without salestax and valid as of June 1st 1995. All prices for renting space on our webserver are for three months.

Prices

The pricing of announcing on the Web consists of two parts.

One part consists of the work involved in preparing and maintaining the information you want announce. The cost is estimated by hours used.

The second part is rent, i.e. the price you pay for renting space on our webserver for three months.

The price you pay for renting space depends on the amount of data. In addition you can order extra services such as statistics, order forms, WAIS searching mechanism and so forth. Each of these services cost extra, but for some of our products these extra services are included. More on this below.

Webproducts

Oslonett have four main products
Advertisement, external referance
An advertisement of a product, job etc. as in a newspaper.

In the same category are external references. If you have information on another webserver, you can link to this information.
No extra services included

Company profile
A short presentation of the company and its products. Limited to five pages and 2 pictures.
Weekly statistics included.

Information center, small
Suitable if you want to make more information available. Limited to 15 pages and 5 pictures.
Statistics and one order form included.

Information center, large
The complete package. No limits on number of pages or pictures, just a datavolume limit on 50 Mb.
Statistics, order form and WAIS based search engine included.

Extra services

 It is possible to order extra services in addition to having information on Oslonett's Web server.
Statistics
All announcers are interested in statistics showing how many are reading their information. Oslonett can generate detailed statistics with number of hits, where the requests come from and so forth. The statistics will be sent by email or fax once a week.
Order forms
The many possibilities for two way communication and interaction makes it especially easy to make order forms, coupons, questionaires etc. The contents can be sent to you by email or fax.
WAIS based search engines
WAIS is an acronym meaning Wide Area Information Servers, and is a database system which makes it possible to search a set of of files. It is possible to put up a WAIS database for an announcer. This makes it very easy to find interesting quickly.
