Here you will find information about how to navigate around in Oslonett's Web, explaining the meaning of the different buttons and symbols frequently used.
One might question the necessity for explaining how to navigate through a Web-site like this, and we would be the first to agree. However, most of the buttons and information-pointers are in Norwegian, a language not easily understood by foreginers. Therefore we will provide some basic information to help our visitors to navigate inside Oslonett's extensive Web.

The Homepage

Our main homepage - in Norwegian - gives easy and quick access to the different informatioon categories in our Web. In the upper left corner there is an image of the Union Jack - click there and you will get to the English homepage.

Most of the information in these cathegories are in Norwegian. However, some of our announcers present themselves in English. Links to these pages are found under the "click here" field in the introductionary text. It is advisable, though, that you try out the "ordinary" cathegories, there might be much of interest to you.

KVASIR is a very powerful search facility, developed by Oslonett, giving you "instant" access to information from all over Scandinavia. Enter your search words, or just click around and see what you find.

The headers on ON's pages are imagemaps; try to click on different parts of the headers and see whats happening.

The links on the bottom of the page - About Oslonett, Internet, What's Up, Help and Search are all provided to give fast access to relevant information.

The 8 sub-categories

Choosing one of the sub-categories, you are presented with a new page with links to either new sub-categories or links to our announcers. The "Info" button in the header gives information about this particular category, in Norwegian.

The buttons on the bottom
