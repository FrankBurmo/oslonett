Information about Oslonett AS

Oslonett AS was founded 12. december 1991, in the building housing Dept. of Informatics (Computer Science), Gaustadbekkdalen, University of Oslo. All 16 people involved in this projects were computer scientists.


The idea was to offer services in connection with the Internet, electronic mail, UNIX and WANs to the commercial and industrial enterprise. Most of the partners in Oslonett have years of theoretical and practical experience in the areas of interest, as the scientific community of Gaustadbekkdalen was one of the first in Norway to use UNIX and Internet.

The persons behind Oslonett all have experience either as researchers or system staff. Together with the employees, this is an unique source of competence and skill providing the backbone of our commercial ideas.


This closeness to an active research community is in many ways Oslonett's trademark, and will be continually emphasised in the future. We believe knowledge and a dynamic structure is vital to survive in the market of Internet services.

Oslonett is proud to be one of the firms laying the fundament for commercial Internet activities in Norway.
