Information on services from Oslonett AS

Welcome to Oslonett AS, Norway's only complete Internet provider. We deliver a complete range of services for business and private alike.
Oslonett Aksess/PPP
Our main service for private and small business customers wanting an affordable "plug-and-play" connection to the Internet.
Oslonett Internett
Oslonett will also deliver permanent connections to Internet, based on permanent lines, ISDN and Frame-Relay. We cover most of the country, providing POPs in several regions. systems- and network management
Oslonett offers consulting services regarding construction and design of Internet services. These include firewalls and systems- and network management of UNIX systems. We have unique competence when it comes to UNIX and Internet, with more than 10 years of experience.
Announcing on the World Wide Web
Norway's first commercial World Wide Web server was set up by Oslonett. Today we offer a range of services for buying, selling and announcing through the Internet and World Wide Web, and also all kind of consultant services in connection with the Web.
Oslonett Course - "Intervett"
Oslonett's course department - Intervett - has developed a series of very popular courses, specializing on Internet and the World Wide Web.
