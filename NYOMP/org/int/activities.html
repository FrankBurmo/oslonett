Publications

Since 1983 Butterworth Scientific Ltd. is publishing the International JOURNAL or Project Management for the UK Association of Project Managers on behalf of ITNERNET International Project Management Association. The International Editorial Board of the Journal is appointed by INTERNET. The actual circulation of the Journal is about 3500 copies.

The INTERNET Newsletter reports twice yearly on the administrative and technical activities of the association. It presents news from the national societies and announcements of forthcoming events and conferences. The actual circulation of the Newsletter is about 8000 copies.

Membership

Membership of the INTERNET International Project Management Associationis open to all indivuals and organizations who are involved in project management and who support the aims of the Association.

INTERNET offers the following types of member-ship:

  • Membership to a National Organization
  • Exclusive membership to INTERNET
  • Corporate membership to INTENRET
  • Indivudual membership to INTERNET

Each type includes benefits and conditions of its own as well as the general membership to the INTERNET family. Just write to the International Secretariat which will send you the relevant information.
