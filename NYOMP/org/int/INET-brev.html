E-Mail in INTERNET, - on Internet

by Thorhallur Gudmundsson

Background

In order to start up ICBM, INTERNET Computer Based Member service, the phase I, E- Mail services, we have establish "post-box" at the Oslonett, and subscription/ IP provider opportunities has been mate available all over memberships countries through organisation of IP providers, EUnett.

This article is meant to give overview how and where to go further, as an follow up on the article written in the last issue of INTERNET Newsletter.

What is it all about-?

Most of today's corporations have internal so-cold E-Mail services which started up in the late 80 ties. Main issue has been able to distribute massages to individuals, groups as departments, marketing areas etc. through the company computer network.

In the universities, in the research and development environment in general there has been great needs for open communication. Therefore, the E-mail/ communication channel Internet got a extensive and broadly usage. Internet has approx 25 mill users today and are rapidly growing.

In addition to Internet, several external E-mail services are offered in the market. However, there is two main directions, closed i.e. X 400 standard and open network as Internet.

Many international companies are now connecting their internal mail to external mail service. This has result in a that several of our members are on the nett, eater through their corporations or those working at the universities have link to Internet

What are we doing at our side-?

From INTERNET side we will provide assess to the nett infrastructure and assist to help our members to obtain "IP provider" in most of major towns in Europe. At the users side, the users have to have assess to standard PC and modem, to link the PC to normal telephone network.

To obtain local IP provider, a telephone assess to Internet in your district at local telephone fare, we have been working to get IP provider organisation EUnet to organise link for our INTERNET members to our server at Oslonett, for the cost of local telephone tariff for the users. This service is available in all INTERNET countries of Europe and as well in Egypt.

How to get started-?

To link members of INTERNET on the nett, we request you to contact our Service Secretariat which have the list of EUnet service offices through out Europe. Our service Secretariat will send you the software needed together with the manuals, with the list of personal E-Mail addresses and workgroups, after you have obtain subscription locally.

What is the cost to run -?

By use of Eunet, the users will only pay the local telephone tariff during connection to the "Nett" together with approx 1.00 NOK= 0.15 CFR pr. min while you emptying the mailbox or you sending a mail, which only take less than 5 sec. for an A4 letter.

By connection to Internet through Eunet you will be "routed" to our server, after the password, you are on the nett!! You will been able to contact the Service Secretariat and they will give you hand if needed the first times.

By use of Eunet as a local router to INTERNET server, you will only pay for each time you use by

0.15 CFR pr. min. You can alternatively obtain local IP provider if you are using the nett extensively, the price for the annual subscription in Norway e.g. is approx 100,- CFR. and the hourly fee ( in addition to the local telephone) varieties from 10,- Sfr. pr hour to 0.-, depending on time of the day.
