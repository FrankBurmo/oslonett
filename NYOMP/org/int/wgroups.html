Working groups

The service of providing working groups is a kind of mail list where members of IPMA associations can develop their contactnet by being connected to people with similar interests crossing boarders within various special interest groups.

The distribution is based on two typs of «mailing lists» open and «closed» which have been definded. New working group/ mailing list can easily be added/ established. The open mailing list are listed below and it is just for you to request to be on tjhe list by «dobble clikk» on the name of the maillist and you will be contact to our maillist. To be able to control the mailing distrubution, you have to be confirmed as registrated as member in a IPMA National Associtation. Therefore we have to request for 10 CHF for each maillist connection pr. year.

For members of IPMA project teams, the system of working groups will be a facilitator of a communication infrastructure between the members of the team as a closed mailinglist and open forgiven general informasjon to all others of who are interested, for instance Certificaton.

We have furher exceptions that people within similar practice / branches within PM are interested in establish their own working group, i.e. pharmacy, telecommunications, risk, cost etc. It is up to the members to be define the needs and what should be offered,- your effort is just to give us a signal! In APM, the Specific Interest Group, SIG, has already been established as a forum for interest groups and hopefully this type of services can be a good facilator for both them and others with simular interest.

The main grouping of the working groups has been defind as the following:

  • SIG groups; both with PM within specific branches and PM topics
  • All IPMA ongoing project, both as external- and internal information channel
  • All Countries with in IPMA
  • For distribution of administration matters, both for information of internal- and external parties.

Based on the mail list, our server will then distribute all incoming «mail»to all addresses/ members on the list. For instants, for those which are interested in certification and want to be kept updated by being on the mail list will be given address on the «mail list; cert@IPMA.no». List of working groups will be updated regularly in the Newsletter and on the WWW .
