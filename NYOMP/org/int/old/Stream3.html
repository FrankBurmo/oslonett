Stream#3: Risk Management

For confident decision making in an uncertain world 

Session 3.1 Risk Management in Business and organizations
Principles and Methods
Time: Thursday June 9, 1400 - 1800 hrs.

Risk and uncertainty are always present in society and in the business arenas. Decisions must be made, and a structured Risk Management approach is a necessity for handling uncertainty in the decision process. This session deals with the state of the art in Risk Management. Basic principles and methods will be discussed and recommendations and guidelines will be presented. 

session 3.2 Risk Management and Decision Making
Prior to project start up
Time: Friday June 10, 0830 - 1200 hrs.

Project success is heavily dependent on the activities prior to formal project start up. During this period, the level of uncertainty is at it's greatest. Fortunately, the rate of influence on decisions is also it's largest. Focus is on handling uncertainties and risk leading to confident decision making. Recommended procedures and approaches are outlined with specific emphasis on the managerial and critical decision making challenges. 

Session 3.3 Risk Management and Decision Making
During Project Execution
Time: Friday June 10, 1300 - 1630 hrs.

The nature of risk and uncertainty during the project execution phase differs from the phases prior to project start up. This has an impact in the types of Risk Management approaches to be utilized, and in particular on what is the appropriate level of dealing for the various tasks. Risk Management is of special interest for consequence assessments. Deviations in expect performance and result may require such analyses in order to find the way back on track according to original plans and schedules. 

Session 3.4 Risk Management
Case Stories
Time: Saturday June 11, 0900 - 1230 hrs.

Case stories are summarized in this session, with examples of successful planning cases. Both large and small events and tasks from the business sector and from public affairs are summarized. Focus is made on clarification and simplification by use of risk analyses.
