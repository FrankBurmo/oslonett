The organization

The national societies, the exclusive members, the individual and corporate members of INTERNET elect the representatives to the Council of Delegates which has the function of a general assembly, meeting once a year to decide on matters of importance to the association. It elects its Chairman and the President and the Auditors of the Association.

The INTERNET International Board is responsible for the management of the association and it sets the guidelines for its activities. The Board constitutes itself and appoints the Executive Committee and the manager of the International Secretariat. The International Secretariat with its officers in Zürich/Switzerland is the centre of communication for the National Organizations and for all INTERNET bodies and members.

The Culture

An important INTERNET function is the international exchange of ideas, attitudes and experiences. Communication is being improved by presenting new types of congress and seminar work and by producing new international publications on project management.

Special emphasis is given to the discussion between the scientists of the academic world and the experts from industry.
