Format Of The Congress/Who Should Attend

INTERNET'94 will be a marketplace for the exchange of experience in the latest developments and trends in management and leadership. We aim to initiate the generation of a new set of standards for management and leadership in a future world of rapid changes and turbulence.

The form of the Congress will vary - plenary sessions, parallel streams and workshops where active participation from each individual is required.

In addition, through the layout of the Congress we intend to stimulate integration between the exhibitors and the participants. The exhibitors area is directly connected to the plenary conference hall and the conference halls hosting the parallel streams.

Quality communication between all involved parties is a top priority for the Congress!

The main market to be served by the Congress are professionals like yourself working within industry, Financial Services, Construction, Public organizations and Consultancies. People who utilize executive and project management skills, and wish to improve their current performance in these areas.

CONGRESS LOCATION: SAS SCANDINAVIA HOTEL, OSLO

The SAS Scandinavia Hotel offers the very best conference facilities. The hotel is centrally located in downtown Oslo, just 5 minute walk from the city centre and only 10 minutes drive from Fornebu Airport, There is a regular airport bus service to and from the hotel.

The hotel has 491 rooms. All rooms are equipped with a bath, radio, colour TV, CNN, video, telephone and mini-bar and have 24-hour room service. The hotel has a health club with swimming pool, sauna, solarium and fitness centre. There is a hairdresser and shopping arcade on the ground floor and a SAS ticket office in the lobby with airline check-in service. (Monday-Sunday 7 a.m. - 2 p.m.)
