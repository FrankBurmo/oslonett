The History

INTERNET - International Project Management Association is a non-profit making organization registered in Zürich as an international Association under Swiss law.

Its objective is to improve, on an international basis, the ability and the will of people and of organizations to work together in managing resources, and to plan and implement important development projects and programmes.

In 1965 the Association began in Europe as a discussion group of managers of international projects who were concerned with the increasing difficulty of managing large projects and scarce resources. The first International Congress was held 1967 in Vienna and participants came from 30 different countries. Since that time, INTERNET has developed steadily.

The Goals and Activities

The International Project Management Associations is continuing to

  • develop the association on a worldwide basis, as a viable organization of practitioners and academics concerned with the problem of managing projects, operations and programmes.

  • provide an international forum for the exchange of ideas and experiences, and to identify and explore problems and requirements

  • provide international guidelines and standards for education and practice in managing developmental work

  • promote international friendship, trust, goodwill and cooperation between professionals and educators in project management

  • promote the development and use of improved management tools

  • explore and resolve the cross-cultural communication problems of international projects

  • improve the recognition and acceptance of project management concepts and methods

  • improve the general management enviroment in whcih the projct manager is working

The Organization

The national societies, the exclusive members, the individual and corporate members of INTERNET elect the representatives to the Council of Delegates which has the function of a general assembly, meeting once a year to decide on matters of importance to the association. It elects its Chairman and the President and the Auditors of the Association.

The INTERNET International Board is responsible for the management of the association and it sets the guidelines for its activities. The Board constitutes itself and appoints the Executive Committee and the manager of the International Secretariat. The International Secretariat with its officers in Zürich/Switzerland is the centre of communication for the National Organizations and for all INTERNET bodies and members.

The Culture

An important INTERNET function is the international exchange of ideas, attitudes and experiences. Communication is being improved by presenting new types of congress and seminar work and by producing new international publications on project management.

Special emphasis is given to the discussion between the scientists of the academic world and the experts from industry.

The Publications

Since 1983 Butterworth Scientific Ltd. is publishing the International JOURNAL or Project Management for the UK Association of Project Managers on behalf of ITNERNET International Project Management Association. The International Editorial Board of the Journal is appointed by INTERNET. The actual circulation of the Journal is about 3500 copies.

The INTERNET Newsletter reports twice yearly on the administrative and technical activities of the association. It presents news from the national societies and announcements of forthcoming events and conferences. The actual circulation of the Newsletter is about 8000 copies.

Your Membership

Membership of the INTERNET International Project Management Associationis open to all indivuals and organizations who are involved in project management and who support the aims of the Association.

INTERNET offers the following types of member-ship:

  • Membership to a National Organization
  • Exclusive membership to INTERNET
  • Corporate membership to INTENRET
  • Indivudual membership to INTERNET

Each type includes benefits and conditions of its own as well as the general membership to the INTERNET family. Just write to the International Secretariat which will send you the relevant information.
