New York General Information

Return to New York At-A-Glance

For information and descriptive flyers about New York City, stop by the Visitor Information Center of the New York Convention & Visitors Bureau, Two Columbus Circle at Broadway and 59th St. (212) 397-8222. Hours: Mon.-Fri. 9am-6pm,weekends and holidays 10am-3pm.

Visitor information is also available from the following organizations:

Big Apple Greeter. Free program to welcome tourists to New York City, invites visitors to share New York with a volunteer "friend." Greeters show visitors the color and flavor of the city's many neighborhoods throughout the five boroughs. Call 48-72 hours in advance for appointment. Special language requirements or disability needs can be met. Contact: Office of the Manhattan Borough President, 1 Centre St., NY, NY 10007 (212) 669-2896.

The Dairy, Central Park Visitor Center, Central Park near 64th St. Features exhibits about park, games and a gift shop. Tue.-Sun. 11am-5pm, Fri. 1pm-5pm. (212)794-6564

Bronx. For information on free and low-cost Bronx events--theater, dance, music, art--contact: Bronx Council on the Arts, 1738 Hone Ave. (718) 931-9500. Bronx Tourism Council, 851 Grand Concourse, (718) 590--3518.

Brooklyn. For information on cultural events, BACA, 200 Eastern Pkwy., Brooklyn, (718)625-0800.

Lower Manhattan Cultural Council. Ongoing music, dance, and readings. For information and complimentary copy of Downtown, LMCC's arts and cultural activities calendar, call (212) 432-0900.

Queens. For arts and cultural events information, including the Queens Cultural Guide, please call Queens Council on the Arts (718) 291-ARTS. For the Discover Queens Guide, contact Queens Borough President's Office, (718) 520-3823.

Staten Island. For tourist information call the Staten Island Chamber of Commerce, (718) 727-1900.

745-ARTS. New York City's free centralized "information-by-Telephone" service for the arts.

Transportation
Bus and Subway Information
Call NYC Transit Authority 6am-9pm daily, (718) 330-1234. Subway fare is $1.25; tokens available at booths. Exact change of $1.25 or subway token required on buses.

Airports
You can fly into any of three airports operated by the Port Authority from almost anywhere in the world--Newark Airport, LaGuardia Airport, or JFK International Airport. Bus or rail links these busy airports to The Big Apple

By bus, you'll arrive in the heart of Manhattan at The Port Authority Bus Terminal at Eighth Avenue and 42nd Street. By car, interconnecting parkways and highways take you across bridges or through tunnels that lead directly into the city. By train, Grand Central Station and Pennsylvania Station, both in midtown Manhattan, serve thousands daily.
