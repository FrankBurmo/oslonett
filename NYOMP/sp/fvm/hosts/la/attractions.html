Downtown / Exposition Park

El Pueblo de Los Angeles
(213) 625-5045
This historic monument is the site of early Los Angeles where forty-four settlers established a farming community in 1781. It is now a monument of the city of Los Angeles and home to the world-famous Olvera Street.

George C. Page Museum of La Brea Discoveries
(213) 936-2230
The world-famous La Brea Tar Pits provide the setting for this unique museum, which showcases outstanding examples of the four million mammal and bird fossils recovered from the site--the richest discovery of Ice Age fossils the world has ever known.

Hollywood Wax Museum
(213) 462-5991
See over 200 wax figures of movie, music, sports and television stars. Enter the Chamber of Horrors. Feast your eyes on Leonardo Da Vinci's Last Supper.

MOCA-The Museum of Contemporary Art
(213) 626-6222
Located in a unique art exhibition space designed by architect Arata Isozaki, MOCA is devoted to regional, national and international art from 1940 to the present. It features painting, sculpture, drawings, prints, photographs, installations and environmental work as well as the newest forms in contemporary art.

Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County
(213) 744-3466
Discover 600 million years of earth's history in the nation's third largest natural history museum. Dinosaurs, animal dioramas, a live insect zoo, beautiful gems and minerals, exotic pre-Columbian artifacts, Native American cultures--you'll find it all here.

Plaza de la Raza
(213) 223-2475
Plaza de la Raza is the cultural heart of East Los Angeles, presenting the best of Latino art and culture through theatre, musical performances, dance and revolving exhibits in their Boathouse Gallery.

Hollywood / Central Los Angeles

Los Angeles County Museum of Art
(213) 857-6111
LACMA is an encyclopedic museum featuring art from many periods and cultures. The art of starting your weekend right! Surround yourself with interesting people, beautiful art, terrific music, food and fun every Friday evening at the museum from 4:30-9:00 pm.

Tamara
(213) 851-9999
Truly the ultimate, intimate theatrical experience, this true story allows you to follow the characters throughout an elaborate Italian villa as the exciting events unfold around you. The evening includes a champagne reception, a lavish intermission buffet and free parking!

Pasadena / North County / Valley

Actors Alley Repertory Theatre
(818) 508-4200
An exciting live performance theatre with subscription season, including musicals, comedy, drama and new works. Located in the historic El Portal Theatre. Actors Alley presents the first Equity performance space in the San Fernando Valley in 26 years.

Art Galleries, California State University, Northridge
(818) 885-2248
Two galleries emphasizing art from international and multicultural perspectives, particularly concentrating on art of the Pacific Rim. Historical and contemporary, thematic and media-oriented exhibitions reflect the broad spectrum of art in our global society. Third gallery features student work.

Descanso Gardens
(818) 952-4400
125 acres of lush vegetation, babbling streams, waterfalls, Koi ponds and a water fowl sanctuary. Nature trails lead through a native oak forest filled with over 100,000 camellias and a collection of California native plants. Seasonal color displays and International Rosarium.

Gene Autry Western Heritage Museum
(213) 667-2000
Experience all the excitement of the American West. Seven permanent galleries and one changing gallery depict both the real West as well as the mythical West of movies and TV. Authentic clothes, tools, vehicles, weapons, sculpture, artifacts and fine Western art.

Huntington Library, Art Collections and Botanical Gardens
(818) 405-2141
Escape to a cultural oasis at The Huntington. Enjoy an outstanding collection of 18th and 19th century British, French, and American art works, a world-renowned library of rare books and manuscripts, and 130 acres of breathtaking gardens.

L.A. County Arboretum
(818) 821-3222
127 acre arboretum and botanic garden. Perennial garden, waterfalls, herb garden and rain forest being developed. Site of "Lucky" Baldwin's original ranch, historic buildings include Victorian cottage, Adobe, Coach Barn and Depot. Bird watching, tram tours, gift shop and cafe.

Los Angeles Zoo
(213) 666-4090
Enjoy more than 1500 rare animals in natural settings. Explore Adventure Island, featuring animals of the American Southwest and interactive games for children of all ages. Marvel at the World of Birds and Wild in the City animal shows.

Pacific Asia Museum
(818) 449-2742
Pacific Asia Museum is the only museum in the Southland dedicated to the arts of Asia and the Pacific Rim. A California State Historic Landmark with an authentic Chinese courtyard garden.

Six Flags Magic Mountain
(805) 255-4100
From the most thrilling world-class roller coasters, to adventurous water rides, to action-packed shows, to an enchanted six-acre paradise just for kids, Six Flags Magic Mountain treats you and your family to the ride of your life.

The Pasadena Playhouse
(818) 356-PLAY
The Pasadena Playhouse, built in 1927, is the State Theatre of California. Many illustrious figures, including Dana Andrews, Victor Mature, Rue McClanahan, Raymond Burr, Dustin Hoffman, Sally Struthers, Gene Hackman and Charles Bronson, thread through the history of this legendary theatre. Since its refurbishment and reopening in 1985, the Playhouse has produced over seventy productions including six World Premieres.

Southwest Museum
(213) 221-2164
Visit the Southwest Museum and explore Native Cultures of North America. Four main galleries, temporary exhibitions, and educational programs focus on the native peoples of the Southwest, California, the Great Plains and the Pacific Northwest Coast.

Westside / Santa Monica

Beyond Baroque Literary/Arts Center
(310) 822-3006
The only literary center in Southern California, presenting readings, writer's workshops, music, a small gallery, theatre, film screenings and an alternative press bookstore and library. The Friday Night Reading Series features over 80 local, national and international writers annually.

Fowler Museum of Cultural History, UCLA
(310) 825-4361
Changing exhibition galleries explore the beauty and richness of world arts and cultures. Exciting multimedia presentations focus on people of Africa, Asia, Latin America and the Pacific. The meaning and use of silver in 17th - 19th century Europe is explored in permanent exhibition.

The J. Paul Getty Museum
(310) 458-2003
Greek and roman antiquities, pre-20th century European paintings, drawings, sculpture, illuminated manuscripts, decorative arts and 19th and 20th century American and European photographs. Museum building is a recreation of a first century A.D. Roman country villa with gardens.

Santa Monica Museum of Art
(310) 399-0433
Experience contemporary art in an architectural setting, Edgemar, designed by Frank Gehry. Changing exhibitions include works by emerging LA based artist, mid-career surveys, and thematic shows. Free public tours on the first Sunday of each month.

Simon Wiesenthal Center, Beit Hashoah-Museum of Tolerance
(310) 553-8403
This exciting experiential museum promotes understanding among all people. Two themes are explored through unique interactive exhibits: the dynamics of racism and prejudice in American life; and the history of the Holocaust, the most monumental example of man's inhumanity to man.

UCLA Film and Television Archive
(310) 206-FILM
Presenting throughout the year a changing schedule of public screenings, from preserved and restored classics of the Hollywood silver screen, to retrospectives of acclaimed directors, to new and emerging talent in film and video productions from around the world.
