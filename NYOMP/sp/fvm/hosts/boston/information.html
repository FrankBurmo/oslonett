Greater Boston General Information

Boston is the capital of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and the largest city in New England. The city was founded in 1630, 10 years after the Pilgrims landed at Plymouth. Surrounded on three sides by water, Boston grew as a harbor town and has always been strongly linked to the sea. Much of the Boston landscape is the result of extensive land-fill operations that began in the early 1700's and continued well into the 20th century.

Today, Boston thrives as a center for medicine, high tech, finance, education, and much more. Boston's busy port is one of the nation's finest and its more than 60 colleges and universities offer outstanding educational opportunities.

The City of Boston is 48 square miles and has a population of 575,000. Boston is the center of a large metropolitan area that is 1,100 square miles and is home to almost 3 million people.

Boston, one of the most popular destinations in the world, hosts over eight and one-half million visitors, convention delegates and business travelers each year.

Liquor Laws

 One must be at least 21 years of age to purchase liquor. A valid driver's license, liquor purchase identification card or passport is required as proof of age.
All liquor stores close at 11:00pm and all day Sunday. Bars in Massachusetts close anywhere from 11:00pm to 2:00am.

Boston Information Sources

 There is no shortage of things to see and enjoy in Boston, Cambridge and surrounding areas, and all of its treasures are within walking distance of each other. The Greater Boston Convention & Visitors Bureau is a visitor's resource for where to go to get the best from Boston. Visit our information center to learn the best way to explore and enjoy the area.

Boston Common Visitor Information Center
Located at the Tremont Street side of Boston Common. Public transportation: Park Street subway station is 50 yards from the Center servicing Green and Red line subways. Marking the beginning of Boston's historic Freedom Trail, this booth provides visitor information about Greater Boston, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and New England. Open Monday-Saturday 8:30am-5pm, Sunday 9am-5pm.

Prudential Center Visitor Kiosk
Located at Center Court in the Prudential Center, this booth provides visitor information about Greater Boston, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and New England. Open Monday-Friday 8:30am-6pm, Saturday 10am-6pm, and Sunday 11:30am-6pm.

For Up-to-Date Visitor Information call the Greater Boston Convention & Visitors Bureau at (617) 536-4100 or (800) 888-5515 (outside Massachusetts), Monday-Friday 9am-5pm.
