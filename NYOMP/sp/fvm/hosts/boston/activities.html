Activities

 While you're in town, check out some of the wonderful entertainment Boston has to offer. Call the phone number following each description for more information and reservations.

For up-to-date information, call the Greater Boston Convention and Visitors Bureau at 1-800-888-5515 (outside Massachusetts) or (617) 536-4100.

Call Boston By Phone(TM) for a one-stop shopping and reservation service which connects your call directly to hotels, restaurants, museums, attractions, sightseeing and more. 1-800-374-7400

Boston Pops

 Treat yourself to light classics and popular favorites. The Boston Pops performs Tuesday through Sunday from May through mid July at Symphony Hall. In early July, they perform a series of outdoor concerts at the Hatch Shell on the Esplande. (617) 266-1200

Boston Red Sox Baseball

Visit historic Fenway Park to watch America's favorite pastime and Boston's favorite team! (617) 267-8661

Metropolitan District Commission's Performing Arts Series

You can enjoy classic cinema or classic rock, theatre or dance at Boston's ``open air theatre'' -- Esplande's Hatch Shell. (617) 727-5114, ext. 555

Boston Harbor Cruises

Admire the Boston skyline from an aquatic point of view. Listen to live music, dance, eat a delightful dinner, or simply book passage on one of the many cruise ships that sail the Harbor. (617) 536-4100

Concerts at Great Woods

Your favorite world-renown musicians show their style at this outdoor setting in Mansfield. (508) 339-2333

Swan Boat

Experience a cherished Boston tradition--ride on a pedal-driven Swan Boat around the lagoon in the Public Garden. (617) 522-1966

WBOS Earthwatch Concert Series

Spread out a blanket and enjoy a picnic supper at Copley Square Park while local and national musicians perform every Thursday evening. (617) 787-0929

Dorothy Curran Concert Series on City Hall Plaza

Bring your lawn chairs and dancing shoes to City Hall Plaza for Wednesday evening concerts, perfect for an audience of all ages. (617) 635-4505

Waterfront Park Jazz Series

Fridays, from July 1 through August 26. Enjoy the jive of jazz musicians and the spectacular Harbor view from Christopher Columbus Waterfront Park. (617) 635-3911

Shopping on Newberry Street

Boston Neighborhood Festivals

Celebrate Boston's diverse ethnicity with:

  • Italian Feasts in the North End
  • Puerto Rican Festival in the South End
  • August Moon Festival in Chinatown
  • Dominican Festival in the South End
  • Caribbean Carnival in Dorchester

(617) 635-3911
