MICHIGAN STATE FAIR

Background:
Dating back to 1849, the Michigan State Fair is the oldest in the nation. Many of the traditional attractions of the fair still remain. Visitors can see livestock shows, sheep shearers, wool weavers and award-winning agricultural products from around the state. This is a great chance for children and city dwellers to touch prize farm animals and sample farm-made dairy products. The fair's permanent location is the Michigan State Fairgrounds where you can find buildings from 1921 to present.
If you prefer the more modern attractions, you can find them here as well. Young and old alike can ride the double ferris wheel, watch the famous racing pigs, listen to free concerts and sample a variety of great foods.

Details:
The Michigan Exposition and State Fairgrounds are located at 1120 W. State Fair Ave. (Eight Mile Road and Woodward Avenue), Detroit, MI 48203, (313) 368-1000
The 1994 Michigan State Fair will be held from August 26 - September 5, 1994 (Laborday).
