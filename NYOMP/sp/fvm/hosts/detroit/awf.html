AFRICAN WORLD FESTIVAL



Background:
The African World Festival is Detroit's largest ethnic festival bringing people from all ages, cultures and backgrounds together to explore the African and African American experience. Now entering its 13th year, the popular festival offers free music, a diverse artist's market, international cuisine from more than 20 countries, and cultural activities for kids and adults.
The festival takes place annually the third weekend in August in Detroit's popular gathering place, Hart Plaza. In 1994, more than 1.5 million people turned out to free reggae, jazz, rhythm & blues, pop and gospel concerts performed by Jamaican, British, Ugandan, Liberian, national and regional entertainers.
What's Special:
The more than 150 Artist Marketplace artisans that travel from around the world to sell their authentic hand-crafted African apparel, fine art, sculptures, dolls and jewelry. The Artist Marketplace magically transports festival goers to an open-air market in Senegal or the West Indies.
