Sherry Washington, owner of Detroit's Sherry Washington Gallery:
I love the curried shrimp, caviar and samosa at the Himalaya Dining Room (Windsor, 841 Ouellette Ave., Windsor; 519/258-2804). Chef Oza prays over your food and I be lieve you feel better. And the Phoe nicia Restaurant (Suburban North, 588 S. Woodward, Birmingham; 810/644-3122) has the best rice pudding in the world. I've been in practically every Middle Eastern restaurant within a 100-mile radius and it's the best and the freshest.

Emily Murphy, host of The Emily Murphy Show, aired on WTVS-TV Channel 56 (PBS) and on Booth ca ble stations: I love the grilled chicken fettuc cine at Kruse and Muer on Main (Suburban North, 327 Main, Roch ester; 810/652-9400). It's made with tomatoes, grilled chicken, peppers, mushrooms and a cream sauce. It's out of this world.

Ric Bohy: I'm nuts about the sea food risotto at Gino's Surf Ristorante (37400 Jefferson, Harrison Township; 810/468-2611). Intense flavor, and it's full of shrimp, scallops and such.

SOUL FOOD
Sherry Washington: The East Franklin Restaurant (Downtown, 1440 Franklin, Detroit; 313/393-0018) has the best chicken and greens.

Ric Bohy: I love just about any thing made by Brenda the Soul Food Cook at Bea's Comedy Kitchen (Downtown, 541 E. Larned, Detroit; 313/961-2581). I am particularly fond of her greens smoky, rich, the best around and cornbread stuffing. Too bad their meal schedule is cut back to just dinner, Friday and Saturday.

SOUP'S ON
Reverend Dr. William Quick: You can't beat the minestrone soup at Lelli's Inn (Downtown, 7618 Woodward, Detroit; 313/871-1590). It's very thick and seasoned just perfectly.

ICE CREAM
Emily Murphy: Ray's chocolate ice cream (Suburban North, 4233 Coolidge, Royal Oak; 810/549-5256) is worth standing in line for.

Ron Garbinski, executive editor of AAA Michigan Living magazine:
