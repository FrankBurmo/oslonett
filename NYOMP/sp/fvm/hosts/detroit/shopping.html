SHOPPING

Background:
Metropolitan Detroit offers 150 shopping centers with at least 100,000 sq. ft., averaging about 40 stores and service establishments. These areas include 23 major centers in excess of 500,000 sq. ft., vibrant shopping districts in communities like Birmingham and Grosse Pointe, and dynamic theme attractions like The Alley in Greektown. The New Center Area near downtown also offers exciting shopping destinations.
Strong demand has attracted and retained a number of major department stores, including Crowley's, Hudson's, Jacobson's, Kohl's, Lord & Taylor, Mervyn's, Montgomery Ward, J.C. Penney, Saks Fifth Avenue, Sears, Neiman-Marcus and Nordstrom's coming soon.
If you're a bargain hunter, you'll love Metro Detroit. We're home to Kmart, the world's second largest retailer and the largest discount department store chain. Stiff competition comes from the like of Meijer, Service Merchandise, Target, F.W. Woolworth, F&M and Wal Mart. Specialty off-price and discount stores abound, including discount malls, Burlington Coat Factory, Fretter's Appliance, Kuppenheimer, ABC Warehouse, Designer Shoe Warehouse, A.L. Price and Bed, Bath & Beyond, among others.
If you're here to shop, Detroit has it all!
