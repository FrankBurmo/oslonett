MICHIGAN RENAISSANCE FESTIVAL

Background:
If you suddenly find yourself standing among knights in armor, court jesters or 16th century villagers, you must have wandered through time at the Michigan Renaissance Festival.
Each year, visitors drive to join the merrymaking of the European Renaissance village of Hollygrove. Costumed townspeople, artisans, minstrels and magicians provide continuous entertainment. You can become a part of the pageantry as you witness jousting battles, participate in axe-throw, snack on a hand held turkey drumstick, or stroll through town in the daily parade.

What's Special:
In addition, to the regular activities, each weekend features a different theme. Jump right in with grape stomping and w-h-i-n-i-n-g contests during the Country Wine Arrair, or, partake in foolish antics and let the games begin at the Peasant Olympics. Choose your favorite and make history.
The 1994 festival will be held August 13th-September 25th.

Details:
The festival is open for seven consecutive weekends on Saturday, Sunday and Labor Day only. Hours are 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. every day. Admission at the gate is $11.95 for adults and $5.95 for children 5-12. Advance tickets can also be purchased for a discount price.
The festival grounds are located one mile north of Mt. Holly Inc. between Pontiac and Flint, (810) 645-9640.
