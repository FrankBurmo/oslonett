DOWNTOWN HOEDOWN

Background:
Billed as the world's largest free country music festival, the Downtown Hoedown takes place in late May on Detroit's Hart Plaza, attracting some of the top country music talent in the nation. Now entering its 12th year, the event attracts more than 800,000 country music fans annually.

What's Special:
Each year, an all-star country music line up takes to the Hart Plaza stage. Past performers include: Hank Williams, Dwight Yoakum, Garth Brooks, Crystal Gale, Tammy Wynette and the Charlie Daniels Band.

Details:
The 1994 Downtown Hoedown is scheduled for May 27-29. Admission is free. The event is produced by the Detroit Attractions, Inc., 22007 Woodward Avenue, Detroit, MI 48220, (313) 544-7450.
