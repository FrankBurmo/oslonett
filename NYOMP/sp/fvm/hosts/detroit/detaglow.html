DETROIT AGLOW

Background:

Detroit Aglow is the traditional lighting ceremony that begins the holiday season in downtown Detroit. Each year, on the Monday before Thanksgiving, thousands of holiday lights are turned on, lighting downtown's holiday decorations and welcoming the entire holiday season including Thanksgiving, Hanukkah, the Swedish Festival of Lights, Christmas and the New Year. Generally, the mayor or deputy mayor are given the honors of "throwing the switch."

What's Special:

In addition to the holiday lights, Detroit Aglow offers the carols and holiday music of the Detroit Sing-Along. One week after the Lighting Ceremony, thousands of people join together for an evening of old-fashioned sing-alongs with the Detroit Symphony Orchestra. Choirs and performers, families and children all gather at the Fox Theatre to participate in an evening of music and excitement. The Detroit Sing-Along will celebrate its 10th anniversary in 1994.

Extra Special:
For the past 35 years, the Central Business District Association, through its foundation, has sponsored a lighting program known as "Starring Detroit," where the Ambassador Bridge, churches, downtown statues, key landmarks and public areas have been permanently illuminated.
In 1994, the promenade of Hart Plaza, from the Veteran's building to the Mariners' Church is slated to receive 29 beautifying lights designed to enhance the visibility and security of Detroit's popular downtown destination.

Details:

Detroit Aglow takes place each year on the Monday before Thanksgiving. The Detroit Sing-Along takes place one week after the Lighting Ceremony. For more information, call (313) 961-1403.
