June/July Weather

 Palo Alto average summer high temperature is 77 degrees; the average low is 54 degrees. The high in June og '93was 98 degrees and in July, 86 degrees. Average rainfall for June is 0.13 inches and for July 0.01 inches. Daytime humidity averages from 59 to 76 percent. Winds prevail from the northwest. Palo Alto is usually warmer than San Francisco but cooler than San Jose.

Transportation

Bus

 San Mateo County Transit District buses go to San Francisco from Palo Alto and Santa Clara County buses go from Palo Alto to San Jose and cities in between. For more information, please call (800) 660-4287 / (415) 508-6472.

Transportation Agency (TA) will provide 15 minute bus service along El Camino Real between San Jose and Palo Alto, with extended trips to Menlo Park every 30 minutes. Other TA routes connect with both line 22 and CALTRAIN providing access to the stadium. For more information, please call (408) 321-2300.

Train

CALTRAIN: CALTRAIN runs between San Francisco and San Jose. It will be making a special stop at the Stanford Stadium Station for the World Cup games. The station is located within walking distance of the stadium. CalTrain offers a World Cup Transit Pass for unlimited travel on train, bus, light rail and cable cars for two and half weeks from June 20 through July 10, 1994. World Cup Transit Passes are $75 each. (800) 660-4827

Parks

 Palo Alto is known for its parks throughout the city. Larger Parks include Arastradero Preserve, Baylands, Nature Interpretive Center, Johnson Park, Mitchell Park, and Rinconada Park. For more information call 329-2261.

Shopping

 Claifornia Avenue
The original business district in Palo Alto, this area offers several blocks of hometown atmosphere, including a supermarket, a large health food store, cafes, book stores, financial institutions and many other shops and restaurants that serve both surrounding neighborhoods and visitors. Near the center of the city, with excellent access to Cal Train, California Avenue is a dynamic and diverse shopping disctrict.

Downtown Palo Alto
Six blocks of University Avenue and connecting streets in north Palo Alto are lined with shops that appeal to casual shoppers and entertainment seekers as well as local residents meeting thier daily needs. Three movie theatres, diverse restaurants, book stores, art galleries, coffee houses, bakeries, jewelery stores, and boutiques attract shoppers from a wide area at all times of the day and evening to downtown Palo Alto.

Stanford Shopping Center
This vibrant outdoor center just north of Stanford University includes department stores such as Macy's, Saks Fifth Avenue, Neiman Marcus, Nordstrom, Polo-Ralph Lauren, I. Magnin, Emporium, boutiques and restaurants, as well as one market section of the mall devoted to food stores, including a butcher, a produce market, and a large delicatessen. Over 140 extraordinary shops restaurants and services are nestled in the beautiful, open-air center.

Town and Country Village
This hacienda-style mall at the main intersection of Embarcadero and El Camino Real holds a supermarket, restaurants, boutiques and shops providing personal and household needs. From health food restaurants to unique jewelery stores, visitors and shoppers enjoy a variety of shops in the Village, all with plenty of store front parking.
