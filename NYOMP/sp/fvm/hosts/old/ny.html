Stadium Facts: Built in 1976, home of the New York Cosmos soccer team 1977-1984. Capacity 76,891. Giants Stadium is part of the Meadowlands Complex located in East Rutherford, New Jersey, approximately five miles from New York City.

June/July weather: average temperature is 74 degrees F/23.3 degrees C.

Attractions: New Jersey boasts attractions such as Atlantic City and Princeton University. Known as the Garden State, New Jersey is famous for its extensive small scale agricul ture. New York City is just across the Hudson River from Giants Stadium, approximately a five mile drive. Tourists can visit the Empire State Building, Statue of Liberty, United Nations, Times Square, Central Park, or go shopping and enjoy all there is to do in New York City!
