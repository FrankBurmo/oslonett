Population: Metropolitan area USA Rank: 2

Stadium Facts: Built in 1922, famous for the Rose Bowl game held annually on New Year's Day. Capacity 102,083. The Rose Bowl in Pasadena, CA is seven miles from downtown Los Angeles.

June/July weather: average temperature is 71 degrees F/21.7 degrees C.

Attractions: By 1910, Los Angeles was already well known as the motion picture capital of the world. The city has 46 miles of waterfront a superb highway system. Los Angeles was the home of the 1932 and 1984 Olympic Games. The charming town of Pasadena is famous for the annual Tournament of Roses Parade and the Rose Bowl game. The Rose Bowl will be the site of the 1994 World Cup Final Match.
