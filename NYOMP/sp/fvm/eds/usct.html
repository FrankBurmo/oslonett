U.S. Cycling Team

In a cooperative effort between EDS, GM, and the United States Cycling Federation (USCF), wind tunnel testing is supplying the sport of cycling with new technology focused on gold medals at the 1996 Olympics in Atlanta. EDS and the USCF have joined forces to streamline the USCF business operations, increase the exposure of the sport of cycling, and contribute resources to help athletes become more competitive.

As part of this four-year partnership, members of the U.S. Cycling Team participated in the sophisticated wind tunnel tests conducted at the General Motors Aerodynamic Laboratory in Warrren, Michigan. This testing is enhancing the competitiveness of the cyclists representing the United States in international competition.
