Team EDS

Team EDS is an international cycling team that not only demonstrates the caliber of EDS employees, but also serves as a business showcase by applying sports technology on the cycling track. By working with top manufacturers to develop and test the newest and most sophisticated aerodynamic equipment available, this championship team brings new technology to international competition.

The team's successes include medals at the U.S. and World Masters Championships, State and Regional Championships, World Qualifiers, Olympic Trials, and the Olympic Festival. Founded in 1988, the team consists of seven full-time employees whose jobs range from legal and marketing to engineering and fitness. The team's business and athletic success lies in their dedication, consummate quest for excellence, and EDS' advanced technological capabilities.
