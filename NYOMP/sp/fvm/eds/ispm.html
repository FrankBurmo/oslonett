Inventory, Staffing and Protocol Applications

The Inventory system provides complete inventory management for World Cup USA 1994. Information pertaining to World Cup's physical assets e.g. computers, office furniture, telecommunications equipment and merchandise may be entered into the database along with a digitized full-color photograph of the item. For World Cup merchandise available for sale, an item's sale price, stock-on-hand and reorder quantity may be tracked.

The Staffing system provides for the management of the tens of thousands of World Cup staff volunteers. Each volunteer is identified in the database, along with information about the person describing their work experience and skills, especially foreign-language skills. This information is useful in identifying the most qualified volunteers for those positions or assignments which require specialized knowledge, such as greeting or providing transportation services for a visiting foreign party.

The Protocol system assists in the support of visiting dignitaries who will be attending the World Cup games. VIP's are identified in the database along with their protocol requirements such as whether they require full or part-time escorts and what level of gift, if any, they may receive.
