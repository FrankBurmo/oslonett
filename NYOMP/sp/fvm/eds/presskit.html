Corporate Background

EDS

For 31 years, EDS has helped customers achieve their business objectives through the application of information technology. EDS has more than 70,000 employees worldwide and serves more than 8,000 customers. In 1993, EDS posted revenues of $8.5 billion.

EDS serves all major industries, from communications and insurance to manufacturing and finance. Customers range from small businesses to the world's largest corporations and governments. A 1991 Wall Street Journal report described EDS as "a classic story of productivity enhancement." Enhancing the productivity of our customers is the most important service we offer.

SCOPE


  • Founded in 1962, EDS became an independent subsidiary of General Motors in 1984.
  • EDS serves customers in more than 30 countries.
  • More than 15,000 EDS employees work outside the United States.

SERVICES


  • EDS provides a full range of services that includes:
    • Consulting
    • Systems Development
    • Systems Integration
    • Systems Management
    • Process Management

  • Customers are linked to EDS through EDS*NET, a global telecommunications and computing network.
  • By focusing on business needs, EDS helps its customers achieve measurable results -- from improving quality to gaining market share.
