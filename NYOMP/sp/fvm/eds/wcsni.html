Systems and Network Integration

EDS has installed and is managing the largest client/server network ever built for a single sporting event. Linking all nine World Cup venues, the network features:

  • Digital fiber-optic WAN connectivity provided by Sprint.

  • Approximately 1,000 workstations and servers provided by Sun Microsystems.

  • Fully redundant T-1 and fractional DS-3 backbone connectivity.

  • Access to stadiums, host hotels, venue offices, US Soccer Federation headquarters and FIFA headquarters in Zurich, Switzerland.

  • Integrated office management software tools for word-processing, spreadsheet, graphics, project management and scheduling.
