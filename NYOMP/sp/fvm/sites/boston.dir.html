Directions to the Boston Venue: Foxboro Stadium

FROM LOGAN INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT
Follow airport exit roadway and bear left to the Sumner Tunnel toll booths ($1). At the end of the tunnel, take a sharp right and quickly bear left under (Interstate 93). Proceed up the ramp onto (Interstate 93) South. Follow (Interstate 93) South to (Interstate 95) South, Exit 1. Take Exit 9 (Wrentham) onto (Route 1) South. Stadium is approximately 3 miles.

FROM BOSTON
Follow (Interstate 93) South to (Interstate 95) South, Exit 1. Take Exit 9 (Wrentham) onto (Route 1) South. Stadium is approximately 3 miles.

FROM CAPE COD
Take (Interstate 495) North to Exit 14A (Wrentham) onto (Route 1) North. Stadium is approximately 4 miles.

FROM MAINE, NEW HAMPSHIRE
Take (Interstate 495) South onto (Route 1). Stadium is approximately 3 miles.

FROM NORTHERN CONNECTICUT, VERMONT AND UPSTATE NEW YORK
Take (Interstate 90) East (Massachusetts Turnpike) to (Interstate 495) South. Take Exit 14A (Wrentham) onto (Route 1) North. Stadium is approximately 4 miles.

FROM SOUTHERN CONNECTICUT AND RHODE ISLAND
Take (Interstate 95) North to (Interstate 495) North. Take Exit 14A (Wrentham) onto (Route 1) North. Stadium is approximately 4 miles.
