Directions to the Washington D.C. Venue: R.F.K. Memorial Stadium

From the Northeast:
(Interstate 95) South to (495) East to Baltimore/Washington Parkway South. Exit Benning Road - Entrance to Lot 6 and Lot 7 to the left or turn left on Oklahoma Avenue, NE to access lots. OR: Exit East Capitol Street (Westbound) - Entrance to Lot 6 and Lot 7 on the right immediately after crossing bridge.

From the Northwest:
(Interstate 270) South to (495) East Baltimore/Washington Parkway South. Exit Benning Road - Entrance to Lot 6 and Lot 7 to the left or turn left on Oklahoma Avenue, NE to access lots. OR: Exit East Capitol Street (Westbound) - Entrance to Lot 6 and Lot 7 on the right immediately after crossing bridge.

From the West:
(Interstate 66) East to Roslyn, Exit SR110 South to (Interstate 395) North. Cross 14th Street Bridge, follow freeway to the end. (DO NOT take 3rd Street Interstate 395 North Exit) Access to Lot 8 is at end of freeway. OR: (Interstate 66) East, cross Theodore Roosevelt Bridge to Independence Avenue. Follow Independence Avenue to Stadium. Entrance to Lot 8 will be on the right.

From the South:
(Interstate 95) North to (Interstate 395) North. Cross 14th Street Bridge, follow freeway to end. (DO NOT take 3rd Street Interstae 395 North Exit) Access to Lot 8 is at end of freeway (Barney Circle).

From the East:
(SR50) West to Baltimore/Washington Parkway South. Exit Benning Road - Entrance to Lot 6 and Lot 7 to the left or turn left on Oklahoma Avenue, NE to access lots. OR: Exit East Capitol Street (Westbound) - Entrance to Lot 6 and Lot 7 on the right immediately after crossing bridge.
