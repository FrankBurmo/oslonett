Directions to the Detroit Venue: The Silverdome

From Chicago:
(Interstate 90) East to (Interstate 94) East to (Interstate 275) North to (Interstate 696) East to (M-24) (Telegraph Road) North to Square Lake Road East to first exit, Opdyke Road. At stop sign turn right, proceed to light, turn right. Silverdome 2.7 miles on left.

From Benton Harbor, Kalamazoo, Battle Creek, Jackson, Ann Arbor:
(Interstate 94) East to (Interstate 275) North to (Interstate 696) East to (M-24) (Telegraph Road) North to Square Lake Road East to first exit, Opdyke Road. At stop sign turn right, proceed to light, turn right. Silverdome is 2.7 miles on left.

From Muskegon, Grand Rapids, Lansing:
(Interstate 96) East to (Interstate 696) East to (M-24) (Telegraph Road) North to Square Lake Road East to first exit, Opdyke Road. At stop sign turn right, proceed to light, turn right. Silverdome is 2.7 miles on left. OR: (Interstate 96) to (M-59) East (through City of Pontiac) to Opdyke Road. Turn left at light. Silverdome on corner of Opdyke and M-59.

From Toledo, Monroe, Detroit Windsor:
(Interstate 75) North to (M-59 West). Take first exit, Opdyke Road. Turn left. Silverdome on corner of (M-59) and Opdyke Roads.

From Sarnia, Port Huron:
(Interstate 94) West to (M-59) West to Opdyke Road. Turn left. Silverdome on corner of (M-59) and Opdyke Roads.

From Bay City, Saginaw, Flint:
(Interstate 75) South to University Drive. Turn right at stop sign proceed to light. Turn left onto Opdyke Road. Silverdome is 1 mile on right.

From Detroit Metropolitan Airport:
(Interstate 94) West to (Interstate 275) North to (Interstate 696) East to (M-24) (Telegraph Road) North to Square Lake Road East to first exit, Opdyke Road. Turn right at stop sign, proceed to light, turn right. Silverdome is 1 mile on left. OR: (Interstate 94) East to (M-24) North (Telegraph Road) to Square Lake Road East to first exit, Opdyke Road. Turn right at stop sign, proceed to light turn right. Silverdome 2.7 miles on left. OR: (Interstate 94) East to (Interstate 75) North to (M-59) West to first exit, Opdyke Road. Turn right on Opdyke Road. Silverdome on corner of Opdyke and (M-59) Roads.
