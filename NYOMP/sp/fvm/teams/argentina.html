  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Ten
    • Record: 24-15-9
    • Best Result: Champions, 1978 and 1986.

    Argentina was introduced to soccer by the British in 1865. Soccer has continued to be the dominant sport there since that time.

    The Argentines were the forefathers of soccer in South America. They played the first full international game, outside of Britian, against Uruguay in 1901. Argentina remained a soccer powerhouse in South America until the late 1950's , yet its talent was never observed throughout the world. Frequently, Argentina would send its third-string teams to national events due to its fear of losing players to other nations. This practice made it difficult to consistently enter multinational events such as World Cup and the Olympics. When FIFA forbade players to side with more than one nation, Argentina's national teams became a dominant force internationally. They qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup on November 17, 1993, when they beat Australia, 1-0, in Buenos Aires.

    Argentina has won two World Cup tournaments. Its first victory came in 1978 when it hosted the World Cup, and the second was in 1986.
