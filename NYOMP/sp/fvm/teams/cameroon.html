  • World Cup History:
    • Previous Appearances: Two
    • Record: 3-2-3
    • Best Result: 1990: Lost to England in quarter-finals.

    Club soccer in Cameroon became prevalent in the 1940s. Since the 1980s though, Cameroon has focused most of its attention on its national team. By 1984, it was the pride of African soccer.

    Cameroon qualified for its first World Cup in 1982. Following that, it appeared in three consecutive African Nations Cup finals between 1984 and 1988. It won the Nations Cup twice during that time. Qualification for the 1994 FIFA World Cup came when Cameroon beat Zimbabwe, 3-1, on October 10, 1993, in Yaounde.

    The 1990 World Cup in Italy was the second time that Cameroon qualified for the tournament. This will be their third.
