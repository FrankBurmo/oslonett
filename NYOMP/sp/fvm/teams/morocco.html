    • Best Result: 1986: Lost to West Germany in second round.

    Soccer in Morocco can be traced all the way back to when the sport was first introduced into the region by French Colonists. Some of the clubs there became the centers for anti-colonial sentiment.

    Morocco was the first African country to play in the World Cup in 1970. It was absent from World Cup finals play until 1986 in Mexico. It reached the quarterfinals during that tournament which was a first for African football. Morocco qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup when it beat Zambia, 1-0, on October 10, 1993, in Casablanca.
