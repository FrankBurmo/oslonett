    In Greece, soccer remained low key until the second half of the 20th century. Greece's Football Federation was formed after 1926 and it was a short time thereafter that league soccer began to take shape, but a national league was not established until 1960.

    Since 1954, Greece has attempted to qualify for the World Cup finals. After many years of diligence and hard work, it will make its initial run in the World Cup competition. Greece was assured qualification for the 1994 FIFA World Cup ina 1-1 draw with Russia on May 23, 1993.
