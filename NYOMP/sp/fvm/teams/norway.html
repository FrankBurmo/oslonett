    • Best Result: 1938: Lost to Italy .

    Norway founded its first soccer club just before the turn of the century in 1894. The Norges Fotbaliforbund was founded in 1902. It began competition the same year.

    The first national team played against Sweden in 1908. Norway is traditionally the third strongest country in the Scandinavian competitions, however in the 1930s it won the 1920-1932 editions of the Scandinavian Championship. The last time Norway qualified for the World Cup finals was in the 1938 tournament in France. Norway's run through the 1994 qualification round saw them take points consistently and secured them a spot for the 1994 FIFA World Cup before its loss to Turkey on November 19, 1993.
