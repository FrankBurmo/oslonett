    • Best Result: 1986: Tied Bulgaria in first round.

    Korea was introduced to soccer in 1910 when the sport was brought to the peninsula by European technicians and missionaries. The Korean Football Federation was founded in 1938.

    Korea put together a national team shortly after its liberation in 1945. It played its first international games in 1948 and qualified for its first World Cup finals in 1954. South Korea remained a dominant force on its own continent, and once again qualified for the World Cup finals in 1986 and 1990. South Korea qualified for the 1994 FIFA World Cup when it beat North Korea, 3-0, on October 28, 1993, in Doha, Qatar.
