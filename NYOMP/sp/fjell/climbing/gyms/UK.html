Scotland

Glasgow Climbing Centre

Ibrox Church
Paisley Rd. West
Ibrox
Glasgow

How to get there:
by car: Take the Ibrox exit from the M8.
by public transport: 5 min walk from Ibrox tube.
Height:
15m.
Hours of opening:
10am-10pm weekdays, 10am-6pm weekends
Other comments:
There are about 50 routes of all grades most of which can be lead as well as toproped. This is a small bouldering wall.
