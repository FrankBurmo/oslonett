Matterhorn

Hoeksekade 141c
Berschenhoek
phone (0)1892-21092

This center is just north of Rotterdam in the west of Holland. At this climbing center it is possible to climb on the inside and the outside. The outside looks like the Matterhorn, therefore, the name. It is possible to lead most of the routes. The top is 34 meters high, 36 if you count the flagpost. The wall is constructed as a small moutain, so you can climb on all sides. When completely finished you will also be able to climb inside the mountain to a hight of 30 meters. At the moment about 2/3 is finished. The highest route is at the moment (dd 17 febr 1995) about 23m high, from which an overhang of 13m. Changing rooms etc. will also become available. There are routes all over the place. Mostly in the high 5 and 6 areas with a few 7's and a few 4's. Also one climb rated 3. There is a small plateau on one side for those who want to practice multi pitch climbing.

The routes are mainly build with plastic holds. But there is enough relief in the wall to use and there is even one 6a route build only using the relief. The concrete used for the wall is roughened enough for friction climbing. There are routes up to two pitches on the outside. On the inside this looks like an ordinary climbing gym. The total climbing area is about 2500 square meter. The price for one day climbing is about F16,-. Members of the KNAV pay fl 14,- guilders. It is open tuesday to sunday from 11.00h to 21.30h and monday from 17.00h to 21.30h.

Other comments:
The inside of the Materhorn will be open the 14th of januar 1995 (if they make it). At first instance they build it to 25m high.

Spaarnwoude

Spaarnwoude

This outdoor wall is a special one. This is also an piece of art up to 10m high, where they made prints of rocks in Belgium to structure it. The grades of the routes are from 5c and higher, except for a small section where they put in changeble grips. It is situated in the recreaction parc of Spaarnwoude near the ski center.

Yellowstone

Address:
Buijenstraat 12,
4705 RD Roosendaal
(at the Red Band sports area)
Phone: 01650-58426 or 01650-49702 (ask for Jac Jongeneelen)

Description:
a new building with about 40 ropes for 100 routes, and a bouldering area with a floor area of 30 square meters. The walls have relief.
Height / Climbing area:
about 800 square meters, one part of the gym is 18 meters high, the other part is about 10 meters high (this last part will be ready at the end of January 1995).
Opening times:
tuesday, thursday, friday: 17.00 - 22.00 hrs;
wednesday: 14.00 - 22.00 hrs;
saturday: 10.00 - 22.00 hrs;
sunday: 10.00 - 18.00 hrs;
monday: closed.
Entry fees:
fl 16.00 per person, groups of 10 persons onward fl 13.50 per person. Members of the NBV get 15% discount as an individual climber.
Renting climbing gear:
fl 5.00 per person (karabiner, eight, belt). Members of the NBV get 15% discount as an individual climber.
Subscriptions:
10 entrees cart fl 140.00;
quarterly ticket fl 175.00;
half year ticket fl 325.00;
year ticket fl 600.00.
Public transportation:
From the railway station it is a 20 minutes walk. Go out of the railway station and turn left ("Spoorstraat"). Turn left at the traffic lights. 200 meters after the next railway crossing the "Buijenstraat" is at your right hand side. Bus lines 33 and 103 (departing every half hour from the railway station) stop at this corner of the "Buijenstraat".
By car:
From Rotterdam / Dordrecht, follow the A-17, exit Roosendaal-Noord. Turn left at the end of the exit. 100 meters after the railway crossing the "Buijenstraat" is at your left hand side.
From Bergen op Zoom / Antwerp / Zeeland, follow the A-17, exit Oud-Gastel / Roosendaal-Noord. Turn right at the end of the exit. 100 meters after the railway crossing the "Buijenstraat" is at your left hand side. From Breda, follow the A-58, exit Roosendaal-Oost. Turn right at the end of the exit. Turn right at the traffic lights. Turn left at the second street. Turn left after the soccer stadium. Turn right at the traffic lights. 200 meters after the next railway crossing the "Buijenstraat" is at your right hand side.
