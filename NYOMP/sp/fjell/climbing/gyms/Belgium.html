Klimzaal Blok, New climbing dimension

Industriepark Terbekehof
Moerelei 153
2610 Wilrijk-Antwerpen
Phone: 32 (0)3 825 55 33

How to get there:
By car: from Antwerp, take the OLD highway to Brussels (A12). At the end of a large bridge take the first way out and the first road to the right. Turn left at the T-cross. This is the moerelei. After 1 km. you will see the climbing gym at your left side.
By public transport: Take a train to Antwerp central station. From there, you can take a bus (bus 17 or bus 25) to Wilrijk. From the center of Wilrijk to Klimzaal Blok it's about 3 km.
Height/ Climbing area: Klimzaal Blok is a typical bouldering gym. Heigh is up to 4.5 meter. Climbing surface is about 450 sq. meter.
Security is garanteed by 120 sq.meter of safety mats (thick!!! 40 cm!!)
Hours of opening:
Monday to friday: 14:00 to 23:00.
Saturday and sunday: 14:00 to 21:00
Other comments: Klimzaal Blok is first and till now the only bouldering gym in Belgium. Don't let the low height fool you: some complex routes have a lenght of ten meters. There is also a long traverse (50 m.) that goes around the gym (from 3 to 7b).
Prices: one pass: adults - 250 BFr., children - 150 BFr.. Ten pass: 2.200 BFr., 3 months: 3000 BFr., 1 year: 8.500 BFr. Group (at least 10 climbers): 100 BFr. / hour / person.
Shoe rental: 100 BFr.
Tel.: 32 (0)3 825 55 33
