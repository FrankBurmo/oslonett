Elbsandsteingebirge

Location:
Germany->Saxony->SE of Dresden-> area around the river Elbe.
Kind of Rock:
Sandstone
Area with hard Sandstone: Bielatal
Area with soft Sandstone: Rathener Gebiet
Height:
10m - 100m.
Season:
Climbing all the year, best is from April/May to last days of Sept. It is NOT allowed to climb within 24 hours after the Rain.
Bolted:
NONE!! There are no bolts, there are some Rings. The distance between 2 Rings MUST be over 3m. 3 rings must have a distance between 1st and 3rd Ring of 7.5m! It's only allowed to put rings in a NEW route WITHOUT rapelling. Bolts are NOT ALLOWED (because the are not useful because of the soft stone).
New routes MUST be discovered from the ground without aid. You are not allowed to use Friends, Nuts etc; You may only use Knots. For this you need a complete range of rope between 4mm to 12mm with a length of 1.65m (to hang the rope over your shoulder).
For a lead in Saxon Switzerland (other Name for the Elbsandtsteingebirge) you must have good nerves, because you often risk falls of 10 or more Meters.

MAGNESIA OR OTHERS ARE NOT ALLOWED!!

Climbing is only allowed on towers, that are higher than 10 m.

History:
Climbing started in 1864, when gymnasts climbed the Falkenstein, a 80-meters-tower. They climbed up a grade III (UIAA) way. In the 16th -18th cent. sometimes people climbed important towers, often using aid. In the beginning of the 20th century the rules of climbing in the Elbsandstein were defined. They include it that you may not use any aid. The only kind of aiding is climbing at the shoulders (head, hands...) of another climber. this other climber MUST keep himself not using any illegal aid. In the 1960s/70s/80s one man dominated the Elbsandstein: Bernd Arnold. Until now he made more than 600 routes, some of them were the most difficult to that time. He made the first 10a (9-) in 1982. In my opinion the first 9a (8-) of the world was climbed in the Elbsandstein in (January or February) 1965.
Comments:
There are 1099 towers at the Elbsandsteingebirge, with over 12000 (twelve- thousand!!) routes on it. There are several (>100) Persons in our area, who climbed all the towers.

Ith-Area

It is 40 km to the south of Hannover. Ith is one of the greatest climbing spots in germany. Most climbs are 20m long. Most routes are bolted, but you will need rocks too.
There are 5 areas:
Ith, Süntel, Kanstein, Selter and Heinberg.

Ith Area:

Main area with a German Alpin Club Camp Ground nearby. 5 min to walk to the climbs. Many Climber especially on weekends and easy TR-climbs. Expo. to SW.

Lüerdissen Area:

Climbs from UIAA 3-9, Windy drys fast after rain.

Holzen Area:

Climbs from UIAA 5-9, less easier routes, with hard boulder area "Heulboje" 30min from Campground or 1/4h by car. Midi crouwded.
Must do´s:
5 Verschneiungswand Eierkante

Bisperoder Area:

Not easy to find. 15m climbs from UIAA 3-8. Less Climber, because: 30min to walk + 1/4h by car.

Selter-Area

No easy climbs:
UIAA from 6-10.
Sportroute:
fingerpower and roofs. Less climbers, but good area if you climb 7 and more.

Hohenstein-Area:

Trad-routes UIAA 3-7 100m high.

Many Hannover climbers are there at the weekends. Try to contact them about the "Deutscher Alpen Verein Sektion Hannover".

Igel

Location: Igel - very easy to find, it's between Trier, Germany, and Wasserbillig, Luxembourg. When driving INTO Germany from Luxembourg look up to the left side of the valley, you'll see a band of white/grey limestone above a wine vineyard. That's Igel! It's probably about 5 kilometers from Trier.

Kind of Rock:Limestone

Height:10- 20 meters (?)

Season: Summer, Fall, Sping (but it's kind of rainy)

Bolted: YES, all sport climbs

Camping sites: Not really, maybe people could ask the farmer there if it's okay to camp. I've seen people camp there before..but I would ask first.

Topo: 2) NO topo for Igel, however, there might be one now in print. It's been awhile since I've been there. Try the climbing/camping store in Trier.

Grades: AMERICAN RATING (sorry): 5.4 to 5.13

The climbing at Igel is VERY good. The climbs aren't very high, however, the quality of the rock is VERY good! I highly recommend this area for people who are beginners!
