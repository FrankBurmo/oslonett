Rock Climbing Areas In Luxembourg

Berdorf

Location: About 10 kilometers Northwest of Echternacht. (I'll send you the exact locationat a later date, it's located near a small village, but I've forgotten it's name).

Kind of Rock: Very SOLID Sandstone.

Height: 20 - 40 meters.

Season: Summer, Fall, Sping (but it's kind of rainy)

Bolted: YES (actually, there are probably too many bolts. I never understood bolting cracks!)

Camping sites: YES, about a 5 min. hike away. Of course, it's commerical.

Topo: There is one that was printed in the 70's. It's very rare to find somebody who has this topo...best advice is to talk to the locals.

There is also an English topo with climbing in Belgium and Luxembourg lwith lots of info about luxembourg.

Grades: AMERICAN RATING (sorry): 5.4 to 5.13

Interesting Routes: Die Spinne (the spider). Very interesting crack to a fingery overhanging face. Unless your over 2m tall, you have to dyno for the last hold, while your perched on this overhanging face. Excellent Climb! By the way, don't expect to see the pocket your going to dyno for. 6+, A0 (french rating) (b) The Grand Face. Once again, a nice little crack that runs out into a fingery, slopey face. Easy to get 'sick' runout on this climb, saw my friend take a 20m fall, take flexi-friends! Rating unknown.

Other comments: Well, the locals probably won't like me for telling everyone about these places! Sorry, but we all share this place (earth), right?
