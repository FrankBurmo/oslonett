A Summernight's Dream

International Theatre Festival, Porsgrunn, June 4-11, 1995

Seminar information

During the A Summernight's Dream theatre festival, some of the leading acting teachers in Europe will conduct seminars.

Four seminars on acting, June 4-8 1995:

  1. Zygmund Molik (Pl): Voice and Body
  2. Cristina Castrillo (Ch): Memory
  3. Anna Zubrzycka og Gregorz Bral (Pl): The Living Actor
  4. Maria Cassi og Leonardo Brizzi (It): The Comedian's Song Workshop

Two seminars for children and young actors:

    June 6-8:   Stella Polaris (N):
                Tutorial on parades for young actors

    June 10:    Cirkus Sibylla (N):
                Circus School for Children
