The Oslo Card

With the Oslo Card you may get to know Oslo easily and at low cost.

All this is yours with the Oslo Card:

  • Free admission to most museums and sights.
  • Free "Mini-Cruise" with Båtservice Sightseeing
  • Free admission to Bjerke Travbane (horse trotting) and Øvrevoll Galoppbane (track-racing)
  • Free admission to TusenFryd Amusement Park.
  • Free admission to the "Tøyenbath", the indoor Swimming pool and the Frogner Public Bath.
  • Free parking at all municipal car parks in Oslo.
  • Unlimited free travel on all public transportation within the Oslo area.
  • 30% discount on return ticket by NSB to and from Oslo from anywhere in Norway.
  • Discount on tickets for cinemas (May, June, July), theatres, opera etc.
  • Special offers in shops.
  • Car rental discount.
  • Discount to the Ibsen underground car park.
  • Discount on Land Rover Safari trip in the Oslo forest area.
  • Discount on ski- and bicycle rental.
  • Discount on gas/diesel at Texaco and Hydro.
The Tourist Information Offices at Norway Information Centre and at the Central Station, at most hotels in Oslo's city centre, Trafikanten at Jernbanetorget, Narvesen Kiosks in the city centre, Camping sites in Oslo, some post offices in Oslo, many travel agencies and on most of the passenger ships calling at Oslo.
