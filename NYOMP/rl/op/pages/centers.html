Meeting facilities

Oslo is today a well equipped conference city, and has hotels and meeting facilities for up to 7.000 persons. Here we present some of the conventions centre in Oslo;

Gamle Logen

is situated on Grev Wedels Plass, surrounded by the Akershus Fortress right in the heart of Oslo. Gamle Logen is one of Oslo«s oldest cultural buildings and has undergone complete restoration and reopened in October 1988. Great efforts were made to preserve and restore the historic elegance and atmosphere of the building. Gamle Logen consists of 8 different halls, The main hall - Store Sal - is known for the quality of its acoustics and rich decor, and is frequently used for concerts. On the first floor there are 7 smaller halls. The varying sizes of the halls give flexibility and opportunity for experience and variation. Gamle Logen may be rented as a whole, or the various halls may be rented seperatly.

The Oslo Concert Hall

combines all the facilities of a modern multipurpose-hall with an inspiring atmosphere created by the world«s greates artists. Located in the heart of Oslo it is the ideal venue for conventions with up to 1400 delegates.

The Main Hall with 1400 seats is the ideal auditorium for your conventions. Here we will meet the challenge of staging your event with a highly professional, state-of-the-art technology. The technicians like their jobs. Our Small Hall with up to 266 seats offers all the flexibility you need. We can set up any kind of meeting, thanks to our flexible seating arrangement. You will enjoy working here. And you might even dance a late night's tango here!

Our foyers represent 400 square metres of potential exhibition space. The catering-department looks forward to serve you coffee, lunch and even a complete gala-dinner in elegant, relaxing surroundings after a hard day's work. The choice is yours, and we will make it easy!

BI Conference Centre Ekeberg

is a modern conference centre in a traditional frame. Located proudly in the hillsides east of the city, overlooking the fjord and the harbour, this old "Sailor's Academy" from 1917 is a unique memory of old times. Totally refurbished for school purposes in 1990, it offers a variety of state-of-the-art meeting rooms, from 3 to 300 persons. Easy access by car as well as by public transportation.
