Museums and Sights

Oslo offers more than 50 museums and galleries and has in relation to its population, more of these than most capitals. International attractions such as the Holmenkollen Ski jump, Vigelands Sculpture Park, the Munch Museum, the Kon-Tiki and the Polar-ship "Fram", the Viking Ships and the National Gallery, have important national and international collections. The new Museum of Contemporary Art at Bankplassen and the Astrup Fearnley Museum of Modern Art make Oslo even more exciting as an art city.
