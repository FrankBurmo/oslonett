Restaurants

Oslo might be on the top of the world. By checking the menus you would think we where at its hub. The range of cuisine's are truly cosmopolitan.

Of course you must try some of the specialities of the Norwegian country. And there's no getting round the national dish, "fårikål" - mutton and cabbage stew, or reindeer fillet - or moose steak. You'd have to be a bit more daring to order "lutefisk" (a kind of boiled cod "marinated" in lye) or "rakørret" (partially fermented trout). Recommended restaurants offering typical Norwegian cuisine's are: Kaffistova, Engebret, Det Gamle Raadhus, Stortorvets Gjæstegiveri, D/S Louise Restaurant and Hotel Stefan.

Ranked among the finer kitchens are Bagatelle in Bygdøy Allé. Fashionable gastronomic spots include d'Artagnan, Annen Etage, De Fem Stuer, Presidenten, Feinschmecker, Det Blå kjøkken, Blom and many others. Here you'll get exquisite dishes and excellent service.

Many Oslo restaurants have a proud history as meeting places for artists. The "Grand Café" was frequently visited by Henrik Ibsen, Edvard Munch and other well known artists. "Theatercaféen" is perhaps even more famous for its impressive clientele through the years - today too, the celebrities visit regularly. Blom is a third venue for the cultural elite. A unique atmosphere still fills these venerable rooms.

During the summer season there are idyllic out-of-town spots like "Herregårdskroen" and "Frognerparken Café", or perhaps you would rather book a table with a panoramic view at the "Holmenkollen" Restaurant.

Engebret

Engebret Café is named after Engebret Christoffersen, who founded the establishment in 1857. Due to its location next door to the Christiania Theatre, Engebret became the first "theatre café" in Oslo (then Christiania). It was regularly frequented by actors and both Ibsen and Bjørnson would drop in to Engebret after a show. The famous actress Johanne Dybwad was one of many regular visitors. These venerable premises, whose very walls reflect its history and create a unique atmosphere, have been faithfully restored by the present owners. The restaurant boasts a good selection of Norwegian and international specialities, and during the summer food is also served outdoors. For further information please contact
