Tour B.

INVITATION TO "THE FRIDTJOF NANSEN COMMEMORATIVE EXPEDITION" - SKIING ACROSS GREENLAND-

In 1994 Viking Spesialreiser arranged what has been called the world longest, coldest and toughest ski race across the mighty inland ice of Greenland. 18 persons from 6 different countries participated. In April/may 1996 we will arrange Fridtjof Nansens Commemorative Expeditions (650 km) along the same track.

Route

The Fridtjof Nansen Commemorative Race is scheduled to 24. April (dep. Oslo/Copenhagen)- 15-16. May 1996 (arr. Copenhagen/Oslo). The starting point of the race will be at Søndre Strømfjord(Kangerlussuaq) at the west-coast of Greenland. The first two days of the race, the trail passes through a beautiful land of reindeer and musk-oxes. The next two days the participants must force their way through a breath-taking area of crevasses on the western margin of the in-land ice. Most of the equipment and the snow-mobiles will be transported to the top of that icefall with helicopter. The next fourteen days will be spent across a cold and endless desert of snow and ice.

The final stage of the expedition will take place through the icefall and the alpine country to a small Eskimo-village in the vicinity of Ammassalik which is the only town on the east coast of Greenland. Dependent on the snow and ice conditions on the eastern and western margin of the glacier, it may be necessary to use helicopter transport for a short distance.

On the march

Four to six persons will be on the staff. The staff will be transported on snow-mobiles. Most of the equipment, tents and food will be transported on sledges. The trail will be marked by sticks with regular intervals (dependent on the weather). A snow-mobile will follow the hindmost skier collecting the sticks.

If a skier have problem to cover the days distance within the time appointed, he/she will be picked up and pulled behind a snow-mobile the last kilometers.

During the march, the skiers will have to carry a rucksack containing clothes, two rations of food, emergency-equipment, sleeping bag etc. Distances of about 40-55 km will be covered each day. In good weather, the skiers will be allowed to set their own pace after a common start in the morning. In case of bad weather (storm or heavy snowfall) the skiers will walk together as a group, and, if necessary stay tied together with a rope.

Necessary qualifications of the competitors

The skiers must document and affirm excellent physical shape and skiing skills and that they are familiar with outdoor life in extreme climate.

Insurance and guarantee against the Danish/Greenland`s government

Viking Spesialreiser gives a search and rescue security of at least 1.000 000,- NOK (appr. US$ 140 000,) against the Danish State for the group, and an ambulance-transportation-insurance of 300 000 NOK

(appr US$ 35.000,-) per person.

Registration and price.
