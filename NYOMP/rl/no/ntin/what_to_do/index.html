Climbing guide to Norway
Almost all climbing in Norway is on crystalline rock. Gneisses and gabbros predominate, though numerous granite wallsare located in southern Norway (Valle, Nisser/Fyresdal, Andersnatten), Jotunheimen (Hurrungane) and northern Norway (Tysfjord and Kvaløya).
