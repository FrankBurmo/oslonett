Norwegian Wildlife & Rafting AS

Norwegian Wildlife & Rafting AS (NWR) is Scandinavias oldest, largest and most experienced rafting company, established in 1984. NWR have through the years established the best of knowledge, safety, equipment, service and experience. With our help you will experience exiting adventures, and fantastic scenery.
We are situated in idyllic Randsverk, accessible from both east and west Norway.
During the last 10 years NWR have made rafting on the Sjoa river a popular adventure. Our skillfull guides have wide experience from the most demanding rivers in the world, and they are pioneers on the most challenging rivers in Norway. NWR have many references from guests who have taken part in our adventures.

We wish you welcome as our guest!

Meeting point:


Meet at NWR Raftingcenter in Randsverk. This is 300 km north of Oslo.

How to get there:

CAR FROM THE SOUTH/EAST (OSLO)
Follow E6 north to exit Sjoa. Make a left and follow R257. Randsverk is 20 km up this road.

CAR FROM THE WEST (BERGEN)
Drive to Beitostølen and continue on R51. This takes you over Valdresflya, down the Sjoa Valley and straight to Randsverk.

TRAIN
There are trains running daily from Oslo and Trondheim to Otta. NWR will assist you with transportation from Otta to Randsverk.

BUS
There are buses running daily from Oslo and Bergen to Randsverk.

AIRPLANE
Fly to Fagernes Airport. NWR will help you with transportation from the airport to Randsverk.

CHARTERBUS
NWR's two buses can provide bus-transportation for bigger groups at any time.

Please feel free to call NWR for help or advice.
