About Norwegian Travel Information Network (NTIN)

Norwegian Travel Information Network is an effort initiated by Oslonett, Inc. and Arctic Adventours, Inc.

Goal

NTIN aims to establish a virtual network of information of potential interest to travelers and tourists visiting Norway. NTIN will be the missing link which glues together already existing information in the field, but a huge amount of new information has to be brought online and dynamically updated and maintained. NTIN will provide the technical expertise and infrastructure to do all this.

Note that NTIN will cover all Norway!

Time Schedule

NTIN will never be "finished", since new areas will be dynamically added, old areas will be updated and new information providers will be brought online.

But NTIN will be useful from day #1, and the plan is to have a some information online by Q4-94. We guarantee, however, that at the time you are reading this, some information is in place already - start browsing from the NTIN Home Page and see for yourself.

Business strategy

Revenues in NTIN will mainly come from information providers which actually buy a point of presence within NTIN. Even information providers who have their own network based information services will probably want to buy a spot, since NTIN aims to be the main network based Norwegian point-of-presence for companies selling into this important and huge market.

Information providers

There are many potential business areas and markets of possible interest to tourists in Norway, and the most evident ones are:
  • Hotels, motels, hostels
  • Museums, exhibitions etc.
  • Restaurants
  • Travel bureaus, tour operators
  • Regional tourist information offices
  • Transporters (bus, train, plane, ferries, ..)
  • Other service providers
  • Misc. shops

Readers and Availability

Please note that all information within NTIN will be free to the whole global Internet! However, we foresee situations where limitations may exist regarding reuse or resale of the information we bring you. Limitations may for instance stem from copyright issues on images and text made available by third parties.

Technical Implementation

NTIN will be realized using the Internet and World Wide Web, and Oslonett Inc. was selected as the company responsible for the technical implementation. Oslonett is the focal point for World Wide Web activities in Norway, and was nominated as a candidate to "Best Commercial WWW Site" in the contest "Best of the Web '94". Oslonett is also known as the company who brought The Winter Olympics to the Internet and World Wide Web.
