Navigational aids in Norwegian Travel Information Network - NTIN

To be able to navigate around in NTIN and to decide whether you are within NTIN or not, you will find navigational icons at the bottom of most NTIN information pages. Getting familiar with these icons, will enable you to quickly get at information and switch information view in NTIN.
This is the map-icon. Clicking it, will give you a map of Norway where you can selecet different geographical areas you are interested in.

This town-icon will give you a list of towns in Norway which have their own information pages.

The NTIN home-page icon will take you to the top of the NTIN information hierarchy.

The National view-icon. Click this icon when you want to go the information page which gives you general information on Norway.

This is the search-icon. Clicking it, will let you type in topics and words to search for within the NTIN information base. Searches may be context dependent.
