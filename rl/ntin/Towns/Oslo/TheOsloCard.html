Norwegian Travel Information Network: The Oslo Card

Get to know Oslo easily and unexpensively with the Oslo card. With the Card you get the following advantages:

Free transport

Unlimited free travel on all forms of public transport run by Oslo Sporveier, Stor-Oslo Lokaltrafikk and four zones of NSB's (Norwegian State Railways) local railway network within Oslo (this includes buses, trams, the underground, trains and boats).

Free Parking

in all municipal parking lots and car parks. Parking is otherwise expensive in Oslo! to most museums, the swimming pools at T|yenbadet, Frognerbadet, the Tusenfryd amusement park (tivoli), Bjerke horsetrotting and \vrevoll trackracing. Free "Mini-Cruise" with B}tservice sightseeing.

Discounts

on cinema tickets (in May, June and July), car hire, parking in the Ibsen P-Hus car park, Marka Safari trips, sightseeing bus tours and ski- and bike hiring. Rebate on gas/petrol and diesel. NOK 0.30 in discountper litre on all Texaco gas stations in Norway during 1994-95.

30% Discount

on adult return tickets with NSB to and from Oslo from anywhere in Norway. This discount is given for the adult 3-Day Oslo Card only.

You can buy the Oslo Card at:

  • most hotels in Oslo
  • Travel Information Offices at Oslo Central Station and the "Norway Information Centre"
  • camping sites in Oslo
  • "TRAFIKKANTEN" (bus- and tram information) at Jernbanetorget
  • Narvesen shops and kiosks in the centre of Oslo
  • Aker Brygge (by the main harbour)
  • Ibsen P-Hus (car park)
  • post offices and gas stations in Oslo
