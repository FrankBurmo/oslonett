Finding your way around in Norwegian Travel Information Network (NTIN)

This guide will hopefully help you to find your way around in NTIN, and to get at information quick. To be able to do this, it is important that you get a quick grasp on the architechture of NTIN and what information you can expect to find within NTIN.

Background

The first thing you should have in mind, is that NTIN is not one big computer or one big database residing on one computer.

NTIN is a network of information.

This implies that information may be spread out on different computers which may be geographically apart, but connected by a network - in this case the Internet and the World Wide Web framework. This is indeed true, and in some cases information resides on computers elsewhere in Norway, and even at the other end of the globe!

Those computers are not controlled by NTIN's operational staff, but have some information of potential interest to you, so we let you get at this information gracefully and transparently from within NTIN. Note however, that we can not guarantee for the quality of this information. Also, there is a possibility that you may travel way out of NTIN by following such links! However, in such cases you should be able "to turn your car" and find your way back again by issuing special commands to your computer software (look for commands called "Back" or "History").

Some external links to relevant information are only useful while we are building NTIN. These links will be substituted by our own information after a while.

Conventions

To let you easily see whether you are within NTIN's central information base or at some remote information resource elsewhere in the world, each page of information within NTIN has a special layout, but there are different types of information pages reflecting the different types of information we bring you.

You should look out for our navigational aids at the bottom of most pages. These icons will tell you that you are reading an information page within NTIN.
