Tour A. Tour on the ice cap

The mighty ice cap of Greenland has in hundreds of years fascinated adventures and explorers. In the past century one thought that in the middle of the glacier there were lush pastures and fertile oases. But the legendary FRIDTJOF NANSEN disproved this in 1888 when he crossed Greenland and found only snow, ice and coldness. Now you also have the possibility to experience the unique never ending flat snowland witch Nansen described after his expedition. A support team with experience from several crossings over the ice cap will give necessary assistance and will take care of the safety for both the 9-10 days long journey on the glacier and through the adventurous and tremendous glacier fall.

Day by day:

1 Fly to Kangerlussuaq (Søndre Strømfjord). Preparations, packing of equipment, instructions, etc.

2 By foot or with skis through the valley of Sandfluktdalen (easy day)

3 To the glacier front. Instructions in glacier hiking. (easy day)

4 Through the ice fall - a long and hard day. We arrive at base camp.

5-9 200 km to the abandoned base DYE 2. We reopen the kitchen and the bar, and arrange Polar Evening inside the base.

10-13 Return to the base camp

14 Helicopter back to Kangerlussuaq. A fantastic tour over the ice fall and through the valley. Banquet

15 Optional activities. We recommend the following:

  • Tour on ski/by foot to see muskoxes and reindeers.
  • Dogsledge tour.
  • Rent `snow mobile/tricycle.
  • Go on a fishing/boat tour on the fjord.
  • Go 1 1/2 day to Nuuk (Godthåp)

16 Return to Copenhagen / Oslo

Price list and terms of travel

Participant must have experience from camping in the winter, and have reasonable level of skiing profiency. However, you can choose if you wish to go the whole tour on skis or combine skiing with snow scootering. You can for example chose to go on skis the whole tour to Dye, and on the way back you can drive a snow scooter yourself one day, skijoring the two next days and tackle the skis again the last day. Those who wish to go all the days on skis will not become disturbed by snow scooters - because if the weather permits, you will follow a marked ski track which the first scooter leaves in the morning. The other scooter follows quite a while after the last skier.
