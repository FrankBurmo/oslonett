Hovden Mountain Resort
Hovden Høyfjellshotell is located m the middle of Southern Norway - on top of Setesdal, 830 m above sea level. Great for skiing.

Nidelva
Nidelva is one of southern Norways largest rivers, with both salmon and trout fishing.
Audna river
Audna is an attractive river for salmon and sea-trout fishing
Otra river
Otra is a fairly large river for this part of the country. From the sources in the upper reaches of Setesdal the water travels a good 240 kilometres before meeting the sea at the center of Kristiansand.
Storelva
Storelva is one of southern Norways hidden secrets, uncrowded, but with a good run of sea-trout.
