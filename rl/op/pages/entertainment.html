Forklore

The Folk Dance Group of the Young Farmers Union (Bondeungdomslaget) in Oslo, gives dance performances in Oslo Concert Hall, Munkedamsvn. 14, N-0270 Oslo, on Mondays and Thursdays at 9 p.m. during July and August.

The Norwegian Folk Museum at Bygdøy presents folk dance performances by its own folk dance ensemble at the museum's open air theatre during summer. See daily press and "What's on in Oslo" - for details.

Cinemas

Oslo has one of Europe's best cinema selections with 30 screens. Foreign films are shown in the original language versions with Norwegian subtitles. The Oslo daily press gives details of titles, times and tickets. Discount with the Oslo Card during June, July and August.

Tusenfryd Amusement Park (Norgesparken TusenFryd)

Amusement park for the entire family. Roller coaster with loop and cork screw, flumride, enterprise, bumper cars, mini-golf, cinema 180, amfiscene, restaurants, kiosks, shops etc. 20 km south of Central Station, close to E6 and E18. Free bus from Oslo Sentralstation (Seaside).

Special prices for groups. Free entrance with the Oslo-Card.

"Land of the Vikings" (Viking Land)
Spring 1995 will a new theme park open - "Land of the Vikings" Here you will be able to leave the twentieth century and journey 1000 years back in time.

You will experience a living Viking community with a busy Kaupang with craftworkers and bartering - shipbuilding the Viking way and preparation for the famous voyages - a burial mound where you will see a chieftain equipped for his final journey.

Walk on, and you will come to Leikvollen, with archery and other sports and games for children. Tingplassen will be site of Viking Age pageants. "Ravnejenta", the Raven girl, is a medicine woman and she will show you round her cottage, and how large and small ills were cured. At Storgarden, tne Manor, you will see how the Vikings lived and survived in peacetiem. The Earl will probably show you around the farm, and invite you into Snorrestova afterwards.

The dramatic climax will be Toktet, the Expedition. In a cave on the outskirts of VikingLandet we have discovered a huge Viking ship which was the victim of the wrath of the Gods, one stormy winter night, more than a thousand years ago. You have been fortunate enough to be chosen to join the crew, one of the greatest honors a Viking could aspire to! It is a very realistic reconstruction of a real Viking expedition. Among the wonders you will experience on this perilous voyage are a storm, attacks from other Viking ships, the mystery of the Viking Gods, and the discovery of a new continent. Fortunately, the helmsman has had 1000 years experience, and has a steady hand, and alsways brings you safely back to your home port at VikingLandet.
