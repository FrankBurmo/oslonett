Museums and sights in Oslo

Museums and Sights
in Oslo

Akershus castle and fortress

Akershus Slott, Oslo Mil./Akershus,
N-0015 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 40 36 55 - 22 41 25 21

A unique fortress and medieval castle built 1299. Rebuilt as a renaissance palace in the 17th century. The castle, with its beautiful halls, is open to the public from May to October.

Museum of Applied Art

St. Olavsgt. 1,
N-0165 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 20 35 78,
fax. +(47) 22 11 39 71

Norwegian and foreign applied art from the Middle Ages to present. The Baldishol Tapestry from 1180. Scandinavian Design, Royal Costumes. 18th century interior cafeteria (from the Solli Løkke).

Architecture, The Norwegian Museum of

Josefinesgt. 34,
N-0351 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 60 22 90,
fax +(47) 22 69 59 48.

Situated in a residential area from the 1850s, the museum has changing exhibitions and a special Library.

Armed Forces Museum, The Norwegian

Oslo Mil./Akershus Fortress, bygning 62,
N-0015 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 40 35 82,

Norwegian Military History from 1500 until 1950. Cafeteria.

Astrup Fearnley Museum of Modern Art

Grev Wedels Plass 9,
N-0107 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 93 60 60,
fax +(47) 22 93 60 65

Changing exhibitions of Norwegian and foreign art from the post-war period to our days. The items come from the museums own collections, from 4 private collections and from other origins in connection with special exhibitions. Guided visits can be arranged.

Children Art Museum, The International

Lille Frøensv. 4,
N-0369 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 46 85 73
fax. +(47) 22 69 29 10

Children's art (drawings, paintings, ceramics, sculpture, tapestry and handicrafts) collected from more than 130 countries. Children's workshop.

The Model of the city of Christiania

Høymagasinet, Akershus Fortress,
N-0015 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 33 31 47.

The exhibition shows a big model of the city of Christiania (Oslo), and is part of a multi-media program depicting the history of Christiania from its foundation in 1624 until 1840.

The City Hall

Oslo City Hall,
N-0037 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 86 16 00,
fax. +(47) 22 86 16 30

The City Hall is the seat of the city's political and administrative management. Inaugurated in 1950 (Oslo's 900th anniversary). Rich decorations include magnificent frescoes by some of Norway's most prominent artists. (The worlds largest fresco painting.)

City Museum and Frogner Manor

Frognerveien 67 (Vigeland Park),
N-0266 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 43 06 45,
fax +(47)22434891

The City Museum is open all year round. Its interior preserves the atmosphere of the epoch. A new wing presents the history of Oslo (models, prints, 45000 photos, 20000 books, etc.). The Old Frogner Manor from 1790 is open only in the summer season. Cafeteria.

Customs Museum, The Norwegian

Tollbugt. 1a,
N-0152 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 86 09 99

Exhibition of original items and models concerning import and export to and from Norway and the Old Customs House.

Ethnographic Museum

Frederiksgt. 2,
N-0164 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 41 63 00, ext. 935,

An important collection of items from Norwegian antiquity, Viking and Medieval age. The Viking age room shows the background of the discovery of the Viking ships which are exhibited at the Vikingships Museum at Bygdøy.

Folk Museum, The Norwegian

Museumsvn. 10 (Bygdøy),
N-0287 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 43 70 20,
fax. +(47) 22 43 18 28.

170 buildings from all over Norway. Farmsteads. A Stave church from 1200. Urban collection of houses from the 18th and 19th centuries. Indoor collections: Urban and Rural cultures, Ecclesiastical art, Sami (Lapp) section, Norwegian Pharmaceutical Museum. Restaurant and cafeteria.

Fram Museum

Bygdøynes,
N-0286 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 43 83 70 or 22 12 36 50/67 54 01 44

Polar exploration ship "Fram". Constructed by Colin Archer in 1892. Used by Fridtjof Nansen (1893-96, North Pole Expedition) and Roald Amundsen (1910-12 South Pole expedition).

Historical Museum

Fredriksgt. 2,
N-0164 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 41 63 00.

The University's historical exhibitions present the Norwegian prehistory , the Viking age and the middle ages through many artifacts. The University's coin collection is a selection of coins and other means of payment from Norway and a presentation of the worlds numismatic history from the 7th century to present.

Ibsen Museum

Arbinsgate 1,
N-0253 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 55 20 09

Henrik Ibsen's apartment in Oslo from 1895 until his death in 1906. His study has the original furniture. The whole apartment has been entirely restored so that decorations, inventory and even colours are as similar to the original as possible.

Kon-Tiki Museum

Bygdøynesvn. 36, N-0286 Oslo, tel.: +(47) 22 43 80 50,
fax. +(47) 22 44 50 85.

The Kon-Tiki museum displays crafts and objects from Thor Heyerdahl's various expeditions: the Kon-Tiki raft (1947), monumental statues from the Easter Islands (1955), the raft Ra II (1970) , a model of the reed boat Tigris (1977), and showcases with finds from Peru and Polynesia.

Maritime Museum, Norwegian

Bygdøynesv. 37, N-0286 Oslo, tel.: +(47) 22 43 82 40,
fax. +(47) 22 56 20 37.

Coastal and maritime Norway is vividly presented by original sailing vessels, traditional craft, ship interiors and models, paintings and antiques. Restaurant and cafeteria.

Munch Museum

Tøyengt. 53, N-0578 Oslo, tel.: +(47) 22 67 37 74,
fax. +(47) 22 67 33 41.

The painter Edvard Munch's bequest to the City of Oslo. Opened 1963. It represents, together with gifts later received by the museum and through art exchanges, a total of 1100 paintings, 18000 prints as well as drawings, printing plates, letters etc. Concerts are held regularly in the museum. Cafeteria.

National Gallery

Universitetsgt. 13,
N-0164 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 20 04 04,
fax. +(47) 22 36 11 32,

Norway's largest public collection of paintings, drawings, sculptures and graphic art.

National Museum of Contemporary Art

Bankplassen 4,
N-0034 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 33 58 20,
fax +(47) 22 33 57 90

Based on post-war collections from the National Gallery. Approx. 1400 Paintings, 1000 Graphic Works, 340 Drawings, 425 sculptures. The Museum also presents artist books, photos, installations, collage and textile works. Placed in a monumental building. Cafeteria.

The Natural History Museums

Sarsgt. 1, N-0562 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 85 16 00,
fax. +(47) 22 57 14 37.

Botanical Garden and Museum. Conservatories, Mineralogical/Geological Museum, Paleontological Museum, Zoological Museum.

Oslo Cathedral

Stortorget 1,
N-0154 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 41 27 93

Our Saviours Church was consecrated in 1697. Baroque altar piece and pulpit from 1699. Baroque organ facade from the 1720's. Lavishly decorated ceiling from the period 1930-1955.

Resistance Museum

Akershus Fortress,
N-0015 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 40 31 38,
fax. +(47) 22 40 31 37

The museum presents a picture of the dramatic events in Norway during the German occupation 1940-45 : the invasion, occupation and liberation of Norway.

Postal Museum

Tollbugt. 17,
N-0152 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 40 80 59.

The museum has the country's largest stamp collection. Exhibits, documents and pictures illustrate the nearly 350 year old postal and philatelic history of Norway.

Science & Industry, Norwegian Museum of

Kjelsåsv. 143,
N-0491 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 22 25 50,
fax. +(47) 22 22 29 50.

The Norwegian Museum of Science and Technology. Energy, Industry and Communication collections. The "Teknoteket" is a place for discovery and experimenting in fields of science. The library with its collections of books and photos is open for the public. Cafeteria.

Ski museum/Holmenkollen ski jump

Kongevn. 5,
N-0390 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 92 32 00,
fax. +(47) 22 92 32 50.

The worlds oldest ski museum showing the development of skiing in Norway. Some of Nansens and Amundsens polar equipment. Located in the Holmenkollen Ski jump. From the top of the ski jump (elevator) there is a magnificent view. Situated 450 m/1350 feet above sea-level.

Vigeland Museum

Nobelsgt. 2,
N-0268 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 44 11 36,
fax. +(47) 22 44 65 37.

Built in 1920 as Gustav Vigelands residence, studio and workshop from 1922-1943. The museum contains nearly all his works : 1800 sculptures in bronze, marble, granite and plaster. 1200 drawings and 420 woodcuts. The museum is situated next door to the Vigeland Sculpture Park.

Vigeland Sculpture Park

Frogner,
N-0268 Oslo ,(entree. from Kirkeveien)
tel.: +(47) 22 55 28 84.

Gustav Vigeland designed the central part of the park (80 acres) and made the models of all the sculptures in bronze, granite and wrought iron, including the bridge sculptures, the Fountain with the Life Cycle and the Monolith. 192 sculptures totalling 650 figures.

Emanuel Vigeland Museum

Grimelundsv. 8,
N-0387 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 14 93 42.

Exhibit house and Fresco painting by Emanuel Vigeland (Gustav Vigelands brother). Open on Sundays only.

Viking Ships Museum

Huk Avenue 35,
N-0287 Oslo,
tel.: +(47) 22 43 83 79,
fax. +(47) 22 41 10 12 (Bygdøy)

The three Viking ships : Gokstad, Oseberg and Tune, and impressive finds from graves of Viking chiefs in the Oslofjord area. Small rowing boats, sledges and a richly decorated carriage, tools, harnesses, textiles and kitchen utensils.
