Suggestions for Coach Excursions

Oslo Highlights

These suggestions are to be combined with a short city tour.

6 alternative 3 hrs. programs (starting from the city centre):

  1. Vigeland Sculpture Park, Holmenkollen Ski jump, Viking Ships Museum.
  2. Vigeland Sculpture Park, Viking Ships Museum, Polar Ship "Fram" or Kon-Tiki.
  3. Vigeland Sculpture Park, Viking Ship Museum, City Hall or National Gallery.
  4. Folk Museum, Viking Ship Museum, Polar Ship "Fram" or Kon-Tiki.
  5. Vigeland Sculpture Park, Akershus Castle or Resistance Museum, City Hall.
  6. Vigeland Sculpture Park, Holmenkollen Ski jump, City Hall or National Gallery. The Cathedral of Oslo may substitute one of the museums.

6 alternative 4 hrs programs (starting from the city centre):

  1. Vigeland Sculpture Park, Holmenkollen and the Ski Museum, Viking Ship Museum, Polar Ship Fram or Kon-Tiki.
  2. Vigeland sculpture Park, Viking Ship Museum, Maritime Museum (boathall only) and Folk Museum.
  3. Akershus Castle or Resistance Museum, City Hall, Folk Museum.
  4. Museum of Applied Art, City Hall or National Gallery. Lunch at e.g. Bygdøystuene or Najaden. Folk Museum including indoor collections. Viking Ship Museum.
  5. Munch Museum, Vigeland Park or City Hall and Folk Museum.
  6. Munch Museum, Vigeland Museum and Folk Museum.

This is only a selection of the many possible combinations.

  • A visit to the Munch Museum is calculated to take a minimum of 1 hour.
  • A visit to the Folk Museum is calculated to take a minimum of 1 1/2 hours. (The Stave Church - Telemark Farmhouse - Setesdal Farmhouse - Sami Exhibition or another indoor collection).

Suggestion for Art Programs - see under Theme Tours/Special Tours.

Suggestion for children:

  • Norwegian Museum for Science & Technology
  • The Ski Museum/Ski Jump or the Tryvann Observation tower
  • Museum of Childrens' Art
  • Zoological Museum
  • Tusenfryd Amusement Park and Viking Land

Theme & Special Tours in Oslo

Oslo's Three Periods
The Old Town - Christiania - Modern Oslo

Architecture
From old wooden houses to modern buildings

Aker River Environment Park
9 kms - 20 waterfalls - combined bus/wandering - perhaps a visit to the Museum of Science & Technology.

Maritime Tour
Oslo Harbour. Norwegian Maritime Museum (boat hall), Polar Ship "Fram"/ Kon-Tiki Museum/Viking Ship Museum.

Viking Age and Middle Ages
Akershus Castle and Fortress/Historical Museum/Viking Ship Museum/Old Aker Church/Ladegården Manor.

Island Tour
By boat to Hovedøya Island with the ruins of the old medieval monastery.

Oslo in War & Peace
Akershus Castle, Norwegian Resistance Museum, Armed Forces Museum.

"Art in Oslo"
1. Folk Art & Applied Art from Viking Age to present day The Folk Museum, Viking Ship Museum, Museum of Applied Art, combined with visits to homecraft stores : Husfliden - Heimen, Norway Designs-David Andersen.

2. Norwegian Art Munch Museum, Oslo City Hall, National Gallery, Vigeland Park / Museum.

3. Modern Art Museum of Contemporary Art, Henie-Onstad Art Centre. The Artists Association/art galleries.

In Henrik Ibsen and Edvard Munchs Footsteps
  1. In Henrik Ibsen's and Edvard Munch's Footsteps Tour to places where the two lived and worked while living in Christiania. Visit to the Ibsen Museum (his last home), Oslo City Museum, The National Theatre, Munch Museum and Cemetery of Honour.
  2. Walking tour in Ibsen's Footsteps 2 hours tour : The National Theatre, the Ibsen Museum and Grand Café.

Nordmarka (The Oslo forest)
Land Rover Safari/Sleigh Ride/Wandering in the forest on skis or by foot, with visit to the Ski Museum. Lunch in one of the Ski Society's cabins or a "rucksack lunch".

In Fridtjof Nansen and Roald Amundsens footsteps
Combined coach and boat tour. Visits to Polhøgda (Nansens Home) and Svartskog (Amundsens Home) and visits to the Oslo ski museum, to the Polar Ship Fram and the Maritime Museum.
