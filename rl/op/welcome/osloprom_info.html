Oslo Promotion A/S

is a limited company with shareholders from the city's travel trade and commerce. Oslo Promotion A/S is the Visitors and Convention Bureau for Oslo, and thus serves as the city's tourist marketing and service institution.

Oslo Promotion produces and distributes the "Oslo Card" as well as the Oslo Package, the latter being a package sold by travel agencies.

Oslo Promotion has the following divisions : Convention Bureau, Tourism, Marketing, Public Relations, Tourist Information Centres, Oslo Package and the Oslo Card.
