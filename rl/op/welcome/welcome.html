Welcome to Oslo!

Oslo -- the capital of Norway -- is a city undergoing rapid development. Oslo is one of the main cultural centres in the north, and a popular meeting place for national and international activities. Events in the fields of art, culture, science, economics and international sports have made the city a more dynamic and colourful capital. Oslo has become a city that offers a lot of variety.

Oslo is widely known as the big-hearted city. Many would add that Oslo has woken up and become one of the most exciting and entertaining capitals in Scandinavia. Oslo is surrounded by magnificent scenery with the fjord on one side, and forested hills encircling the city. Together these offer outdoor recreational opportunities with which no other capital in the world can compare. In addition Oslo has a wide variety of famous museums and attractions, like the Viking Ship Museum, the Munch Museum, the Kon-Tiki and Ra Museum,Ski Museum/Holmenkollen Ski jump, the Polar Ship Fram Museum,Vigeland Sculpture Park just to mention a few.

There are so many things to do and to see in Oslo - you'll just have to stay another day!
