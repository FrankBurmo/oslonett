Vinterhaven

Med sine palmer og Chesterfield møbler er vinterhaven som en liten oase i hotellets lobby. Smørbrød, kaker, små varmretter og forfriskninger serveres hver dag.
Hyggepianist fra kl 11.30.

The Winter Garden with its palms and Chesterfield sofas, is an oasis of calm in the center of the hotel lobby. Enjoy open sandwiches, a delicious selection of cakes, coffee, tea or just a drink. From 11:30 a.m. our pianist provides the music.
