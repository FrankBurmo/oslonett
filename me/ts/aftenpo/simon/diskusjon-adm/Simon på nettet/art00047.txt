It makes it clear that the problem with many young Norwegians<br>is even more serious than I would have thought.<br>  For the past quarter century, I have watched the disappearance of<br>courtesy from Norwegian life. One of the forms it takes is the behavior<br>of young people on the trikk.<br>  For a long time, I felt compelled to point out to those who put their<br>feet on the seat or pushed ahead of elderly people when boarding the<br>trikk what they were doing.
