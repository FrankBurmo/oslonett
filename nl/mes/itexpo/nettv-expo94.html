There are about 45.000 Local Area Networks in Norway today, and the number is growing rapidly. The estimates of the growth in the total networking market, are in the 30-40% per year area. Networking applications, like message forwarding, groupware and client/server databases, are experiencing a tremendous growth, in the 50-100% area.

Increasing use of graphics in existing applications, and the growth of new applications like voice mail and video conferences, will lead to an increasing need for bandwith. High speed technologies, like FDDI, Fast Ethernet and ATM, will therefore experience a strong growth in 1994.

More and more users choose to link their networks - both locally and across Wide Area Networks. Because of this, the use of routers will also increase. The introduction by the Norwegian Telecom Association of a commercial ISDN service in May 1994, and the growing market for commercial Internet services, will only aggravate this tendency.
