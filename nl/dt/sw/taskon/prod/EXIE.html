TASKON/EXIE®

The EXIE product is an "Executive Information System" developed by utilizing the INTEGRATOR tools.

EXIE gives each user a tailored information access and tailored computer-based tools for retrieving, processing and distributing information. This implies that each user gets a personal information system which supports combination and aggregation of information from customer enterprise systems and external systems. Manual input can be (and should be) a part of the aggregated information, eg. assumptions and analysis.

It is possible to relate different information resources to a context, eg. a particular task in the user's work calendar. Further the user's personal information system supports exchange of multimedia information between users. Exchange of information may be context-driven, eg. by sending a task together with relevant information to another user.

The EXIE solutions are produced through customer projects, and are not software packages sold without customisation. We address especially support of cooporation between users, in addition to giving the individual users customised access to different types of information and tools for working with the information.

An EXIE solution can be customised to individual needs and gradually improve the solutions according to changes in the user needs. The main objective is, together with the customer, to create decison-oriented information flow within the customer organisation. This also implies that methods, template models and tools for modelling of decision processes and continous quality assurance of information flow and information systems are elements of a EXIE solution.

When we are producing a solution to a group of managers, also their key people are included as EXIE users. This in order to cover the information flow not only between managers, but also between the managers and their key people. The fact that EXIE gives a top-down approach to utilize information and computerbased tools, will give requirements to information flow and processing on lower levels.

In an EXIE production we work closely together with the customer. After the first steps in a production, the customer will, independant of Taskon, be able to further extend the solution and do implementations to new user groups.
