Computers and Learning A/S

Computers and Learning A/S (usually shortened to "CANDLE") was founded in 1988 and has completed a number of projects developing pedagogical software.

Hardware platform

The IBM-compatible PC was chosen as the standard delivery platform, with EGA color graphics (or VGA or better) and a mouse. This simple platform was chosen because it is good enough for reasonable graphics and animation and exists in greater numbers throughout industry, home and educational facilities than any other single architecture platform.

Software development platform

In CANDLE, we recognized early the need for high level authoring tools, and finding very little having the desired combination of power and simplicity for presentations, simulation and animation, we developed our own tools, called the CANDLE Authoring System. All pedagogical software we have developed has used this toolset, and we still don't know of any tool that better suits our needs.

Shareware and commercial software

We believe in software as intellectual products that should be as widely disseminated as possible. Therefore we try to offer as much as possible of what we produce at the lowest possible cost to the user. Most of what we have produced is now being offered as freeware for private use and as shareware to businesses, educational and other institutions. Some software is also offered as commercial products and can be ordered (at very reasonable costs of course).
