About ITC

About

ITC was established in 1986/87 and is partly owned by Transocean and the staff. The largest single customer is Saga Petroleum who has partly financed the PFAS development project. The project staff is petroleum engineers, geomechanics and software developers. ITC has a small organisation supported by a large number of expert consultants (40).

SOFTWARE

ITC is develloping drilling oriented software systems now in use both in the North-Sea and in other locations world wide. The systems are developed in the objectoriented C++ language under Windows, and will run on your PCs.

The software range from integrated systems like the PFAS to Computer Based Training Software and Field Tools for on-site use.

CONSULTING

The typical ITC project may be a combination of development of software, training and consulting.

The consulting work is therefore of a wide variety, from developing training standards in frontier areas of the world, to running projects for field stress analysis and postanalysis of drilled wells in the North-Sea.

TRAINING

We offer training related to our customers projects or internal projects. But we also run open drilling classes in basic and advanced drilling.

The training is on a technical college level for drillers and tool-pushers. Geomechanics training related to PFAS covers both theory and practical use of the ITC software.
