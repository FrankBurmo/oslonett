The People at

Svein Fagereng
Managing Director, ITC, 42 years old.
Holds a BS degree in Petroleum Engineering (Drilling), Rogaland University 1984.
A BS degree in civil engineering from 1976,

Norad, World Bank in Southern Africa (1977-1981),
Dowell Schlumberger as Field Engineer in Bolivia and West Africa (1981-1983),
Part owner of Matre Instruments (1984-1986) developing down hole well test equipment,

Partner in ITC from start in 1987.
Also active as well control trainer, basic and advanced drilling training, and systemization of computer based training software (CBT) and drilling operational software.
Member of the Norwegian national committee of IWCF.

Sverre Bautz
Head of Training and Software development, ITC, 41 years old.
Holds a BA in teaching from 1977.

Extensive experience from the Norwegian school system.
Background in development of training software (CBT) and other software.
Extensive software development training.

Partner in ITC from start in 1987.
Involved in drilling training and heading ITC's drilling operational software projects.

Rune Roberg
Account Manager, 32 years old.
Business Candidate BI, Norwegian School of Management (1991).

Scanvest Ring, major computer company, (1985) as a account consultants.
Economical consultant in local Bank (Sparebanken Vestfold 1986-87).

Major shareholder and partner in ITC from start in 1987.
QA-manager, and course administrator.

Einar Skjeggerud
Manager Drilling, ITC, 40 years old.
Holds a BS degree in Construction Engineering from 1978,
a Tool-Pusher technical college diploma from 91,
and the drilling-II subjects on a BS level from Rogaland University Centre in 92.

He has worked his way off-shore on third and fourth generation semi-subs from 82-87.
Since 87 he has been teaching driller and well control courses, developing CBT's and participating in projects related to drilling operations.
He was involved in the development of the practical training system for offshore personnel for Ross Offshore.

Today he is heading the drilling training section at ITC and is in charge of the project developing the IWCF trainer.

Torsten Jørgensen
Geomechanics Consultant, ITC, 31 years old.
Holds a MSc in Geology from NTH 93.
A MSc in Civil Engineering from Denmark.

He started working for Saga Petroleum in 94, and with ITC in October 94.
He has taken part in several technical papers on geo-mechanics.
He is lecturing on geo-mechanics and the use of PFAS at ITC.
He is heading several customer projects on stress analysis and geo-mech post analysis using the PFAS system.
