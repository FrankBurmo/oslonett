Well Integrity for Windows

Approach
The main objective in well planning is to ensure well integrity in all operational phases; i.e. avoid collapse or fracturing of the well bore. PFAS allows for a systematic planning of drilling operations through a multi-disciplinary approach.

PFAS may in particular be advantageous when planning advanced drilling operations, like Slim Hole Drilling, Under-Balanced Drilling, HTHP wells, Horizontal wells, Multi-Branch wells and in Deep Water Drilling operations, where hole integrity control issues are critical.

Design
PFAS is a Windows based modular computer system that will transform your collected data into useful information.

An open system: The data is imported or exported in a simple text format, to or from your own project. The data may be presented, displayed or printed, in any preferred form, and standard reports may be designed using the report generator.

Re-use of data: Due to the system openness it is now possible to utilise the vast amounts of data collected during operations, for planning and follow-up of new wells in an area.

Use
Well Planning is based on geological prognosis, log strength correlations, in-situ stresses, pore pressure, well geometry. A prognosis for well integrity trends, based on effective stresses and strengths, can then be established.

Drilling mode involves updating of well path, formation tops, pore pressure, stresses, lithology and mud weight. Post analysis: The first stage is to utilise the collected data to produce the Final Well Report. Based on experience transfer from previous wells the prognosis for the next well in an area may be established.
