Experience, Status and Future Plans With an IWCF Level Computer Based Training and Field Tool System

Giljarhus, S. Stene, F., Rod, J.P., Fagereng, S., Bautz, S., "Experience, Status and Future Plans With an IWCF Level Computer Based Training and Field Tool System", IADC European Well Control Conference 1995, 7-9 June 1995, Milano.

Summary

This paper will present the experience, status and future plans with a computer based training (CBT) and field tool system. It will cover field experience over 8 years on 3rd and 4th generation semi-subs in the North-Sea and in well control training courses in the North sea area and in frontier areas such as Southern Africa. The presentation will show the various components, such as CBT's for, basic and advanced well control theory together with system simulation and equipment theory. The "test database" generating tests on various levels and topics will be demonstrated, along with a discussion of the future potential of computer based testing either on mailed diskettes or via Internet..

The use of such systems in accordance with the IWCF standards will be presented. And we will discuss both the need for pre "IWCF test training" and "refreshment training" in front of critical operations like drilling of HTHP wells. Also the potential of higher availability through higher frequency of up-dating of information through new communication systems like Internet, thus leading to higher quality training, will be discussed in this paper. Again a feature favourable for distant locations in frontier areas. Thoughts on the "cost versus benefit" issue of CBT and field tool systems will be tabled. The presentation will be run with "live" software on a colour LCD.
