sAmbO's Home Studio Music Production Page

Brief History

Around 1988, a friend of mine had a beat up Tascam Porta-01 Ministudio Multitrack System in his dorm room. He was making some really raw but great demo tapes of his funk and R&B. Just down the hall, there was another individual with a full-on studio in his dorm room with an Oberheim Synth all MIDI'ed up to his Mac and sequenced to his 4-track. We had some great times jammin' till the early mornings.

So when I finally moved out of the dorms during my Sophomore year, I knew it was time for me to start my own ministudio, albeit a very humble one. I was going to build a functional studio with a poor starvin' student's budget. Anything I couldn't afford, I'll borrow.. or bribe friends with cold pizza for their musicianship or their instrument.

Since then, I've managed to engineer, produce, and perform on quite a number of songs using my most humble ministudio. Some of these cuts are covers, some of them originals. I picked Beatle's tunes to butcher since they had very strong material and best of all, were quite easy to play. I wound up playing most of the instruments but occasionally, friends would make cameo appearances.

Looking back at those good old dayz, I think the most rewarding part of this whole experience was learning how to be a better producer, paying attention to the whole stereo spectrum and using dynamics and frequency ranges to spatially orient the variety of sounds at my disposal. From this, I've been able to better appreciate what real producers out there are doing on real albums. My ear has been that much more sensitive since.
