Webster Definition for "SAMBO-EFFECT"

1. sambo-effect \sam'*bo a*fekt'\ n [L sambus efficere] the illusion of viewing through an ultra-wide angle lens (14mm and wider) hence distorting people, places, and things. This phenomenon is often used to add a sense of presence and dramatics to an otherwise mundane scene coldly generated by a computer. Often abused by someone by the name of "sAmbO" hence the name. Use at your own risk.
